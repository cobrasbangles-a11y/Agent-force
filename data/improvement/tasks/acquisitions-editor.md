# Task

A literary agent has submitted a full manuscript for a debut literary thriller,
92,000 words, along with a two-paragraph query letter. The author has about
8,000 Instagram followers in the true-crime niche and previously self-published
one novella that sold roughly 400 copies. The agent's pitch comps the book to
two recent bestsellers and says two other houses are already reading it, with
a decision wanted within three weeks and an advance ask of $75,000. The house
already has one debut thriller under contract for next spring's list. Assess
whether to pursue this manuscript and on what terms.
