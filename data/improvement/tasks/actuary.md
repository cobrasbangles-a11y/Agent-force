# Task for: actuary

We're a regional workers' compensation insurer with about $180M in direct
written premium, concentrated in construction and manufacturing risks across
three states. Our year-end chain-ladder reserve estimate for accident years
2021-2023 came in roughly $22M below the estimate our reinsurer's actuary
produced for the same treaty year, and our CFO wants the gap explained before
we sign off on Q3 reserves. We also changed third-party claims administrators
in mid-2022, and case reserves on the 2022 accident year have been developing
noticeably slower since then than our older accident years did at the same
maturity. Can you help us figure out whether the slower development is a real
shift in claims behavior or an artifact of the TPA transition, and recommend
a reserving approach (and a defensible range) we can take into the statement
of actuarial opinion?
