# Task

I farm about 340 acres of corn-soybean rotation in central Iowa (Story
County), and for the last two seasons I've had increasing patches of soybeans
turning yellow and stunted in the same low-lying, poorly-drained parts of two
fields — worse after wet springs. Neighbors say it could be SCN
(soybean cyst nematode) or just iron deficiency chlorosis, but I've never
had either confirmed. I want to know what's actually going on before I plant
soybeans in those fields again next spring, and whether there's a variety or
management change that would fix it or if I need to switch that ground to
corn for a season. I have about $1,500 I can put toward testing or a
demonstration this fall, and I'd like a plan I can act on before pre-order
seed decisions are due in December.
