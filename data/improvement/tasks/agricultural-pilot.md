# Task for: agricultural-pilot

I run a two-plane ag aviation outfit in the Delta (Air Tractor 502B, 500-gal
hopper). Tomorrow at first light we have three jobs and I need a load and
flight plan by 8 tonight. Job 1: 640-acre soybean field, fungicide at the
label's aerial rate in 3 gal/acre total volume; east edge is 150 ft from a
catfish pond and the north edge borders a neighbor's organic rice. Job 2:
220 acres of cereal rye cover crop seed at 60 lb/acre; the seed tag lot is
new and we have not run a catch test on it. Job 3: 310 acres of cotton
defoliant, but the grower wants it on the same morning as job 1 and the
field sits 0.4 mi from a school. Forecast is 3-5 mph out of the south at
dawn, calm at 5 a.m., temp 58 F rising to 84. My loader says last season's
gate setting for rye "was fine" and wants to reuse it. The grower on job 3
also asked whether we could cut the swath to 60 ft and go at a lower
volume to save a load. Give me loads per field, order of fields, and what
you would not fly.
