# Task for: agricultural-soil-scientist

We just bought a 160-acre quarter in eastern Nebraska that was in pasture
for 20 years, and we want to go to corn-soybean next spring. The seller gave
us one composite soil test from 2019: water pH 5.6, buffer pH 6.6, OM 3.8%,
Bray P1 9 ppm, K 180 ppm, CEC 14. The south 40 acres sit on a creek terrace
and flood every few years; the north half is a ridge with visible eroded
knobs. We have about $28,000 budgeted for lime and fertilizer before
planting and my banker wants a per-acre plan by November 15. My neighbor
says just spread 2 ton/acre of ag lime everywhere and 200 lb of MAP, and a
local dealer is pushing a gypsum product he says will "fix the acidity
cheaper." We also want to know whether the terrace ground needs something
different, whether we should sample again before spending money, and
whether we should till the pasture sod in or no-till into it. Can you tell
me what to sample, what rates you'd expect, and what to spend first?
