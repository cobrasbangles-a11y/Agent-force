# Task for: agronomist

I farm 900 acres of corn and soybeans in central Illinois. Field 12 (180
acres, corn after corn) is at V6 and has pale yellow striping between the
veins on the upper leaves in the low spots only; plants on the knolls look
fine. We had 4 inches of rain in ten days and cold nights. I put 160 lb N
as anhydrous last fall and my co-op rep wants to sell me another 60 lb of
UAN plus a zinc and manganese foliar "to be safe," about $38/acre. My
soil test from two years ago shows pH 6.4, OM 3.5%, zinc 1.1 ppm. On the
same field I'm also seeing western bean cutworm moths in my trap and
about 3% of plants with egg masses, and a neighbor already sprayed a
pyrethroid. I have to decide by Friday on the N and the insecticide, and
I also need hybrid picks for field 12 next year, when I'm thinking of
planting corn a third year because of the price. What would you do, and
what would you want to know first?
