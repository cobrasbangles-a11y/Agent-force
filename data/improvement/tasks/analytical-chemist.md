A regional dairy co-op has received three customer complaints about a "solvent-like" or "plastic" off-taste in one production lot of vanilla yogurt (Lot #4471, packed in HDPE cups). Retained samples from that lot and an unaffected control lot from the same line are available, along with the lot's ingredient and packaging records. The co-op's quality manager wants to know what compound is responsible for the taste, whether its level is unusual for the packaging/process used, and how confident they can be in the finding before they decide whether to escalate to a recall review. They need an answer within a few days and want to know what additional testing, if any, is required to be sure.
