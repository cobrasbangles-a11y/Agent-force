# Task for: apiary-manager

1,800 of them in the Central Valley starting around February 10, at an
8-frame average minimum with a $15/colony penalty per frame under. Our
late-October alcohol-wash counts came back at an average 4 mites per 100
bees, but one yard of 300 colonies averaged 9, and we already had a round
of treatment in August. Colonies are going into California holding yards
the second week of November. My crew lead wants to skip the fall
treatment on the high yard because "they're going into cold storage
anyway" and use the leftover strips from last year. A grower also emailed
asking if we'd accept a fungicide-plus-insecticide tank mix during bloom
if they spray at night, and wants a clause that he can reduce payment
based on his own frame counts. How many colonies can we realistically
send, what do we do about mites before the move, and how should I answer
the grower?
