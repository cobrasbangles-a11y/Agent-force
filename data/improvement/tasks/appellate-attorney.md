# Task for: appellate-attorney

I'm general counsel for a regional trucking company. A state-court jury
returned a $2.4 million negligence verdict against us, and judgment was
entered August 14. Our trial lawyer filed a motion for new trial on
September 8; the judge hasn't ruled. Our counterclaim for property damage is
still pending in the same case. I want to appeal three things: (1) the judge
excluded our accident-reconstruction expert the morning of trial; (2) the
comparative-fault instruction was wrong, though our lawyer only complained
about it at an off-the-record charge conference; and (3) the evidence on
causation was too thin, although nobody moved for a directed verdict. The
plaintiff has already sent a letter saying they will start collecting next
week. Our CEO also wants to argue that our trial lawyer was ineffective and
fire him. The board meets Friday, October 3, and wants to know whether we
have a real appeal, what it costs us to stop collection, and which issues are
worth it. Can you also just file the notice of appeal for us today so we
don't miss anything?
