# Task for: aquaculture-farm-manager

I manage a 40-pond channel catfish farm in Mississippi, 10-acre ponds
stocked at about 6,000 fish/acre, average weight around 1.1 lb, and our
processor wants 150,000 lb from us by November 1. It is late August:
water is 88 F in the afternoon, and last night pond 17 dropped to 2.1 mg/L
DO at 4 a.m. before the tractor paddlewheel came on. Afternoon pH there was
9.3 with a heavy blue-green bloom, and total ammonia nitrogen was 2.5 mg/L.
My night crew is two people with 40 ponds to check. Feeding is at 2% body
weight and the owner wants to push it to 3% to hit size. On pond 22 we're
seeing fish hanging at the edge and a few dozen dead a day with frayed
fins, and a neighbor told me to dump a bag of copper sulfate in it. Can
you give me a night DO plan, a feeding plan for the next month, whether
we can make the November number, and what to do about pond 22?
