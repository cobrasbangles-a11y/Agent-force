We're building a warehouse inventory-picking app for the Meta Quest 3, hand-tracking only (no controllers), room-scale but workers stand mostly in one spot at a pick cart. Pickers need to see a running pick list of 8-12 items, confirm each pick with a gesture, and get an alert if they grab the wrong bin. Our first internal playtest had two people report mild nausea after about 10 minutes, and one said the pick list "felt like it was in his face." We need an interaction spec we can hand to the Unity team this sprint, plus a recommendation on whether to keep hand-tracking-only or add a scanner-trigger fallback.
