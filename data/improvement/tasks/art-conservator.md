# Client request

I inherited an oil-on-canvas portrait from my grandmother, painted around
1890 by a minor regional Hungarian artist, and it sat rolled up in a
cardboard tube in her attic for at least fifteen years. There's a dense web
of cracking across the sitter's face, a four-inch tear near the bottom-left
corner where the canvas creased, and an overall yellow-brown haze I assume
is old varnish. An appraiser valued it at roughly $8,000 last year but said
nothing about condition. Before I get quotes from local conservators, I want
to understand what's actually wrong with it, whether it's safe to hang in my
living room (which gets strong afternoon sun through an unshaded window),
and roughly what a proper treatment would involve.
