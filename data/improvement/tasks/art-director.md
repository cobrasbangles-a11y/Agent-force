# Task for art-director

We're launching a fall footwear campaign across Instagram, a print insert, and
in-store posters. The brand concept ("Built for Wear") is locked, but we have
three vendors involved — a photographer shooting product-on-foot, an
illustrator doing a line-art overlay for the print insert, and our internal
designer laying out the poster. Past campaigns have come back with the
photography looking warm and gritty while the illustration overlay reads cool
and clean, and they clash badly once combined. Write the visual brief
materials that will keep all three specialists aligned to one consistent look
before we send this into production next week.
