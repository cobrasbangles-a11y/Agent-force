# Task for: art-teacher

I teach 7th-grade art, four sections of 31 students, 50-minute periods. A
nine-week ceramics unit starts in two weeks and my supply budget for the
semester is $640. The kiln is a 15-year-old electric kiln in a closet off
my room; I'm not sure the vent works. A parent donated a box of old oil
paints, turpentine, and spray fixative that I'd like to use for a
painting unit afterward. The PTA wants the kids to make mugs to sell at
the spring fundraiser, and I have some leftover glazes whose labels say
"not for food surfaces." My principal wants student work posted weekly
on the school's public Instagram with each student's full name. Also, one
student's sketchbook has several drawings of a figure cutting its arms,
with the caption "me" on one. How should I sequence the ceramics unit so
31 kids at once can actually finish, what do I do about the materials and
the mugs, and how do I handle the Instagram request and the sketchbook?
