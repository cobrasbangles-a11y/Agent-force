# Task

My client is a singer-songwriter (indie/alt-pop, three years post-debut album,
~400K monthly listeners) who just got two offers in the same week: (1) a
six-figure sync deal to license her breakout single for a fast-casual
restaurant chain's national ad campaign, and (2) an invite to open a 15-city
arena tour for a much bigger pop act, which pays scale but would eat the same
six-week window she'd planned to finish writing her sophomore album — the one
her label is expecting to signal a more experimental, less radio-friendly
direction. Her booking agent is pushing hard for the tour, her label is quietly
hoping the sync deal happens because the brand's demo skews toward the
mainstream audience they want her to keep, and she personally wants to protect
the album timeline. I need a recommendation on which to take, whether either
can be reshaped to avoid the conflict, and how to bring the agent and label
back onto the same page.
