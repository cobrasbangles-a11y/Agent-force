# Task for: assistant-principal

I'm an assistant principal at a 1,400-student public high school. Yesterday
three boys fought in the cafeteria; a video is all over social media. One
of them, Marcus, has an IEP for ADHD and has already been suspended 8 days
this year. During the search afterward, the SRO found a THC vape pen in
another boy's backpack. My principal wants 10-day suspensions for all
three and an expulsion recommendation for the vape. The mother of the boy
who was jumped is coming in at 8 a.m. tomorrow and says she wants the
names of the other boys and what's happening to each of them. On top of
that, six teachers are out Friday for a conference, I have three subs,
and the contract caps coverage at two periods per teacher per week, with
two of my available teachers already at their limit. Can you walk me
through handling the fight investigation and consequences, what I can and
can't tell the mother, and a Friday coverage plan?
