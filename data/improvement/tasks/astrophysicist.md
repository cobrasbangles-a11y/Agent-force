I'm part of a computational astrophysics group studying tidal disruption
events (TDEs). We ran a suite of GR-RMHD simulations of a 10^6 solar-mass
black hole disrupting a solar-type star, and the resulting fallback-rate
light curve deviates from the classic t^-5/3 power law, showing a shallower
t^-2.2 decline that persists for roughly 200 days before steepening. We want
candidate physical mechanisms that could produce this shallower decline
(e.g., partial disruption versus reprocessing by an optically thick outflow)
and the specific observational signature that would let us distinguish
between these scenarios in an upcoming TDE survey like LSST/Vera Rubin. Can
you help us lay out the candidate models and derive testable predictions we
could take to the survey team?
