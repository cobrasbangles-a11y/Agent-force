# Client request

I run a small wholesale bakery supplying sourdough boules to six local cafes,
and I switched flour suppliers three weeks ago from a 11.5% protein bread
flour to a new mill's 13% protein bread flour without changing my formula.
Since then my Tuesday and Thursday batches (72% hydration, 20% levain, bulk
fermented 4 hours at 76°F before an overnight cold retard) have been coming
out with a dense, slightly gummy crumb and less oven spring than before,
even though the same schedule worked fine for over a year. I also just
signed a new hotel contract and need to scale this same formula from my
current 6kg flour batch up to a 45kg batch starting next week. Can you help
me figure out what's causing the crumb problem and tell me how to adjust
the formula and schedule for the bigger batch at the same time?
