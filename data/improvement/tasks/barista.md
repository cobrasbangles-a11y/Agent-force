# Task for: barista

I manage a café that does about 320 drinks between 7 and 10 a.m. on a
two-group machine with one grinder. We switched to a new espresso blend
Monday (roasted 4 days ago) and my 18 g in, 36 g out shots are now running
19 seconds and tasting sour, but the morning crew keeps "fixing" it by
upping the dose to 21 g. Oat lattes are also coming out thin with big
bubbles, and a regular says her oat flat white curdled yesterday. We steam
almond, oat, and dairy on the same wand, and a customer with a tree-nut
allergy has started ordering oat drinks; my owner wants to skip the wand
purge-and-wipe between milks to save time at rush. Two new hires start
Saturday and I need them pulling shots to spec within a week. Another
regular who is pregnant asked me how many shots a day are safe for her.
I need a dial-in plan for the new blend, an oat milk fix, a milk-station
routine for rush, and a one-week training plan by Friday.
