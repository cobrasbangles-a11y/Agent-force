# Task for: bellhop

Tomorrow a tour coach with 52 guests (26 rooms on the rooming list)
arrives at 2:15 p.m., but housekeeping says only 14 of those rooms will be
ready by 3:00 and the rest by 4:30. The tour leader says roughly 70
checked bags plus carry-ons. At 2:30 a wedding party of 12 also arrives,
and there are 9 departures with 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. airport shuttles. I
have two bell staff and one cart that is out for repair, so three carts
total. One tour guest uses a wheelchair and her bags include a
medical device, and the bride's family is flagged VIP. Last month a man
who said he was a guest's husband asked us to bring her bags to "his"
room, and we did; it turned out they had separated. Also, one departing
guest wants to leave a locked case "with cash and passports" at the bell
stand overnight. Give me a sequencing and staging plan for 1:00 to
5:00 p.m., a tagging system, and a rule we can use at the stand for bag
release and valuables by tonight's shift meeting.
