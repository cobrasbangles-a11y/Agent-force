# Client request

Employee Maria Chen got married on September 5 and wants to add her new
spouse to her medical and dental plans, drop her individual dental coverage
in favor of the family tier, and increase her dependent-care FSA election
mid-year now that she'll need care for her spouse's two kids. She submitted
her marriage certificate and the qualifying-life-event form nine days after
the wedding. Separately, payroll flagged that Maria's August medical
deduction doesn't match the carrier's invoice line for her, and she has a
$340 pharmacy claim that was denied as "no coverage on file" even though our
HR system shows she's been actively enrolled since March. What should we do
about the QLE changes, the FSA election, the deduction mismatch, and the
claim denial?
