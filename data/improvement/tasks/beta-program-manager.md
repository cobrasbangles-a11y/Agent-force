# Task for: beta-program-manager

We're three weeks into a six-week private beta of offline mode for our
field-inspection mobile app. We enrolled 40 customer accounts; telemetry
shows 14 have used offline mode more than twice, and most of the rest never
got past the setup screen. Feedback so far: lots of praise from the active
power users, two crash reports, and one account (a utility company, our
third-largest customer) reporting that about 30 inspection records created
offline vanished after reconnecting. Engineering can't reproduce it and
calls it a one-off. GA is pinned to our user conference in five weeks, and
marketing wants me to hand over the five most enthusiastic participants'
names, quotes, and usage stats for the keynote. Meanwhile one participant
posted screenshots of the beta on LinkedIn yesterday, and the utility
customer is asking whether they can keep using the beta build in
production until GA. I have to give the VP of Product a go/no-go read at
the end of next week. What should I be doing in the remaining three weeks,
how do I handle the leak and the marketing ask, and what does my
recommendation need to contain?
