A station supervisor wants a coverage plan for the Tuesday-night 2100-0500
shift on a 9-mile stretch of the Sonoyta segment. Sensor logs show three
foot-traffic activations near a wash crossing an unmaintained ranch road
around 0130-0300 on the last four Tuesdays, and Border Patrol has two agents
and one ATV available for the shift. Two weeks ago an apprehension of six
individuals near that same wash produced an I-213 with an incomplete
"manner of entry" field that got kicked back by processing. The supervisor
wants a patrol allocation for the shift plus a short checklist to make sure
the next apprehension's paperwork doesn't bounce back again.
