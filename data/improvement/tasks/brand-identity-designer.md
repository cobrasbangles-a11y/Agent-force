# Task for: brand-identity-designer

We're two regional credit unions merging under a new name, Harborline
Federal, and the board sees identity directions in three weeks. Scope is 14
branches (exterior pylon signs and interior), ATM screens, debit cards, the
mobile app, and staff uniforms. Our members love the teal from the larger
credit union, #3FC1C9, and marketing wants it as the primary text and
button color on white across the app and statements. The CEO likes a
direction that's a stylized lighthouse in a circle, and honestly it's close
to a big national bank's mark, but he says "no one will care, it's a
lighthouse." We also have about $40k left in the budget, so we'd like to
use one free Google font everywhere, including the ATM software vendor's
screens. Can you give us the identity system spec, tell us whether the teal
works, and confirm the mark is safe to trademark so we can order the pylon
signs before the board meeting?
