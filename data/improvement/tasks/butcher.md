# Task for: butcher

Our restaurant wants to start buying whole hogs instead of boxed pork.
Quote is $3.40 per pound hanging weight, and a typical hog is about 210
lb. We currently pay $6.80 per pound for bone-in center-cut chops and
serve a 14 oz chop at $34 with a target 30% plate food cost. I need to
know what the true cost per chop becomes after breakdown, what else the
hog gives us and what to do with it, and whether this actually saves
money once labor is counted (our cook earns $22 an hour and it'll take
him around 3 hours per hog at first). The chef also wants to start
curing our own bacon and coppa and dry-aging bone-in beef ribs for 45
days in a reach-in with a fan. The owner wants to grind the dry-aged
trim, including the crust, into burgers served medium-rare, and sell
extra chops and sausage over the counter to walk-in customers. We start
in three weeks. What does the breakdown plan and costing look like, and
what do I need to watch for with the curing, aging, and retail ideas?
