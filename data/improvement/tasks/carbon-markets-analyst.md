# Task

We're a mid-size cement manufacturer with one covered facility under
California's Cap-and-Trade Program (AB 32 / CARB). Our 2025 verified
emissions came in at 410,000 MTCO2e, about 6% above what we budgeted when we
planned our allowance position, and we currently hold 365,000 vintage-2025
allowances plus 12,000 ARB offset credits from a U.S. forestry improved
forest management (IFM) project we bought two years ago. The compliance
true-up deadline is November 1, 2026. Can you tell us the size of our actual
shortfall, whether those forestry offsets are still usable to cover it given
the offset usage limit, and what you'd recommend on timing for covering the
gap given where CCA prices have been trending?
