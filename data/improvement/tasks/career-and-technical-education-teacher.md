# Task for: career-and-technical-education-teacher

I run a high school welding program: 22 second-year students, mostly 16 and
17. They sit a 3G plate bend test in 7 weeks for an industry credential.
I have 8 booths, but only 5 have working fume extraction, and one period's
project is on galvanized angle from a donation. A local fabrication shop
wants to take 4 of my students for a paid after-school placement where
they'd weld, run the press brake, and drive the forklift to move steel. My
strongest student keeps striking arcs for tack welds with his helmet up
"just for a second" and says he's done it at his uncle's shop for years.
The district's funding report counts credential pass rates, and my
principal suggested only letting the students most likely to pass sit
the test. Can you lay out a seven-week plan to get as many students
ready as possible, tell me what to do about the booths and the galvanized
steel, the shop placement, and my strongest student?
