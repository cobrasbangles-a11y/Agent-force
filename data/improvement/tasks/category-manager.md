# Task for: category-manager

I'm the category manager for polypropylene resin at a consumer packaging
company: $22M a year across three suppliers, with 65% of that on the
incumbent. My annual budget target was a 3% cost reduction. Instead we're
running 9% over budget, and finance's monthly report says the category
"missed savings." The published resin index is up 14% year to date and
our volumes are down 6%. A Gulf Coast supplier we've never used is
quoting 8% below the incumbent's current price. Their material would
need requalification on our lines (about six months and $180K of trials),
and freight from Texas costs about $0.04 a pound more. The plant manager
wants me to start sending them POs next week and "qualify in parallel."
Separately, the CFO wants me to lock in the incumbent's offer this month:
a five-year take-or-pay at a fixed adder over the index, 40 million
pounds a year. My annual strategy review is in three weeks. What should
my recommendation be?
