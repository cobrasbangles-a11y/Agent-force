# Task for: catering-manager

I have a wedding on July 19: 180 guests at a barn venue with no kitchen,
power for a few warmers only, and a forecast in the 90s. The couple's
budget is $95 per person "all in," and they want plated service for
dinner with a choice of three entrées on the RSVP card. They signed at a
guarantee of 180, and now, three weeks out and after the deposit, they
want to drop to 140 because RSVPs are slow. Twelve guests have dietary
needs, including two with severe tree-nut allergies and one with celiac
disease. The couple also want guests to take leftovers home in boxes,
the groom's aunt wants to bring her homemade cheesecakes for the dessert
table, and they plan to buy their own liquor and have their cousins
bartend. My kitchen is also booked for a 90-person corporate lunch the
same day. I need a revised package and cost that fits the budget, a
response on the guarantee, a staffing and logistics plan for the barn,
and what to tell them about leftovers, the cheesecakes, and the bar, by
Friday's call.
