# Task for: causal-inference-scientist

In March we raised the delivery fee from $2.99 to $3.99 in 14 of our 40
metro markets; the other 26 stayed at $2.99. Leadership picked the 14 because
they had the "strongest demand," not at random. Orders per active customer in
the treated markets fell 6% over the next 12 weeks versus 2% in the control
markets, and finance wants a single number for the price elasticity so they
can decide by November 1 whether to roll the increase out nationally. Two
complications: a competitor launched in 5 of the 14 treated markets in April,
and we changed our loyalty program for everyone in May. I have weekly
market-level data back to January 2024 and customer-level order history. The
product VP has already told the board the fee "only cost us 4 points of
orders," and wants my analysis to confirm that figure. Can you design the
analysis, tell me what the estimate can and can't claim, and tell me how to
handle the VP's number if the data doesn't support it?
