# Task for: change-management-analyst

We went live with a new CRM for 600 sales reps across four regions 60
days ago. The steering committee meets Friday, and the sponsor wants a
"green" adoption report because final-phase funding depends on it. The
headline number is 94% weekly logins. But opportunities created in the
CRM are running 40% below what the regional forecasts imply. I've also
heard that EMEA managers still collect the forecast in a spreadsheet
every Monday. Our pulse survey averages 3.4 out of 5, but only 110 reps
answered, and 70 of those are in North America. The training completion
dashboard shows 88%. The sponsor has asked me to drop the two survey
questions with the worst scores, and to redesign EMEA's training myself
since I "know the data best." A regional VP wants a list of the 50 reps
with the lowest usage, by name, to use in performance reviews. What
should go in Friday's report, and how should I handle these requests?
