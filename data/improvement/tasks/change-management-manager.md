# Task for: change-management-manager

I'm leading change management for our finance and procurement ERP
cutover: 1,100 users across three sites, go-live February 1. That is also
the week of our fiscal year-end close. As part of the same program,
accounts payable is moving to a shared-services center in another
country, and about 40 AP clerk roles at our sites will be eliminated
within six months of go-live. Our training vendor has booked every
session in late November, because that's when their trainers are free.
The CFO is the sponsor but says she's too busy for town halls and wants
to record a five-minute video instead. The buyers are pushing back hard
on the new purchase approval workflow, which adds three approval steps
to orders under $5,000. The project team calls that "normal resistance."
The HR business partner suggested we keep the AP role eliminations quiet
until after go-live so the clerks don't leave before cutover. The
steering committee wants my change plan in two weeks. What should it
look like?
