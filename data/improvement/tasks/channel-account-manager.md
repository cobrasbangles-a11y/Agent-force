distributor for our network security appliances; my Q4 quota is $3.2M
partner-sourced and we're at $1.9M with ten weeks left. Three things are

and last week our direct enterprise rep started working the same account

line. Second, a mid-tier partner spent $38K of MDF on a golf event and
sent me a photo and an attendee list, but no leads have been entered, and
they want their Q1 allocation approved now. Third, my second-largest
partner has six certified engineers but has brought us two deals all year.
My director says "just give the hospital deal back to the reseller and
throw in an extra 5 points of margin so they calm down." What do I do on
each, what do I tell the reseller this week, and how do I realistically
close the $1.3M gap?
