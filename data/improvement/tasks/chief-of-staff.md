# Task for: chief-of-staff

I'm chief of staff to the CEO of a 350-person Series C company. Our board
meeting is in 12 days, and the FY plan is still unresolved. The CRO wants
40 new account executives. The CFO's model supports 18, given 22 months
of runway. The two of them have now argued about it in three leadership
meetings without landing anywhere. The CEO told me to "just pick a
number, tell the CRO, and put it in the deck." One board member emailed
me directly asking for the draft deck and the compensation committee
materials early, so he can "get ahead of it." Yesterday a VP stopped me
in the hallway and said the CRO has repeatedly made demeaning comments
about her in front of her team. She asked me to "handle it quietly"
without going to HR. And the CEO's calendar audit shows 38% of her time
goes to internal one-on-ones, while customers get 6%. What do I do over
the next 12 days, and in what order?
