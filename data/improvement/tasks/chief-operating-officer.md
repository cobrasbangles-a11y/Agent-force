# Task for: chief-operating-officer

I'm COO of a $600M consumer products manufacturer and distributor. Sales
just closed a $30M program with a national retailer, with first
shipments in 10 weeks. Supply chain says we can only meet it by starting
a second shift on our main press line, which means hiring 60 people.
Recruiting is at 55% of this year's hiring plan. The operating plan
assumed 4% freight inflation; we're running 11%, which alone is about
$9M of margin we won't see. The CEO wants the full-year EBITDA number
held for the board meeting in three weeks. The plant's safety manager
says the second shift can't run the presses until added guarding is
installed and new operators finish lockout/tagout training, about six
weeks out. The sales VP wants me to overrule her and start the shift on
day one with experienced operators on overtime covering the gaps.
Customer service backlog is also up 30% since the ERP upgrade. How do I
handle the next three weeks?
