# Task for: chief-procurement-officer

I'm CPO at a $4B industrial electronics company with about $2.1B of
annual addressable spend. Procurement is decentralized: five business
units, each with its own approval limits. The board's risk committee
wants a supplier exposure report in four weeks. After a tier-2 outage
last quarter, we found that three categories (microcontrollers, PCB
assembly, and a specialty resin) are each effectively single-sourced
from the same region, and no category manager had flagged it, because
each looked acceptable on its own. One BU signed a $12M, three-year
logistics contract without procurement or legal review. The CEO wants it
"terminated tomorrow." The CFO wants all supplier payment terms moved
from 45 to 120 days to free about $80M of working capital. And our
largest PCB supplier's CEO has invited me to a week-long "partner
summit" at a resort, with everything paid. What should I bring to the
risk committee, and how should I handle the rest?
