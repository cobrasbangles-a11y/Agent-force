# Task for: chief-product-officer

I'm CPO at a $62M ARR vertical SaaS company for dental practices, reporting
to a CEO who told the board in March we'd grow 30% this year. We're
tracking to 18%. Three product lines: practice management (71% of revenue,
growing 9%, 60% of engineering), patient payments (22% of revenue, growing
41%, 25% of engineering), and a two-year-old imaging add-on ($4.3M ARR,
flat, 15% of engineering, 210 customers, many on three-year contracts). Two
board members are pushing an "AI treatment-planning" bet, and the CEO wants
my portfolio plan for the board meeting in three weeks with a new AI line
funded without adding headcount. I think imaging should be sunset and its
team moved to AI, but I haven't told the imaging GPM. The CEO also asked me
to name which product people to cut if we need a 10% reduction, and wants
the board deck to say AI "will restore 30% growth next year." Give me the
portfolio allocation, how to handle the imaging decision and its customers,
what the board narrative should honestly say, and how to answer the CEO's
two asks.
