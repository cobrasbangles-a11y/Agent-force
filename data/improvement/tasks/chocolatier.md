# Task for: chocolatier

I run a small chocolate shop and need to produce 4,000 bonbons for
Valentine's Day, starting in three weeks, with two people and one
tempering machine. The best-seller is a fresh-cream passion fruit
ganache in a dark shell; the owner wants it displayed at room temperature
with a six-week "best by" so we can make it all at once and also ship
to customers in Arizona and Florida. Our last batch of milk chocolate
bars developed grayish white streaks after two weeks in the display
case near the front window, and my new assistant has been tempering
milk and white chocolate on the same curve as our 70% dark. The owner
also wants to print "nut-free" on the passion fruit box because it has
no nuts, though we make hazelnut praline on the same equipment. Our
current price is $3.00 per bonbon and couverture costs $14 per kg. Give
me a production schedule, the tempering and storage specs, a realistic
shelf life and shipping plan for the passion fruit piece, a diagnosis of
the streaks, and a costing check, by next Wednesday.
