# Task for: choreographer

I'm directing a regional production of a new musical and need the
Act One finale choreographed: a 3:40 song at 132 bpm, 4/4, with a
16-bar slow bridge at half time before a final key change. Cast is 14
(six trained dancers, eight actors who move), on a thrust stage 32 ft
wide by 24 ft deep with audience on three sides and a raked deck at
1:24. I want the ending to have a big lift, the lead flying overhead into
a tableau, and the bridge should feel "like everyone's drowning." We get
three 90-minute dance rehearsals before we add the set, and the lead
actress mentioned she had a shoulder surgery last year but says she's
fine. Two of the ensemble also have a costume quick change offstage right
before this number starts, 30 seconds. Can you build the whole number
for me with counts and formations, and tell me how to use the three
rehearsals? Our dance captain is an ensemble member who's never captained
before.
