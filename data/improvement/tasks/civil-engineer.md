We're developing a 4.2-acre parcel in unincorporated Douglas County, Colorado into a 24-unit townhome community. The site slopes about 8% from the northwest corner down to a seasonal drainage swale along the southern property line, and our geotech report shows clay soils with poor infiltration (measured rate under 0.1 in/hr) and groundwater at 11 feet. The county's stormwater ordinance requires detention to pre-development release rates for the 2-, 10-, and 100-year storms plus water quality treatment before discharge. We need a grading and drainage concept, a rough detention pond sizing, and utility stub-out layout for water and sanitary sewer (public mains run along the frontage road) in time for a pre-application meeting with the county in three weeks. Our main worry is whether the poor infiltration rate is going to force an oversized surface detention pond that eats into our unit count.
