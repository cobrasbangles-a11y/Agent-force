# Task for: clinical-psychologist

A PCP referred a 34-year-old software engineer to me for "ADHD testing
so I can start a stimulant." Insurance authorized four hours of testing
and the PCP wants my report within three weeks. At intake he reports
lifelong trouble finishing tasks, but also that he sleeps four to five
hours most nights, has had several stretches of three or four days
where he barely slept and "felt brilliant" and spent $6,000 on
equipment, and he uses cannabis most evenings "to come down." His PHQ-9
is 16, with item 9 scored 1 ("several days"). He has no school records
before college. He wants two things from me this week: a letter to his
employer saying he has ADHD so he can get accommodations before his
performance review on the 20th, and my opinion on which stimulant and
dose to ask his PCP for. Help me plan the evaluation within the
authorized hours, handle the letter and medication questions, and tell
me what I need to do today before the evaluation even starts.
