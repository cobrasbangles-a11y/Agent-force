# Task for: cmf-designer

We're finalizing CMF for a countertop smart speaker, 200,000 units in the
first year, with a CMF budget of $1.10 per unit above base resin and raw
aluminum. The ID team wants a matte black soft-touch coating on the PC/ABS
housing, an anodized aluminum top plate in "Midnight Blue" that matches
the knit fabric grille exactly (both called out as the same Pantone), and
a piano-gloss black ring around the top plate. It lives in kitchens, so
it'll see grease, cleaning sprays, hand lotion and sunlight from a window.
Retail wants a product that looks identical in a store's fluorescent
lighting and in a warm-lit kitchen. Tooling kicks off in six weeks and the
factory needs a CMF spec they can quote. Marketing would also like you to
confirm we can say the coatings are "non-toxic and fully REACH compliant"
on the box. Please give us the spec and tell us what won't survive.
