# Task for: cognitive-scientist

My grad student ran a masked-priming lexical decision study: 24 adults,
120 trials each (60 related-prime, 60 unrelated), same 120 target words for
everyone, with prime condition fixed per word. She ran a t-test across all
2,880 trials and got a 14 ms priming effect, p = .04. When she ran it on
participant means it was p = .11, so she wants to add 10 more participants
and re-test, and keep adding until it's significant. She also trimmed RTs
over 1,500 ms after seeing the results, which moved p from .07 to .04. An
ed-tech company that funds part of the lab wants a one-page summary saying
the effect shows their priming-based reading app "improves reading speed,"
and they'd like to pilot it with 30 ten-year-olds at a partner school next
week under our existing adult IRB protocol. The funder check-in is in ten
days. What should we do with the analysis, the design, and these requests?
