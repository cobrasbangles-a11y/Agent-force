few boxes of a frozen -20°C companion product to the same trailer and
suggests topping off the 2-8 shippers with dry ice for extra margin. I
need a revised lane plan by Friday: the handoff points and dwell limits,
what to do about the Dallas cross-dock, what the trailer setpoint should
be, and how we should have handled last month's readings.
