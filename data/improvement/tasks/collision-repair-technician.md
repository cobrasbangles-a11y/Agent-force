and a post at maybe 25 mph on the left front. Front airbags did not
deploy, but the driver's seat belt locked up and now won't retract. The

headlamp, "straighten left lower rail," four-wheel alignment, and no ADAS

to heat the rail with a torch and pull it. The vehicle's actual cash value
is about $19,000. The owner wants it back in 10 days for a road trip, and
asked if we could also put the old scrape on the right rear door on this
claim since "it's all one estimate anyway."

Can you write the repair plan and sequence, tell me what's missing from
the insurer's estimate, and whether this is anywhere near a total loss?
