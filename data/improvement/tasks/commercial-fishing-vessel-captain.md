# Task for: commercial-fishing-vessel-captain

I run a 58-ft groundfish trawler out of New Bedford, crew of four,
cruising at 9 knots burning 22 gal/hr, 3,000 gallons of fuel, and a hold
that takes about 40,000 lb iced. I want to leave Thursday for a 6-day trip
to Georges Bank, about 130 nm out. Our sector has 12,000 lb of Georges Bank
cod left on the books and plenty of haddock; last trip we hit cod in the
haddock tows about 1 lb for every 5. The forecast shows a low tracking
up the coast Sunday with 25-30 kt and 10-12 ft seas. One crewman's survival
suit is out for repair and our EPIRB registration expires next month. The
owner wants 35,000 lb of haddock to cover a note due at the end of the
month and suggested we tow in the closed area edge "where the haddock
stack up" and sort out the cod on the way in. Plan the trip for me: fuel,
where to fish, how much haddock we can take before cod shuts us down, and
the weather call.
