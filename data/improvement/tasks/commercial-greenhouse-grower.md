# Task for: commercial-greenhouse-grower

We grow potted poinsettias in a 60,000 sq ft glass range in Ohio and have
a big-box order for 18,000 6.5-inch pots to ship November 20, with a
height spec of 14-16 inches pot included. Cuttings were stuck August 1 and
potted August 25, pinched September 8. Today is October 3 and the crop
averages 10.5 inches, which the graphical tracking says is about an inch
over the curve. Nights have been running 70 F because the back range's
heating is fighting a stuck vent, and we've got 6 plants near a security
light in the loading area that show no color change. We're also seeing
whitefly counts climbing on yellow cards, and a worker suggested we just
drench more growth regulator and spray whatever's in the chem room to
knock back whitefly before the buyer's inspector comes October 20. How do
we get height, color, and pests on target for the ship date without
losing the order?
