# Task for: compensation-analyst

A recruiter needs an offer range by Friday for a Senior Data Engineer in
Austin. The candidate is asking $185,000 base. Our band for that level is
$140,000 to $175,000 (midpoint $157,500). I have two surveys: one with an
effective date of March 2025 showing a 50th percentile of $162,000 from 38
companies, and a newer tech-specific cut from April 2026 showing $178,000,
but that cut has only 4 participating companies. Our policy is national pay
with an Austin tier at 95%. We have three incumbents in the role at
$148,000, $156,000 and $162,000; the $148,000 person was hired two years ago
and is the only woman on the team, and her manager asked me to "just write
in the memo whether that gap is illegal" so he can take it to his VP. The
recruiter also wants to ask the candidate for her current salary to anchor
the offer. What range should I give the recruiter, what does a $185,000 offer
do to the team, and how should I handle the manager's request?
