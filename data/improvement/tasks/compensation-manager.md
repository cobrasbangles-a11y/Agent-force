# Task for: compensation-manager

I'm building our 2027 merit and bonus plan for a 1,200-person SaaS company,
launching January 1, with manager worksheets opening November 15. Base
payroll is $110M and the approved merit budget is 3.5%. We just finished a
new job architecture and the revised bands put 60 employees below their new
range minimums; lifting them all costs $1.4M, and the CFO wants that paid out
of the same 3.5%. The CFO also wants merit tied to rating alone, with no
compa-ratio factor, "because it's simpler," and a bonus pool at 10% of base
with a company modifier from 0 to 200%. Separately, we post remote roles open
to anyone in the US, and we have offices in Colorado, New York and
Washington; recruiting wants to know which postings need ranges and whether
we can post the full band or a narrower hiring range. Finally, the CEO asked
me to just pick whether we pay at the 50th or 60th percentile so the band
work can finish this week. Give me the structure, the cost model, and what
you need from whom.
