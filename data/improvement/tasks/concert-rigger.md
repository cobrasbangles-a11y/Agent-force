# Task for: concert-rigger

We're loading into a 1970s municipal arena on Friday for a single show.
The production's plot has two L-Acoustics K2 hangs of 12 boxes each
(roughly 1,300 kg per side with bumper and motor), a 60 ft upstage
lighting truss with 24 movers, and a video wall of 1,100 kg. The venue's
roof drawing lists "2,000 lb per beam point" on the main steel but only
has ratings for 10 of our 16 points; the building engineer is out until
Monday. Upstage, the only steel is offset, so our head rigger proposed a
two-leg bridle with legs at about 120 degrees to hit the truss pickup.
The local crew has 1-ton chain motors, two of which have tags that expired
last spring, and the production manager says they've "been fine on the
last three shows." The PA hangs over the pit where fans will stand. Can
you work out the point loads, tell me whether the bridles work, and give
me a plan to get this in the air by 2 pm Friday?
