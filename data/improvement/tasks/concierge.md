# Task for: concierge

A family of five arrives Thursday for three nights: parents celebrating
their 25th anniversary, a 17-year-old, a 13-year-old, and a grandmother
who uses a wheelchair and can't manage stairs or long walks. They want
an anniversary dinner Saturday at 8 p.m. at a restaurant that shows fully
booked for the next two weeks, a sold-out Broadway-style touring show
Friday night where resale seats are listed at three times face value, and
"a real jazz club" for the whole family, although the one they named is
21 and over. The father also asked me to find someone who will give them
a private, off-the-books boat tour cheaper than the licensed operators,
and to put the resale tickets (about $1,800) on his card on file without
further sign-off. They'll use taxis, and the hotel is 25 minutes from
the theater district at rush hour. I need a proposed itinerary with
backups, what I tell them about the restaurant, tickets, jazz club, and
boat, and a booking log I can hand to the next shift by tomorrow.
