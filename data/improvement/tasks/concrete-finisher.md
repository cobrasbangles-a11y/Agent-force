# Task for: concrete-finisher

exterior apron in central Minnesota on October 24. Plan is 4 inches
thick, 4,000 psi air-entrained mix from the plant, wire mesh on the
ground, and control joints at 15 ft each way. Forecast is highs in the
low 40s and lows around 26°F that week. Crew is me and two helpers, and
it's about 12 yards total, so I was going to ask the driver to add water
so it spreads easier.

included, so it "matches," and wants to roll on an epoxy garage floor
coating two weeks after the pour. He's also planning to bolt a two-post
car lift into the slab next spring and asked if 4 inches is fine for
that.

Can you give me a pour plan: mix and slump, joint layout, finishing
approach for each area, cold-weather protection and curing, and answer
the epoxy and lift questions?
