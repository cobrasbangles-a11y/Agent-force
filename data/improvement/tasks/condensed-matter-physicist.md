# Task for: condensed-matter-physicist

We grew a 12 nm infinite-layer nickelate film on SrTiO3 by pulsed laser
deposition and topotactic reduction. Four-probe resistivity (silver-paint
contacts, 10 µA) drops about 60% between 32 K and 18 K and then flattens at
a nonzero value down to 2 K. A 3 T field shifts the drop down by about 4 K.
SQUID magnetometry on the same piece shows no clear diamagnetic signal,
which the student attributes to the film being too thin. Only one of five
films grown shows the drop; the other four stay metallic. A collaborator
wants to submit to a high-profile journal in three weeks titled
"Superconductivity at 32 K in ..." and asks me to write the physics
section. I also want to know which measurements to prioritise with our
limited dilution fridge and beamline time this month, and whether the
kink we see near 105 K in every film means anything. What is your read,
and what would you tell the collaborator?
