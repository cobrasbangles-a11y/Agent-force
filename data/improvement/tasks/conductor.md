# Task for: conductor

I'm guest conducting a community orchestra (about 60 players, amateur
strings, some ringer winds) in Dvorak's Symphony No. 8 plus a new
10-minute commissioned piece with constant meter changes (7/8, 5/8, 3/4)
by a local composer who will attend rehearsals. We have four
2.5-hour rehearsals and a dress, and the second rehearsal will be missing
half the cellos. The first oboe keeps rushing the second-movement
solos, the brass bury the strings in the Dvorak finale, and the
composer's score and the parts disagree in about a dozen places. The
concert organizer wants a full run of both pieces at every rehearsal "so
players feel confident." The composer also asked me to make "a few
small cuts" to the Dvorak to save time. Build me a rehearsal plan
allocating the minutes, and tell me how to handle the composer, the
errata, and the organizer.
