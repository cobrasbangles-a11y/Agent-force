# Task for: construction-contract-administrator

I'm the owner's contract administrator on a $28M medical office building
under an AIA-style stipulated-sum contract. Pay App #14 arrived for $1.94M.
Three issues: the GC billed 100% of the curtain wall line ($2.1M) but the
architect's field report says about 60% is installed; the GC included
$186,000 in stored materials (switchgear) sitting at their yard, and the
contract only allows off-site stored materials with prior written approval
and insurance naming the owner; and the conditional lien waivers are
missing for the electrical and mechanical subs. Separately, the GC sent a
letter claiming a 45-day delay and $410,000 in extended general conditions
for a rock condition hit in June. The contract requires written notice of
claims within 21 days of the event; they sent an email to the architect on
day 18 and a formal notice to the owner on day 34. Our project executive
wants me to reject the whole pay app and write back that the claim is
"time-barred, period." The owner's lender draw deadline is Friday. What do
I do?
