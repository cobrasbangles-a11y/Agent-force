# Task for: consumer-product-manager

I run product for a study-habit app with 2.1M monthly actives, about 35% of
whom tell us in onboarding they're 13-17. Day-30 retention is 8% and
falling. Two months ago we moved the paywall from day 7 to right after
signup (a 7-day free trial requiring a card); trial starts went up 22% but
our App Store rating dropped from 4.6 to 4.1 and reviews say "can't even
try it." The growth team now wants to ship three things before back-to-school
in four weeks: a streak system with a "you'll lose your 30-day streak!"
push at 10pm, a contact-upload feature that invites a user's classmates
automatically, and a paid "streak freeze." Our first-launch screen also
fires the iOS notification permission prompt immediately, and opt-in is
about 41%. My CEO wants D30 retention to 12% by the end of the quarter.
Which of these should we ship, change, or kill, what should we do about the
paywall, and how should I sequence the next four weeks to actually move
retention rather than just short-term session counts?
