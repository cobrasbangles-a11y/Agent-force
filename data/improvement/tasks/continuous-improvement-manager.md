# Task for: continuous-improvement-manager

I manage continuous improvement at a three-line food packaging plant.
Our portfolio has 31 active or completed projects, and the dashboard
claims $4.2M in savings. Finance has validated $900K of that; most of the
rest is "labor hours freed" on lines where headcount and overtime didn't
change. We ran 12 kaizen events last year, and a spot audit last month
found 7 of them have slid back to roughly their old performance. The
plant manager wants me to launch a full DMAIC Black Belt project on
changeover time, which averages 95 minutes. He also wants the year-end
report, due in three weeks, to show the full $4.2M. One queued project,
raised by a line lead, proposes dropping the second metal-detector check
at case packing because it slows the line by 6%. The team is short on
facilitators, and I have capacity for maybe four new projects next
quarter. How should I reset the portfolio, and what should go in the
year-end report?
