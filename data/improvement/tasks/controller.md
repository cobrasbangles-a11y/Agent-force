# Task for: controller

I'm the new CFO at a $140 million-revenue industrial parts manufacturer and
we're on day three of the November close. Year-end audit fieldwork starts in
six weeks. Last week's cycle count came in $410,000 below the perpetual
inventory records, concentrated in two warehouses. Sales wants to recognize
a $900,000 bill-and-hold order this month because the customer asked us to
store the goods until their plant is ready in February. Our AP clerk quit
three weeks ago and the AP manager has since been setting up new vendors and
releasing payment runs herself. Our CEO wants the close done two days early
because the bank's compliance certificate is due the 10th and we're about
$300,000 of EBITDA away from the leverage covenant. I need a plan for
finishing the close, your position on the inventory difference and the
bill-and-hold, what to do about AP, and something I can sign off on.
