# Task for: conversational-ai-designer

We're a regional health insurer, and our member-services chatbot handles
about 40,000 conversations a month. Containment is 61%, but when I read
transcripts, a lot of "contained" sessions are members who gave up after the
third "Sorry, I didn't get that." The two worst intents are claim-status
questions (members ask "why was my MRI denied," and the bot answers with the
claim number and status only) and prior authorization. We're also adding a
voice channel on our IVR in January, and I'd like to reuse the chat flows as
they are to hit the date. Leadership wants the bot to tell members whether a
procedure "will be covered" so fewer calls reach agents, and wants the human
handoff button hidden until the fourth failed turn to push containment up.
Can you redesign the claim-denial flow, tell me what has to change for
voice, and tell me how to respond to the two leadership requests? I present
to the VP of member experience on November 6.
