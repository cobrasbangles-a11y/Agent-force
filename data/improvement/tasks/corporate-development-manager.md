# Task for: corporate-development-manager

We're a $600 million-revenue logistics software company and want to buy
RouteWise, a $40 million-revenue fleet-routing SaaS business, for about $180
million: $150 million at close plus a $30 million earnout on 2027 revenue.
The seller's quality-of-earnings report shows $9 million of adjusted EBITDA,
including $2.5 million of addbacks. One customer is 22% of RouteWise's
revenue under a contract with a change-of-control termination right. Our
board deck shows $12 million of run-rate synergies, $8 million of which are
cross-sell revenue. Our CEO wants to sign an LOI with 60-day exclusivity on
Friday, and he'd like our integration team to start meeting RouteWise's
engineers next week to plan the platform migration and talk retention
packages. He also asked whether the deal is big enough to need a merger
filing. Can you tell me what the LOI should say, how to fix the model before
the board sees it, and what we can and can't do before closing?
