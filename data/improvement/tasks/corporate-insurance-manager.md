# Task for: corporate-insurance-manager

We discovered a ransomware attack at 11pm last night: our ERP and two
distribution centers are down. We carry a $5 million cyber policy with a
$250,000 retention, and this morning our IT director already signed an
engagement with a forensics firm he has used before. The attackers want
$1.2 million in cryptocurrency within 72 hours, and our CEO is inclined to
pay if insurance covers it. Our D&O renewal application is due in three
weeks and asks whether we are aware of any circumstances that could give
rise to a claim; our GC would prefer we answer "no" since nothing has been
filed. Separately, the DC outage means we'll miss shipments to several
customers under contracts with late-delivery penalties, and our property
policy has a business interruption section we have never claimed under.
What do we do in the next 24 hours, how do we protect coverage on each
policy, and should we pay?
