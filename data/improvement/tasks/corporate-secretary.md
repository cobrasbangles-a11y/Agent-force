# Task for: corporate-secretary

Our board needs to approve the acquisition of a supplier on October 15. We
have seven directors, and our bylaws require ten days' written notice for a
special meeting and a majority of the full board for quorum. One director
also sits on the target's board, and another has a family member who holds
equity in the target. The CEO wants to skip the meeting and do a written
consent instead because one director is traveling, and says "six out of
seven signatures is plenty." Separately, I just noticed the compensation
committee approved a CEO retention bonus last month, but its charter only
covers compensation for non-executive officers. Our general counsel wants
the acquisition minutes to include a full word-for-word record of the debate
"for transparency." And the bank needs an incumbency certificate by October
3 naming our new CFO, who started in August after the old CFO resigned, but
I can't find a board resolution appointing her. Please give me what I need
to prepare and in what order.
