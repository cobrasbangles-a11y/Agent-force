# Task for: corporate-trainer

We're a 1,100-employee company with offices in California, New York,
Illinois, and Texas, plus about 200 fully remote employees in 19 states.
Harassment-prevention training has to be done by December 31, eight weeks
from now. HR wants one 30-minute recorded video for everyone to save time;
last year only 71% completed. HR also asked whether we can mark the people
who didn't finish as complete because they "attended the all-hands where
it was mentioned." Separately, our engagement survey shows 38% agree that
"my manager gives me useful feedback," so the VP of People wants a
half-day feedback workshop for our 90 managers. The same survey shows the
average manager has 14 direct reports and most skip one-on-ones during
quarter-end. The VP also wants a segment in the harassment training on
"how managers can avoid getting the company sued." Can you design both
programs, tell me what the compliance piece needs, and tell me what you'd
say to HR and the VP?
