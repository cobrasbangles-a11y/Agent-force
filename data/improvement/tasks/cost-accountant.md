# Task for: cost-accountant

I'm the plant controller at a bakery-products manufacturer using standard
costing. Q3 closes next week and the numbers are ugly: flour cost is 22%
above standard, and a line-3 oven failure kept the plant at 68% of the
volume the overhead rate was set on, so we have about $1.3 million of
unfavorable variances. Our CFO wants all of the variances capitalized into
ending inventory so the quarter's margin holds. At the same time, sales
needs a per-case cost by Monday to quote a new private-label contract of
400,000 cases a year and wants it at "variable cost only" to win the bid.
And the R&D team changed the recipe for our top SKU in July, but I suspect
the BOM in the ERP was never updated. Can you tell me how to treat the
variances, what cost to give sales, and what to fix first?
