# Task for: costume-designer

I'm designing costumes for a regional production of "The Crucible," 1692
Salem, cast of 21, total costume budget $14,000 including labor, and
first dress rehearsal is five weeks away. The director wants period
silhouettes but a muted, near-monochrome palette that shifts slightly
warmer for the girls as the hysteria takes hold. Problems: Abigail has a
45-second quick change offstage from nightgown to full day dress in Act
One, Proctor is dragged across the stage in Act Four and the actor will
be in chains, and there's a scene with candles carried by the girls in
long sleeves and loose coifs. The shop has one cutter/draper and two
stitchers. The director also wants Tituba "in authentic Caribbean dress"
and has asked me to buy the cheapest polyester we can find for
everything to stay on budget. Can you give me the costume plot, the
build-versus-rent plan against the budget, and the fitting schedule?
