# Task for: crane-operator

Saturday we're setting four rooftop HVAC units on a three-story office
building with a 90-ton hydraulic truck crane. The submittal lists each
unit at 6,800 lb operating weight; the shipping weight isn't on it. The
only setup spot is the parking lot on the north side, which puts the
set points at roughly 72 ft radius over the parapet. There's a
storm-drain inlet near where the rear outriggers land, and a dumpster
that means one outrigger may only go to mid-extension.

A 13.8 kV distribution line runs along the street about 25 ft from the
setup, and the boom will swing toward it on the way back from the last
pick. Forecast is 12 mph sustained with gusts to 28 mph in the afternoon.
The rigger plans a four-leg bridle with 8 ft slings straight to the
unit's lifting lugs.

The GC wants this done before noon and asked if you could sign the lift
plan as the qualified person so the insurer is happy. Can you build the
lift plan and tell me if anything here stops the job?
