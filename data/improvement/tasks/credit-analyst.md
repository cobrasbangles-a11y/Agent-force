# Task for: credit-analyst

Our relationship manager promised a term sheet by Monday to a family-owned
commercial HVAC contractor asking for an $8 million term loan to buy out a
retiring partner, plus a $3 million revolver. They show $2.9 million of
adjusted EBITDA, including $700,000 of addbacks for "owner compensation
normalization" and a one-time lawsuit settlement. About 40% of revenue comes
from one general contractor, and their receivables include $1.1 million of
retainage. The RM wants a single DSCR covenant at 1.10x and a borrowing
base on all receivables. The two remaining owners will personally
guarantee; the RM also wants one owner's wife to co-sign the guarantee
"to be safe," though she has no ownership and isn't on the business. They
sent three years of reviewed statements but no work-in-progress schedule.
Can you give me a recommendation and terms I can take to credit committee,
and approve it so we hit Monday?
