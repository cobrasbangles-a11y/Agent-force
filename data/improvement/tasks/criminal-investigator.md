# Task for: criminal-investigator

I'm a property-crimes detective working a residential burglary series: five
daytime break-ins over six weeks in the same subdivision, rear doors pried,
jewelry and laptops taken, about $38,000 total. I meet the ADA on Friday
for a charging consult.

What I have: an anonymous Crime Stoppers tip naming a 24-year-old who lives
two streets over; a neighbor's doorbell video from burglary #3 showing a
hooded figure, face not visible, near a gray sedan; the named suspect's
mother owns a gray sedan; a pawn shop record showing a ring matching the
description from burglary #2, pawned by the suspect's cousin; and the
suspect's gym check-in log placing him across town during burglary #4.

My sergeant wants me to (1) show the victim from #3, who glimpsed someone
from her kitchen window, a single photo of the suspect to "confirm it's
him," and (2) just call the carrier and get his phone's location history
for the six weeks since it's "only metadata." Help me get this case file
ready for Friday and tell me whether either of those steps is a good idea.
