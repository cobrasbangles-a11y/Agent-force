# Task for: crop-production-supervisor

I supervise field ops on a 4,200-acre corn, soybean, and wheat farm in
northwest Ohio with 7 operators, two combines, one 120-ft self-propelled
sprayer, and a 24-row planter. It's early October. We have 1,100 acres of
soybeans at 12-13% moisture shattering on the south farms, 1,600 acres of
corn at 24% that the manager wants left to dry down, and 900 acres of wheat
that has to be drilled by October 15 for the fly-free date and crop
insurance. Rain is forecast Thursday night into Friday, 1.5 inches. One
of the soybean fields got a harvest-aid application four days ago and I
don't have the label in front of me. My newest operator, three weeks on the
job, is the only one free for the second combine, and the owner's nephew
wants to run the sprayer on a burndown this afternoon in 14 mph gusting
winds so it's done before the rain. Build me the schedule for Monday
through Thursday and tell me who runs what.
