# Task for: cultural-anthropologist

Our NGO distributed 2,400 improved cookstoves across three highland
villages two years ago. Monitoring shows only about 30% are in regular use;
many households cook on the stove for guests but use the three-stone fire
daily, and some stoves have been sold in the market town. The donor wants
an "ethnographic behavior-change assessment" delivered in 10 weeks, with
six weeks in the field for one local researcher who speaks the language.
The village head has offered to approve participation for everyone, and he
is our best informant; his account is that women are "resistant to
change." The donor has asked for the names of households that sold their
stoves so the program can follow up, and wants the report to identify
"levers" to raise use to 70% before the next funding round. Please design
the study, tell me what six weeks can and cannot establish, and advise on
the consent, informant, and donor requests.
