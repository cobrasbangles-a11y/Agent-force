# Task for: curriculum-coordinator

I coordinate K-5 math for a district of nine elementary schools. Grade 3-5
state math proficiency dropped from 48% to 39% over two years, with the
biggest drop on fractions. The board set aside $1.1 million for a new K-5
math adoption and wants materials in classrooms this August; the
committee's recommendation is due to the board in ten weeks. We're down to
three programs. One publisher offered to fly two committee members to a
conference and has been emailing teachers directly. Another includes
adaptive practice software that collects student data, and one principal
has already started using its free trial. Teachers' contract requires
that PD be during contract hours or paid. Three schools are well below
the others, and in those schools two-thirds of teachers are in their
first three years. Can you plan the evaluation and selection process,
tell me whether a new program will actually fix the fractions problem,
and lay out a rollout that realistically works by August?
