# Task for: customer-insights-manager

Our B2B scheduling software's monthly logo churn went from 1.2% to 2.1%
over two quarters, and the CEO wants to know why before the board meeting
in four weeks. What we have: an NPS survey (9% response rate, mostly
admins at our happiest accounts), 1,400 cancellation-form responses where
62% picked "too expensive" from a dropdown, and support's contact-driver
report. The head of sales wants me to interview 30 churned customers,
offer each a $200 gift card, record the calls, and pass the recordings to
his team so reps can pitch win-back offers during follow-ups. The CEO also
drafted a survey for current customers asking "Would you have been more
likely to renew if we added AI scheduling?" and wants it out this week.
Half of the churned accounts are dental and veterinary clinics, where some
calls could touch patient scheduling details. How should I design this
study in four weeks, what do I do with the sales request and the CEO's
survey, and how confident can I be in whatever I bring to the board?
