# Task for: customer-marketing-manager

I'm the customer marketing manager at a logistics software company. Sales
has 14 active enterprise deals over $250K and every one of them wants a
reference call, mostly with retail or 3PL customers of 5,000+ employees.
We have six referenceable accounts; two of them have done four calls each
this quarter and one champion just told me she's "done for a while." Our
biggest retail customer gave a great quote at our user conference, saying
we cut their mis-shipments by 40%, and sales wants it on the website and in
a press release next week, but their procurement contract has a publicity
clause and the champion's own dashboard actually shows 28%. Our CSM flagged
another account as green, but their renewal is in 60 days and support
tickets tripled last month. Product also wants us to launch a G2 review
campaign offering $50 gift cards only to customers with NPS of 9 or 10.
The VP of Sales wants a plan by Friday. How do I triage the reference
requests, handle the 40% quote, and run the review campaign?
