responses dropped from 2,900 to 1,700. We also had a 6-hour login outage on
September 22. Finance wants to know if we need the 9 extra agents the support
managers asked for, and our CEO wants a one-line answer on whether the bot is
working. I have ticket exports with channel, tag, created/solved timestamps,
status history, and survey scores. What should the analysis look like, what
can I honestly conclude, and what should I recommend?
