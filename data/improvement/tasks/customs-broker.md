# Task for: customs-broker

We import electric bikes into the US and have three containers arriving in
Long Beach in nine days. The supplier invoice says "Country of origin:
Vietnam," but I know the aluminum frames and motors are made in China and
only the final assembly, wheels and packaging happen in Vietnam. Invoice
value is $412,000 for 1,150 bikes; separately we paid the supplier a
$38,000 tooling charge last year for the frame molds and it isn't on the
invoice. Each bike ships with a 48V lithium-ion battery pack. Our old
broker classified them under a heading for "bicycles" at a low rate, and a
competitor told me they use a different subheading with a better rate. The
owner also asked whether we could split future orders into small parcels
shipped direct to customers to avoid duty altogether. I need the
classification, what duties and extra tariffs actually apply, what's
missing from our entry packet, and whether we have a problem with past
entries before the ISF and entry are due.
