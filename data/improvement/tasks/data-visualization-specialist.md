# Task for: data-visualization-specialist

I'm the analytics lead for a county public health department. We need a
one-page chart set for the county commission meeting on October 14 showing
emergency-department visits for opioid overdose by zip code over the last
three years (roughly 2,900 visits total, 41 zip codes, some zips with fewer
than 10 visits per year). The commissioners' main question is whether the
naloxone distribution program launched in March 2025 is working. The
director wants a choropleth map in red with raw counts, a dual-axis chart of
visits against naloxone kits distributed, and she specifically asked me to
start the y-axis at 150 on the monthly trend so "the drop is visible." Two
commissioners are color-blind and the meeting packet is printed in grayscale
too. We also have a request from a local reporter for the zip-level table.
Can you design the chart set, tell me what to push back on, and explain how
to show whether the program is working without overclaiming?
