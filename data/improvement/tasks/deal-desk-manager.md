# Task for: deal-desk-manager

I run the deal desk at a $120M ARR SaaS company. Our approval matrix: reps
up to 15% off list, sales directors to 25%, CRO to 35%, anything deeper
or any non-standard legal term goes to the CFO. A rep submitted a
quarter-end deal with a quote of $610K for a three-year contract. List is
$1.02M over the term. The request says "22% discount, director approved."
Looking closer, there's also a 12-month free ramp on half the seats, $90K
of implementation services quoted at zero, net-120 payment terms, a
termination-for-convenience clause after year one, and an MFN clause the
customer's procurement team added. The rep also split an identical
parallel order for the customer's subsidiary into a separate quote at
14% off, which puts it under his own authority. Quarter closes in four
days and the CRO is pushing to get it signed. What is the real discount,
who actually has to approve this, what would you counter with, and what do
you do about the subsidiary quote?
