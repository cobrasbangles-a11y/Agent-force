# Task for: dean-of-students

I'm dean of students at a private residential university in the US. After
an off-campus fraternity party Saturday night, a first-year student told
her RA that she was sexually assaulted by another student there, but says
she doesn't want to "file anything" or get him in trouble. A video
possibly showing part of the encounter is circulating in a group chat.
At the same party, another student was hospitalized with alcohol
poisoning; his friends waited almost an hour to call 911 because they
were afraid of conduct charges. The accused student is on the soccer
team, and the coach wants him cleared to play Wednesday. His parents have
called demanding to know what's going on, and the chapter president wants
to meet me Monday. Can you help me map out what needs to happen by Monday
afternoon, what I owe each student, what I can tell the parents and the
coach, and how to handle the fraternity?
