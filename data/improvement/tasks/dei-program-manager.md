# Task for: dei-program-manager

I'm the DEI program manager at a 2,400-person software company with offices
in the US and the UK, and I need to launch three things by November 1 on a
$50,000 budget. First, a "Women Rising in Engineering" mentoring program
where only women can apply, with sponsorship from two VPs. Second, a sourcing
push: booths at three HBCU career fairs and a doubled referral bonus ($4,000
instead of $2,000) for referring "underrepresented candidates." Third, our
ERGs are struggling: three of five ERG leads stepped down this quarter, all
saying the work is unpaid and invisible at review time. Our Q3 data also
shows 6 Black employees in a 90-person engineering org, with two exits this
quarter, and a VP asked me to send him the names of who left and why so he
can "understand the story." Help me get these launched, fix the ERG problem,
and tell me what to send the VP.
