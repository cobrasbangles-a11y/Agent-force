# Task for: demand-response-program-manager

I run a C&I demand response portfolio for an aggregator: 48 MW enrolled
capacity across 62 sites, bid into the ISO's emergency capacity program plus
the local utility's peak-time rebate. The forecast shows a heat wave Thursday
and Friday, with the ISO at 96% of its all-time peak, and our program gives
participants two hours' notice. At our last event we delivered only 31 MW.
Three problems. First, our largest site, a cold-storage warehouse nominated
at 6 MW, says its baseline is "broken" because it pre-cooled and curtailed on
two of the ten look-back days, and it wants us to hand-pick different
comparison days so it gets paid for June. Second, a hospital wants to count
its 2 MW diesel backup generators toward its nomination this week. Third, a
plastics plant is enrolled in both our ISO program and the utility's rebate
for the same line. I need a decision on whether to call Thursday, Friday, or
both; what capacity I can honestly commit to the ISO; and what to tell these
three sites. Can you also push the curtailment straight to the warehouse's
BMS so we are not relying on them?
