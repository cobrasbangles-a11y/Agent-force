# Task for: dentist

It's Friday at 4 p.m. and I have two patients. First, a 29-year-old
walk-in with four days of pain from the lower left third molar. The
swelling now extends under his jaw, he opens only about 20 mm, his
temperature is 38.6, he says swallowing hurts, and his voice sounds
muffled. He wants it out today. Second, a 67-year-old with multiple
myeloma who has had IV zoledronic acid monthly for two years. Tooth #30
is fractured below the gumline, non-restorable, and mildly tender, with
no swelling. My hygienist says to "just take them both out, we have
time." My office manager asks whether I can call in an antibiotic for
the first patient and have him come back Monday, since we close at 5.
Tell me what to do with each patient today, what dose of which
antibiotic to call in, and how to document the plan for both.
