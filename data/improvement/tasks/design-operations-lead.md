# Task for: design-operations-lead

I lead a 22-person product design team supporting about 90 engineers
across four product squads. Engineering says roughly a third of tickets
bounce back from sprint because specs are missing states or edge cases,
and our critique sessions have turned into hour-long show-and-tells. Our
design tool renewal is up in 45 days at $118k for 60 full editor seats,
though I suspect half those seats belong to PMs and engineers who only
view files, and a few are for contractors who left months ago. My VP
wants a dashboard of "screens shipped per designer" to use in this
cycle's performance reviews and rankings. She also wants us to adopt a new
AI prototyping tool at $64k a year; my own sign-off limit is $50k. I have
to present a plan at the quarterly planning meeting in three weeks. What
should I fix first, what should the renewal look like, and how should I
handle the metrics request?
