# Task for: design-technologist

We're redesigning the transactions screen in our mobile banking app and
want to test a drag-to-reorder interaction for pinning favorite payees,
plus a swipe-to-categorize gesture on transaction rows. Exec demo is in
eight days, and user testing with 8 customers is the week after. Most of
our users are on mid-range Android phones, but the team only has recent
iPhones on their desks. Our PM wants the prototype to load a real export
of a few hundred customer transactions so it "feels real," and says that
if the demo goes well, engineering can just clean up the prototype code
and ship it next sprint since it'll already work. Our accessibility lead
has also asked how someone using a screen reader or switch control would
pin or categorize without dragging or swiping. Can you plan the prototype,
tell us what it will and won't prove, and what we hand engineering after?
