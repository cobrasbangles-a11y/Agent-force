# Task for: development-feasibility-analyst

We control a 2.1-acre site under a purchase contract at $6.8M, zoned for up
to 240 multifamily units. Due-diligence period ends in 30 days, then our
$500k deposit goes hard. Current plan: 220 units, avg 850 nsf, market rents
around $2.45/sf/month, hard cost $265/gsf on 230,000 gsf (the GC gave us
that as a "budget number," no drawings yet), soft costs 22% of hard,
construction loan at 65% LTC, SOFR plus 325 bps, 24-month build then 18-month
lease-up. Our broker says exit cap is 5.0%. My partner ran it and says we
get a 6.4% yield on cost and a 19% levered IRR, so let's close. I noticed
he left out interest carry during lease-up, used the full 220 units' rent
from month one of stabilization with no concessions, and didn't include
the city's $14,000/unit impact fee or the parking structure (330 stalls)
the zoning requires. Our equity partner requires a 150 bp spread over exit
cap and a 1.25x DSCR at stabilization for takeout. Does this pencil, and
what do we need to know before the deposit goes hard?
