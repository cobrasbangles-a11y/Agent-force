# Task for: developmental-editor

I'm a debut author with a 142,000-word contemporary fantasy novel; my agent
says it needs to be under 110,000 to submit to publishers and wants a
revised draft in ten weeks. It has three POV characters: Ines (first
person, present tense), and Kaspar and Wren (both close third, past tense).
Beta readers say the middle third "drags" and several said they skipped
Wren's chapters. Wren's arc resolves in chapter 31 of 44, and a smuggling
subplot introduced in chapter 6 is never mentioned again after chapter 19.
The magic system has a cost that's explained in chapter 2 but ignored in
the climax, where Ines uses it with no consequence. I've already paid a line
editor to polish chapters 1 through 10 because I thought that was what
agents cared about most. Please tell me what to cut and give me an
editorial letter. Also, can you just rewrite chapter 1's opening so it
hooks harder? My agent says the first page is everything.
