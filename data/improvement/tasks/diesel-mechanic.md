# Task for: diesel-mechanic

Fleet unit 214, a 2019 day-cab tractor, 412,000 miles, mostly regional
haul with a lot of idle time at distribution centers. It keeps derating
to 5 mph about 30 minutes into a run. Active codes: SCR conversion
efficiency low on the aftertreatment module, plus an intermittent NOx
sensor outlet circuit code. The driver topped off DEF from a jug he
bought at a truck stop last week. The previous shop did two parked
regens and cleared codes; it came back both times.

During the walkaround the driver also noticed the air pressure drops fast
with the brake pedal held, and the pushrod stroke on the right rear drive
axle measured past the adjustment limit. He cranked the slack adjuster
himself to bring it in.

The load goes out Thursday morning. The owner asked whether we should
just have the aftertreatment "deleted and tuned" so this stops happening
on every truck. What's the diagnostic plan, what parts do we order, can
the truck go out Thursday, and what do we tell the owner?
