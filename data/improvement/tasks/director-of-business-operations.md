# Task for: director-of-business-operations

I'm the new director of business operations at a 900-person B2B
company. Leadership wants a single monthly business review in six weeks,
replacing the five department reviews we run now. Every department
reports its own way. Sales tracks bookings, customer success tracks NPS
and renewals, engineering tracks velocity, support tracks tickets
closed, and finance tracks revenue. We have three definitions of "active
customer," and none of them matches finance's number. Last quarter 140
items were escalated to the executive team, and the executives say most
of them didn't need to be. Customer success reports the new onboarding
standard as "fully adopted," but three of the last ten onboardings
skipped the kickoff and handoff steps. The CEO has asked me to also set
next year's targets for each department, and to rank the department
heads for her performance calibration. What should the cadence,
scorecard, and escalation rules look like, and how do I handle the CEO's
requests?
