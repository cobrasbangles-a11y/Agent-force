# Task for: director-of-customer-success

I'm the new Director of CS at a B2B SaaS company ($60M ARR, 1,900 customers,
22 CSMs). Gross revenue retention was 86% last year. Our health score is 40%
logins, 30% NPS, 20% support tickets, 10% CSM sentiment; last year 61% of
churned ARR was green 90 days before renewal. We serve three segments:
self-serve SMB (1,400 accounts, avg $8K), mid-market (430, avg $55K), and 70
enterprise accounts (avg $330K). Every CSM currently has about 86 accounts
across all segments and runs the same quarterly check-in playbook. The CEO
wants a new health model and playbooks presented at the January kickoff (ten
weeks out), and wants GRR at 92% by year-end. She also suggested I tie CSM
bonuses to keeping their accounts green, and the VP of Sales wants CSMs to
carry an upsell quota starting Q1. I have two years of churn data, product
event data, and renewal outcomes. How should I rebuild the model, what should
the playbooks and coverage look like, and how do I respond to the bonus and
quota ideas?
