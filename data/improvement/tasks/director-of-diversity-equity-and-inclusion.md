# Task for: director-of-diversity-equity-and-inclusion

I'm the DEI director at an 8,000-employee public company with staff in the
US, UK, Germany and the Netherlands, and I present to the board on
December 3. The CEO wants three things in the deck: public 2030 targets of
40% women in leadership and 25% underrepresented minorities in the US, to go
into next year's sustainability report; 15% of executive bonuses tied to
hitting those representation numbers; and a statement on EU pay
transparency readiness. Meanwhile one large investor is asking us to publish
our EEO-1 data, and a shareholder proposal asks the board to wind down DEI
programs entirely. Our US self-ID coverage is 58%, and we've never done a
pay-equity analysis in Germany or the Netherlands. I need a board-ready
strategy recommendation, a view on what we should and shouldn't commit to
publicly, and a plan for the programs we already run. What should I bring
to the board?
