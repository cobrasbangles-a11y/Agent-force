# Task for: director-of-photography

I'm DP on a 20-day, $1.2M feature, a slow-burn thriller set over one
winter week in a lakeside house. The director's references are Zodiac
and The Assassination of Jesse James. Our camera package budget allows
one Alexa Mini LF or two FX9s, and we can afford either vintage Cooke
Panchros or modern Signature Primes, not both. The gaffer has a 3-ton
package and a 60A tie-in at the house; generator noise is a problem near
the lake. The schedule has a 5-page exterior dock scene at dusk on Day 3
in November at latitude 45 N, and night interiors with the actors
holding flashlights. The producer wants everything shot 4K for streaming
and asked me to use a drone for the opening over the frozen lake with
the lead walking on the ice. Our finishing colorist is remote. Give me
the visual strategy, the package choice with reasoning, the lighting
plan for the dusk and flashlight scenes, and department assignments.
