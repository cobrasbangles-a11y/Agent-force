# Task for: director-of-quality

I'm director of quality at a medical device company with three sites.
Site A makes our Class II devices and is ISO 13485 certified. Site B is
a contract manufacturer we acquired last year; it makes Class I products
and has no certification. Site C builds electronics subassemblies and
has a new automotive customer asking for IATF 16949 within 18 months. I
have $2.4M to allocate next year. Our new Class II infusion accessory
launches in seven weeks, but 2 of 30 units failed design verification
drop testing. The VP of product wants to ship on a deviation and fix it
in a running change. Sales has also asked me to answer "yes, ISO 13485
certified, all sites" on a large hospital system's supplier
questionnaire. And complaint trending shows 9 reports this quarter of a
connector separating during use, one of them involving a patient injury.
The CEO wants my quality plan for next year and my position on the
launch by Friday.
