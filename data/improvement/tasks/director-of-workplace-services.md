# Task for: director-of-workplace-services

I own real estate for a 1,800-person company with nine offices, all
hybrid. Badge data shows average occupancy at 34% of seats, with a
Tuesday peak of 58%. The HQ lease (120,000 sq ft) expires in 20 months,
and the renewal option requires 12 months' written notice. The Chicago
lease auto-renews for five years unless we give notice within 45 days.
The CEO wants real estate costs down 30% by exiting three offices. One
of those three houses our 24/7 support center and the main network room
for the whole company. Our tenant broker, who is also the landlord's
listing agent on our HQ building, is pushing a quick HQ renewal with six
months of free rent. The CEO wants me to sign the broker's renewal LOI
this week. HR has asked for badge data by employee name to enforce the
three-day office policy. I have to present a footprint plan to the
executive team in four weeks. What should it say, and what do I do
right now?
