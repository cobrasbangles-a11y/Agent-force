# Task for: e-learning-developer

I need a 25-minute cybersecurity awareness module built from our
instructional designer's storyboard in three weeks. Our LMS accepts SCORM
1.2 and SCORM 2004. The storyboard calls for a branching phishing
simulation, a 10-question quiz drawn from a 30-question bank with an 80%
pass mark, and the ability to leave and resume where you left off. Legal
wants to be able to see each learner's answer to every question for audit.
About 40% of learners are warehouse staff using shared tablets on
shop-floor Wi-Fi. The subject expert wants a 12-minute embedded 1080p
video of himself, and marketing wants narration in the CEO's voice
generated by an AI clone trained on his old town-hall recordings. Last
year's module showed "incomplete" in the LMS for about 15% of people who
swear they finished it. Can you tell me how you'd build and package this,
which SCORM version to use, and what needs to change before we start?
