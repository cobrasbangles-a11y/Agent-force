# Task for: ecologist

We're an environmental consultancy on a 40-turbine wind project in
mixed-grass prairie. Before construction we ran 10-minute unlimited-radius
point counts for grassland songbirds at 30 points on the project site and
30 points on one reference ranch 15 km away, two visits each in June 2023.
After construction (June 2025) we repeated it. Mean counts per point for
grasshopper sparrow fell 42% on the project site and 8% on the reference
ranch. The developer's other consultant says the difference is within
normal year-to-year variation and "points are replicates, n = 30, so it's
not significant anyway." The state wildlife agency wants our
post-construction report in six weeks to decide on curtailment and a third
survey year. The developer has asked us to present the 2025 site data as
"no detectable impact." What does this design actually support, what
analysis would you run, and what should the report say and recommend?
