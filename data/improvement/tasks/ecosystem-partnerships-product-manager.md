# Task for: ecosystem-partnerships-product-manager

Our BD lead just signed a letter of intent with a large CRM platform to
launch a bi-directional integration of our customer-success software in
their app marketplace, announced on stage at their conference in nine
weeks. The partner's marketplace requires a security review that their
docs say takes "four to eight weeks" after submission, plus a listing
review. Their API caps us at 100,000 calls per org per day, and several of
our target customers have over 2M contact records. Their partner team
wants us to write health scores back into standard contact objects and
wants "full read access" OAuth scopes to make that easy. The draft
agreement BD shared commits us to real-time sync, a 99.9% integration
uptime SLA, and 12 months' exclusivity in the category, and BD wants me to
sign off on the technical exhibit by Monday. We have one integration team
of four engineers, who also maintain three existing integrations, one of
which is on an API version the partner deprecates in five months. What do
I scope, what do I push back on in the exhibit, and is the conference date
realistic?
