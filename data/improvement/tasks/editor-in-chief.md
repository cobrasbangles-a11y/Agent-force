# Task for: editor-in-chief

I'm the managing editor of a 90,000-circulation regional daily. Our
investigations team has a story ready for Sunday: a county sheriff's
deputy, named in the draft, allegedly falsified reports in at least 11 DUI
arrests. Sourcing: four arrest reports that contradict dashcam timestamps
we obtained, two former colleagues on record anonymously, and one on-record
defense attorney. The deputy's lawyer sent a letter Friday threatening to
sue. Outside counsel reviewed it and rated the risk "moderate," mainly over
one sentence saying the deputy "lied under oath," which we can't document.
Complications: one anonymous source was promised by the reporter that we'd
never confirm she works in the sheriff's office, but her details in graf 14
would identify her to anyone there. The sheriff's department is our
third-largest advertiser (about $180,000 a year) and the publisher called
me asking if we "really need to run this before the levy vote." Also, the
draft names two DUI defendants who were never charged. I need your decision
and terms by Friday 5 p.m.
