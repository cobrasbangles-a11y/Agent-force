# Task for: educational-technology-coordinator

I'm the ed tech coordinator for a K-8 district of 3,200 students. Several
grade 4-8 teachers want to use a free AI writing tutor. It requires each
student to create an account with name and email, its terms say users
must be 13 or older, and the privacy policy says inputs may be used to
improve its models. One principal already had about 60 fifth graders sign
up last week, and a parent has complained. Separately, about 900 of our
Chromebooks reach their automatic update expiration in June, and I have
$48,000 left in the device budget. The board wants a recommendation on
AI tools in six weeks, and teachers want something they can use now. Can
you tell me what to do about the students who already signed up, how to
evaluate this tool or a replacement, and how to plan for the Chromebooks?
