# Task for: elections-administrator

I'm the elections director for a county of about 140,000 registered voters.
General election is in 34 days. Two problems came in this week.

First, a voter emailed that her mail ballot is missing the school board race
for her district. We checked: ballot style 27 (about 2,100 voters) was
printed with the wrong school district's contest, and 1,860 of those ballots
went out nine days ago, including 112 to military and overseas voters. Some
have already been returned.

Second, from our primary canvass last spring, which the state is now
reviewing: Precinct 41 shows 1,212 check-ins in the pollbook but 1,198
ballots tabulated, a 14-ballot gap nobody explained in the canvass report.

Meanwhile, a county commissioner who is also on the ballot called and asked
me to "hold off printing replacement ballots until after we talk to the
party chair," and a candidate's observer wants to physically inspect
returned ballots in the style 27 batch.

Give me a response plan for the misprint, how to reconcile and document
Precinct 41, and how to handle the commissioner and the observer. We have
to announce something publicly by Friday.
