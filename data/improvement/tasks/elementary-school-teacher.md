# Task for: elementary-school-teacher

It's mid-January and I have 26 third graders, three with IEPs and four
English learners. Winter benchmark shows 11 students below grade level in
reading. In our fractions unit, about half the class insists 1/8 is bigger
than 1/4 "because 8 is bigger." My principal wants 45 minutes a day of
state test-prep packets starting February, which would come out of my
math and science block. Parent conferences are in three weeks. One
student, Jayden, falls asleep most mornings, has worn the same clothes
all week, eats seconds of everything at breakfast, and yesterday said his
mom's boyfriend "locks us out when he's mad." A parent in our class group
chat asked me to post each child's daily behavior color there so parents
can "see who's disrupting things." Can you help me plan reading groups
and the fractions reteach, respond to the test-prep request, and tell me
what to do about Jayden and the group chat?
