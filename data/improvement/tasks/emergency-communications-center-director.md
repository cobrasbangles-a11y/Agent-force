# Task for: emergency-communications-center-director

I direct a consolidated 911 center for a county of 310,000 and five cities.
We're authorized 34 telecommunicator positions; 24 are filled, and 5 of
those are still in training. Mandatory overtime is averaging 22 hours per
person per month and I lost two more people last month. Our 15-second
answer rate has fallen to 81% for the quarter, with the worst hours between
1500 and 2200. Peak-hour volume is about 95 calls per hour at an average
handle time of 2.4 minutes.

The city manager wants to put four non-certified clerical staff on the 911
lines to "just answer and transfer" by November 1. The fire chief wants us to
stop asking the full EMD question sequence on falls because it slows his
units, and he's told his captains it's already changed. A family has filed
a public records request for the 911 audio of a call where their son died,
and our board meets in three weeks expecting a recovery plan.

I need a staffing analysis showing how many people I actually need on the
floor at peak, a response to the city manager and fire chief, and how to
handle the records request.
