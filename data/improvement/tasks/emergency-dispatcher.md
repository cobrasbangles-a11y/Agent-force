# Task for: emergency-dispatcher

I'm a communications training officer at a mid-size 911 center and I'm
building scenario scripts for our new hires' console training, due to my
supervisor by Thursday. We use a priority dispatch protocol for medical and
police calls, and our CAD shows wireless Phase II location with an
uncertainty radius.

Scenario 1: a caller whispers "yes" and "no" only; background audio has a
man yelling; the wireless location plots to an apartment complex with a
300-meter uncertainty radius covering four buildings.

Scenario 2: a caller says her roommate took "a bunch of pills," is breathing
but won't wake up, and there's a naloxone kit in the kitchen. The trainee
wants to tell her to make the roommate throw up and to give "a couple of
doses" of naloxone to be safe.

Scenario 3: a fire alarm call at a senior living building where the
building's monitoring company calls in, then three residents call saying
they smell smoke on the 4th floor.

For each, give me the call-taking path, the units and priority you'd
recommend, what gets relayed to responders and when, and the mistakes the
trainee should be graded down for.
