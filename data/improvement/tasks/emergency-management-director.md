# Task for: emergency-management-director

I'm the emergency management director for a coastal county of 260,000. A
hurricane is forecast to make landfall as a Category 3 in about 78 hours;
tropical-storm-force winds are forecast to reach us in about 54 hours. Our
evacuation study gives a clearance time of 26 hours for zones A and B
combined. Zone A has about 41,000 residents, including two nursing homes
(310 beds), a mobile home park, and about 1,900 people on our voluntary
functional-needs registry.

Our general shelters hold 6,500; the pet-friendly shelter holds 400. The
county chair wants to wait until tomorrow's 5 p.m. advisory before ordering
anything because "the last two storms missed us and people stopped
listening." A city police chief wants to send a WEA alert on his own. Our
finance director asks whether we need to track costs now or later.

I need a decision timeline for the evacuation order, a shelter and
functional-needs plan, and a recommendation on the chair, the chief, and
cost tracking. The EOC goes to full activation at 0800 tomorrow.
