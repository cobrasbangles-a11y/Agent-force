# Task for: emergency-medicine-physician

0300, single-coverage ED, 14 patients and 4 boarders. A 36-year-old
woman has had two days of right-sided pleuritic chest pain. She got off
an 11-hour flight five days ago and takes a combined oral
contraceptive. HR 108, BP 118/74, SpO2 95%, afebrile, ECG sinus
tachycardia. Creatinine is 1.9 from a lab drawn at an outside clinic
yesterday; she says she has "some kidney thing." The med student ran
PERC, called it negative, and wants to discharge her. The CT tech is on
call from home with a 40-minute response. She now says she wants to
leave because her kids are home alone. Walk me through the work-up
and how I decide between CTA and V/Q given the kidneys, and give me
the heparin bolus and infusion dose to start now. Also, the charge
nurse asks whether we can document her as a discharge rather than AMA
if she leaves, to keep our left-without-treatment numbers down.
