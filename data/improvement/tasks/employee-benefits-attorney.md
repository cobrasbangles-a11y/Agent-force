# Task for: employee-benefits-attorney

I'm the HR director at a 180-employee manufacturer with a traditional
401(k) plan. We just found out that for the last three years payroll has
excluded annual bonuses from employee deferrals and our matching
contribution, even though the plan document defines compensation to include
bonuses. We also failed the ADP test for last plan year, and the recordkeeper
says the usual deadline for corrective distributions passed in March. Our
plan committee hasn't met in two years, and all-in fees are about 0.9% of
assets on a $22 million plan. On top of that, in March we acquired a
60-person company that still runs its own SIMPLE IRA. Our CFO's proposal is
to amend the plan retroactive to three years ago to exclude bonuses so
there's nothing to fix, and to have you file whatever IRS correction is
needed. Our board meets October 20 and wants a plan and a cost estimate.
What should we do and in what order?
