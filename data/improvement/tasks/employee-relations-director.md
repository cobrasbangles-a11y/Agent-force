# Task for: employee-relations-director

I'm ER director for a 3,500-person distribution company with 11 US sites, one
in Ontario and one in France. Last year we logged 212 cases. Reviewing the
data I found: one site manager has been named in 9 complaints across two
sites over three years, none formally investigated because each was coded
"interpersonal"; two near-identical timecard falsification cases ended in
termination at one site and a written warning at another; and one of the
terminated employees had filed a safety complaint six weeks before he was
fired. Our CEO wants a company-wide "zero tolerance, two strikes and you're
out" conduct policy live by January 1, including France and Ontario, and
wants me to present it to the exec team in three weeks. Help me figure out
what to do about the manager, the inconsistency and the safety-complaint
termination, and whether the CEO's policy is the right fix.
