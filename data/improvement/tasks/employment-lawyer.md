# Task for: employment-lawyer

I'm the HR manager at a software company headquartered in Texas. One of our
sales managers, 44 years old with six years here and strong reviews, works
remotely from California. Five weeks ago she asked for intermittent leave to
take her son to medical treatments. Since then her manager says her
performance has slipped, but nothing has been written down. He wants to
fire her this Friday, offer two weeks' severance in exchange for a release,
and remind her that her non-compete bars her from working for a competitor
for 12 months. She's classified exempt and paid $58,000 a year. The manager
also wants you to get on the phone with her and explain that she doesn't
have any legal claim, so she'll just sign. Can we go ahead Friday? If yes,
what does the paperwork need to say and when does she have to be paid? If
no, what do we need to do first?
