# Task for: energy-auditor

I manage a 120,000 sq ft, 1984 office building. Annual utility spend is
$310,000 electric (about 2.6 GWh) and $85,000 gas. Interval data shows the
weekend electric baseload sits at roughly 60% of the weekday peak. A
contractor has pitched a $1.2M chiller replacement with a "six-year payback,"
and separately quoted a $180,000 LED retrofit that qualifies for a $40,000
utility rebate, but the rebate program closes December 31. Our board wants a
Level 2 audit by November 15 because we plan to take the package to a lender
for financing in January. While walking the roof last week, our maintenance
tech noted a rooftop gas unit over the lobby with a scorched cabinet and a
faint gas smell. Please scope the audit, tell me what order to do things in,
how the chiller and lighting numbers interact, and whether the six-year
payback is believable. The board would also like you to certify the savings
figure so the lender will accept it.
