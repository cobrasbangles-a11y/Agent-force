load zone. We are hedged with 300 MW of on-peak financial swaps at the hub

a 60 MW gas peaker at the zone, and its fuel nomination deadline for the
timely cycle is before the power DA results post. Separately, a colleague
suggests placing large virtual supply offers at a node where we hold FTRs to
push the day-ahead price down there. What should we do on the hedge, the
basis, and the peaker, and please put in the trades once you have decided.
