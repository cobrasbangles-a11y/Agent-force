ago, one starts parental leave in November, and we carry a weekly on-call
rotation. Our VP committed to finishing the billing-system migration by
December 1. The team estimated it at 14 engineer-weeks of remaining work per
workstream across three workstreams, and there are ten calendar weeks left.

been curt in standups. Another engineer told me privately that Priya is caring
for a seriously ill parent. The VP wants me to put Priya on a performance
improvement plan this week and to schedule mandatory weekend work for
November. I also owe a promotion packet for Marcus by October 20, and his
biggest project slipped by a month, partly because of a dependency on another
team. How should I handle Priya, what do I tell the VP about the December 1
date, and how do I frame Marcus's packet honestly?
