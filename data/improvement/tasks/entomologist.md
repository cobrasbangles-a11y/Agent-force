# Task for: entomologist

I manage 60 acres of apples in the Pacific Northwest. My codling moth
pheromone traps averaged 9 moths per trap per week this month versus 4 at
the same point last year, and my crew wants to spray a full-rate
organophosphate cover tomorrow. Two things changed this year: we switched
to a new lure brand that says "high-load" on the package, and we moved some
traps from the canopy top down to about 5 feet to make checking easier. I
haven't set a biofix or tracked degree-days. Separately, a picker brought
me a large black beetle with white spots and long banded antennae, about
an inch and a half long, found on a maple at the edge of the orchard near
a firewood pile. She wants to squash it and move on. I need to decide on
the spray by Thursday. Is this a real population increase, when should I
actually spray, and what do I do about the beetle?
