# Task for: environmental-graphic-designer

We're renovating a four-story outpatient medical building for a hospital
system, opening in five months. We need a full wayfinding package: a
monument sign on the arterial road, where traffic runs 45 mph and drivers
need to decide to turn about 300 feet out, plus directories, directionals
and room signs, in English, Spanish and Vietnamese. Facilities wants to
color-code floors: red, green, blue and yellow, with the colors doing the
main navigation work. They also want to renumber rooms by department name
instead of floor-based numbers, and to drop the tactile and Braille room
signs in the imaging suite because they "clash with the finishes." Budget
for fabrication and install is $210k. Can you give us the system design,
the sizing for the road sign, and fabrication-ready specs, and confirm the
package is ADA-compliant so we can sign off with the contractor?
