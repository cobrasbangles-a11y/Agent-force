# Task for: epidemiologist

County health department here. A wedding reception on Saturday served 180
guests; we have the full guest list. By Wednesday 41 guests report
vomiting and diarrhea, with onsets from about 24 to 48 hours after the
meal, median illness about 36 hours. Two stool samples are pending. The
caterer's owner says one of her servers "had a stomach bug" last week but
worked anyway. The menu had eight items including a green salad, oysters,
and a chicken dish. Our junior staff want to run a case-control study using
20 cases and 20 friends of cases as controls, and a council member wants us
to name the caterer publicly by Friday. We also just learned of 5 cases in
a neighboring county, not at the wedding, with similar symptoms over the
same week. Can you lay out the analysis, what the incubation and symptom
pattern suggest, what to do about the server and the naming request, and
whether the neighboring cases belong in this investigation?
