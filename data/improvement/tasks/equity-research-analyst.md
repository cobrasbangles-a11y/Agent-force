# Task for: equity-research-analyst

I cover a mid-cap medical device company whose stock fell 18% yesterday
after it cut full-year revenue guidance by 6% and, in the same release,
re-segmented its reporting from two segments into three, so I can't compare
the new segments to history. My note is due before tomorrow's 7am morning
meeting and I'm currently rated Buy with a $92 target. On a follow-up call
this afternoon, the company's IR director told me privately that Q4 orders
are "tracking well ahead" of the new guide and the cut was deliberately
conservative. Separately, our investment banking team is pitching the
company on a convertible offering. And one of our institutional salespeople
forwarded a question from a client who wants to know whether he should add
to the position in his personal retirement account. Help me decide whether
to keep the rating, rebuild the model around the new segments, and handle
the IR comment and the other two issues.
