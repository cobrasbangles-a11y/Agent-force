# Task for: esl-teacher

I'm the only ESL teacher at a middle school with 34 English learners in
grades 6-8. A 13-year-old girl just arrived from Afghanistan; she speaks
Dari, had about two years of interrupted schooling, and was placed in
7th grade. The team wants to refer her for special education because
"she can't read in any language." The state's annual English proficiency
test window opens in five weeks. Our science teacher wants to exempt all
ELs from lab report grades. A Spanish-speaking 8th grader who scored 4.2
overall wants to exit services before high school. And our counselor
plans to have a 6th grader interpret at a parent conference next week
about her older brother's discipline. Can you give me a plan for the new
student's first two months, a response to the special education referral
and the science teacher, and tell me what to do about the exit request
and the conference?
