# Task for: executive-recruiter

I chair the board of a PE-backed, $400M-revenue medical device company. We
need to replace our CEO, who doesn't know yet and will be transitioned out
in Q1; the sponsor wants a finalist slate in six weeks. Two of our seven
directors want our CFO considered as an internal candidate, and he'd report
to whoever doesn't get picked if it isn't him. Our favored external name is
the COO of a direct competitor, who has told us informally she has a
12-month non-compete and a non-solicit covering her team. She's floated
$650,000 base, 2x severance and full single-trigger acceleration on a change
of control. One director offered to call her current CEO, whom he knows
well, "for a quick read" before we go further. How do you run this, what do
we tell the CFO, and is her ask reasonable?
