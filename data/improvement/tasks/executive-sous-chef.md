# Task for: executive-sous-chef

I'm the executive sous at a resort with five outlets: steakhouse, Italian,
pool grill, lobby café, and banquets, all sharing one central prep kitchen.
Saturday we have a 1,200-person convention lunch in banquets on top of a
normal 320-cover Saturday in the steakhouse. Two outlet sous chefs are out
next week, and steakhouse ticket times have climbed from 14 to 21 minutes
over the last three weekends. The steakhouse sous blames the line, but the
central prep kitchen has been delivering portioned proteins late since we
lost a butcher. Our hourly cooks are already averaging 46 hours and HR has
capped overtime. On a walk-through yesterday I found the Italian outlet
cooling 5-gallon buckets of marinara, lidded, in the walk-in overnight, and
they've been doing it for weeks. The executive chef also wants his new
braised lamb shank on every dinner menu by next Friday. Can you help me
staff Saturday across outlets, figure out the real cause of the steakhouse
ticket times, and tell me how to handle the marinara and the lamb rollout?
