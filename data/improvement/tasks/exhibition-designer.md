# Task for: exhibition-designer

We're a mid-size art museum opening a 400 square meter temporary show in
14 weeks: 60 objects, including 22 nineteenth-century watercolors, 8
embroidered textiles, oil paintings, and a few bronzes. Eleven works are
on loan from two lenders. The curator wants a dramatic, dark gallery with
spotlit works at about 250 lux so the watercolors "glow," and wants all
60 objects plus 40 wall texts averaging 180 words in the room. Average
visit to a show like this is 35 minutes. Our fabricator proposes building
the display cases from MDF with standard latex paint to save money, and a
corporate sponsor wants a branded lounge with two touchscreen kiosks
inside the gallery. The build budget is $280k. Can you plan the layout,
visitor flow, lighting, and cases, and tell us what has to change before
we go to fabrication in six weeks?
