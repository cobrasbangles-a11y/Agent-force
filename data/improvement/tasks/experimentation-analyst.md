18 other metrics and "add-to-wishlist" and "mobile conversion" both showed
big wins, which the PM wants to headline. The VP wants a ship decision on
Friday, October 3, and the PM is asking me to write it up as "conversion up
6%, ship it." Separately, the pricing team wants to run their next test
showing different shipping fees to different users. Can you tell me whether
this result holds up, what I should write up, and how to set up the next
test properly?
