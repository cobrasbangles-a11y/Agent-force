# Task for: export-sales-manager

I'm export sales manager for a US maker of industrial flow meters and
ultrasonic thickness gauges ($22M revenue, 30% export). Three live
situations. A new distributor in the UAE wants 200 thickness gauges
($340K) on CIF Jebel Ali, open account net 90, and says the end users are
"oil and gas customers in the region"; their order form lists a
consignee in a free zone and a notify party I don't recognize. Second, our
long-time distributor in Germany, exclusive for 11 years, is underperforming
and my CEO wants to terminate them on the contract's 90-day notice and sign
a bigger competitor next month. Third, a Brazilian customer wants a $1.1M
three-year supply contract priced in reais, with an irrevocable letter of
credit from a local bank, and wants us to handle import clearance. Our
product classification was done years ago and nobody has checked it since.
How should I handle each one, what terms should I propose, and what can I
realistically promise on delivery and payment timing?
