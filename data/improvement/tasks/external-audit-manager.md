# Task for: external-audit-manager

I'm managing the December year-end audit of a privately held manufacturer
(AICPA standards, $210 million revenue). Overall materiality is $1.5 million
and performance materiality $1.1 million. Our revenue cutoff sample found
$620,000 of goods recorded as December sales that shipped January 3, drawn
from a sample covering about a quarter of the last-week population. The
client breached its leverage covenant at year-end and its lender hasn't
granted a waiver yet; the CFO needs our report by February 15 for the lender
and says the breach is "just a timing thing." She has also asked our firm to
draft the financial statements and footnotes this year because her
accounting manager left. And our audit senior mentioned that her husband just
accepted a job as the client's AP supervisor. How should I handle the cutoff
finding, the going-concern question, the drafting request, and the senior,
and can we still hit February 15?
