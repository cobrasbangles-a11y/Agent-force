lease) and facilities requests land on me until our new coordinator starts.

tiles near the IT closet has grown from about one tile to four since Monday,
and one tile is sagging. Our IT guy says he'll just pop the tiles out
himself this afternoon to see where the water is coming from. Second, an
HVAC contractor needs after-hours badge access Saturday and Sunday to work
in the mechanical room, and the project lead in marketing emailed "approved"
since she's the one who booked them. Third, we ran out of paper towels in
both restrooms three weeks in a row even though we restock every Monday, and
someone has started propping the rear stairwell door open with a wedge so
people can carry boxes to the loading area. My manager wants the tickets
cleaned up and a plan for each of these by Friday. What should I do, in what
order, and who needs to hear about what?
