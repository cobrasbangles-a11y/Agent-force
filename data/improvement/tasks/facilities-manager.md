# Task for: facilities-manager

I manage facilities for three leased office sites (about 210,000 sq ft
total). Budget submissions for next fiscal year are due in three weeks and I
have a mess. Site A's 22-year-old rooftop unit serving the third floor lost
its compressor last month; the HVAC vendor quotes $38,000 for a new
compressor or $176,000 to replace the unit, and my capital line is $120,000
for all three sites. Our controller suggested booking the full replacement
as a "repair" in operating expense so it doesn't eat capital, and splitting
it across two fiscal years. At Site B, I just found out the five-year
internal pipe inspection on the sprinkler system is eight months overdue and
the vendor wants to shut the system down for a day to do it. Separately,
badge data shows Site C's second floor averaging 34% peak occupancy against
240 desks, and the VP wants to know if we can give the floor back when the
lease option comes up in 14 months. Help me build the budget position and a
plan for all three.
