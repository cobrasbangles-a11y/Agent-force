# Task for: family-law-attorney

I'm an associate at a small family law firm preparing for a new client
meeting. The client is a wife married 11 years with two children, ages 6 and
9. Her husband owns 60% of an HVAC company he founded two years before the
marriage. His tax returns show $140,000 in income, but their lifestyle runs
closer to $300,000. She received a $200,000 inheritance six years ago that
went into their joint account and was used for the house down payment. He
has talked about taking a job two states away and bringing the kids. She
wants to move out with the children next week and take $80,000 from the
joint account first. She also mentioned he shoved her last month and she
kept the texts where he apologized. She asks whether she can secretly record
his calls and log into his email to find hidden money. The partner wants a
strategy memo by October 10 and plans to file by October 15. What should the
memo cover and what do we tell her at this first meeting?
