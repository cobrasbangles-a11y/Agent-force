# Task for: farm-business-consultant

We farm 2,300 acres of corn and soybeans in Iowa, 1,500 of it rented. Our
APH on corn dropped from 205 to 188 after the 2023 drought and derecho,
and I'm not sure if we took the yield exclusion. My insurance agent wants
me on 80% revenue protection with enterprise units for next year; my dad
insists we've always done 75% optional units. The sales closing date is
March 15. Our operating line is $1.4 million at the bank with a
working-capital covenant of 25% of gross revenue, and last year we ended at
about 22% after a big machinery purchase. Two landlords want to renew at
$310/acre cash rent on 480 acres; their ground has averaged 195 bu corn and
58 bu beans. My banker suggested I prepay next year's fertilizer in
December to cut taxes, and a neighbor said I should put the prepay on the
operating line. I have a lender meeting on February 20. What insurance
should I elect, can I afford the rent, and how do I fix the covenant
before the meeting?
