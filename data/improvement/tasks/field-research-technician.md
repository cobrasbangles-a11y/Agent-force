# Task for: field-research-technician

I'm the PI on a watershed study. Next month you'll run a 3-week campaign
at 12 stream sites in the mountains (1,800-2,600 m), each visited every
three days: grab samples for E. coli, nitrate, and total phosphorus, plus
dissolved oxygen, temperature, and conductivity from our multiparameter
sonde. The contract lab is a 7-hour drive; courier pickup is twice a week.
The sonde was calibrated at the university (elevation 200 m) last week and
you'll carry it up. Site coordinates in the protocol came from a 1990s
report and are in NAD27; our GIS is WGS84. Site 9 is only reachable by
fording a creek that runs high on warm afternoons, and site 11's original
spot is now a beaver pond, so move it wherever looks similar. Please send
me your field plan: sampling schedule, preservation and shipping, sonde
calibration, QA samples, and how you'll handle sites 9 and 11. I need it by
Monday to book the lab.
