# Task for: financial-aid-advisor

I'm an advisor at a private four-year college and I need to answer a
family by Friday, when our May 1 deposit deadline forces their decision.
The student's 2026-27 FAFSA shows a Student Aid Index of 18,400 on parent
AGI of $112,000. Her father was laid off in February and is now on
unemployment of about $2,100 a month, and the family has asked for a
professional-judgment review. Our cost of attendance is $78,500 and her
current package is a $22,000 institutional grant, a $5,500 Direct
Subsidized/Unsubsidized loan, and $3,000 of work-study. She also just won
a $10,000 outside scholarship from a local foundation, and the mother
assumed it would simply come off what they owe. The file was selected for
verification and the household size on the FAFSA says 5, but the tax
return claims 4 dependents plus the parents. The mother also mentioned on
the phone that her ex-husband, who is not on the FAFSA, pays half the
tuition informally, and asked whether she needs to mention that. What can
I tell them, what do I have to resolve first, and what should the revised
package look like?
