# Task for: financial-risk-manager

We're a US-based industrial manufacturer. We carry a $400M floating-rate
term loan at SOFR plus 250 bps, we earn about EUR 120M a year from European
customers against almost entirely USD costs, and we hold $85M of cash and
short-term investments plus a $150M undrawn revolver. Our leverage covenant
is 3.5x and we're at 3.1x with EBITDA softening. Our CFO wants to lock in a
fixed rate on the full $400M with a swap and hedge 100% of next year's
forecast euro revenue with forwards, and she wants us to execute both by
Friday. Separately, the bank that is our largest derivative counterparty
and a revolver lender was downgraded two notches last week, and treasury
reports our liquidity headroom as $235M. Our board-approved policy caps
hedging at 75% of forecast exposure beyond six months. Can you size the
exposures, tell us what to hedge and how much, reassess liquidity and
counterparty risk, and get the trades done?
