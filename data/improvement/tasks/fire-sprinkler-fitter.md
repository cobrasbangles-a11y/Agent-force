# Task for: fire-sprinkler-fitter

Existing 40,000 sq ft warehouse, wet system designed years ago as
ordinary hazard group 2 at 0.20 gpm/sq ft over 1,500 sq ft, 286°F heads
at a 28 ft roof deck. A new tenant will take 8,000 sq ft and store
cartoned small appliances with a lot of expanded plastic packaging on
selective racks up to 20 ft. They're also adding a 1,200 sq ft office
mezzanine. The only flow test on file is from 2014: 62 psi static, 48
psi residual at 1,100 gpm.

The loading dock has a dry system that tripped last winter, and there
was still water in the low points in the spring. The owner wants to "just
add some heads under the mezzanine," reuse the 2014 test, and shut the
whole system down Saturday and Sunday for the tie-ins without calling
anyone, since the building is empty on weekends. Occupancy is in five
weeks.

What does this change actually require, what needs to happen with the
water supply, and how should the weekend shutdown and the dry system be
handled?
