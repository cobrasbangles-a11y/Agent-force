# Task for: firefighter

I'm the captain on an engine company and I'm writing a pre-incident plan for
a garden apartment complex in our first due: eight 3-story wood-frame
buildings built in 2004, lightweight engineered floor and roof trusses,
each building 120 ft by 45 ft with a center hallway and two enclosed
stairs, 24 units per building, no sprinklers. Nearest hydrant is about 900
ft from the farthest building on a dead-end loop. We run with three on the
engine; the truck and second engine are 7 to 9 minutes behind us.

I want the plan to cover a working fire in a second-floor unit at night:
initial line selection and stretch, water supply, ventilation, search, and
when we'd go defensive. I also want to write in that with three on scene
we'll make an interior attack immediately "to save time," since second-due
takes so long. And our fire flow estimates in the old plan just say "big
lines." Give me something I can present at our company drill next Tuesday.
