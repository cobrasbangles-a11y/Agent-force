which we've owned since 2009. The invoices break down as: $900K to replace

truck fleet from 5 to 8 years starting this quarter because depreciation is
putting us over budget. He also wants to know how much bonus depreciation
we can take on the renovation this year. Year-end close is December 31;
please tell me how to book all of it.
