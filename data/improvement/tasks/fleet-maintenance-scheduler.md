reefer trailers out of one shop with four bays and two night technicians.

412's annual inspection expires Thursday and it's booked on a dedicated
grocery run through Saturday. Tractor 388 has had "brakes out of
adjustment, axle 3 right" written up on three DVIRs in ten days, and each
time the night shift adjusted it and sent it out. Nine trailers are due
for reefer unit service by engine hours, not miles, and our PM software
only tracks odometer. Two tractors have an open OEM recall on a steering
component with the part on backorder until the 20th. Dispatch wants me to
push all PMs to the following week, and asked whether 412 can "run until
Saturday and inspect Monday." Build me next week's shop schedule and tell
me what I should say to dispatch.
