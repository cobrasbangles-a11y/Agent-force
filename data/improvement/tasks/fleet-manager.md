# Task for: fleet-manager

I manage a 140-unit fleet for a regional building-supply distributor: 90
box trucks (mix of 26,000 lb and 33,000 lb GVWR), 30 cargo vans and 20
tractors. About a third of the box trucks are 7-9 years old with 280,000+
miles and maintenance cost per mile has roughly doubled in two years. The
CFO wants a replacement plan and budget request by the end of the month,
and she's attracted to a vendor quote for battery-electric box trucks that
leans on a purchase incentive and "fuel savings of 70%." Our utilization
report says the box trucks average 62% of available days, but in spring
we rent 15 trucks for about 12 weeks. Operations also wants the new trucks
spec'd at 33,000 lb GVWR "for flexibility," though we're struggling to
hire CDL drivers. Our maintenance vendor contract renews in 60 days, and
the vendor wants a 9% labor-rate increase. Give me the replacement plan,
the EV analysis, the spec recommendation, and how to approach the vendor.
