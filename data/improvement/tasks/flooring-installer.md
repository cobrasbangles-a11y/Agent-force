# Task for: flooring-installer

Third-floor condo in a 1968 concrete building, about 1,200 sq ft. The
owner wants to replace carpet with a 7 in. wide rigid-core luxury vinyl
plank, floating, run continuously through the living room, kitchen, and
hallway with no transition strips. The kitchen cabinets and a 4 x 8 ft
island are already in, and the owner wants the plank run under the island
"so it's done right." The HOA requires a minimum sound rating on
hard-surface floors and approval before work starts.

When we pulled a corner of carpet in the hallway, there is old 9 x 9 in.
vinyl tile over black adhesive. The owner wants us to scrape and grind it
off so the new floor sits flat. He checked the slab with a pin moisture
meter and says it's "dry." A straightedge shows a 1/4 in. dip over about
6 ft in the living room.

The tenant moves in in two weeks. Can you give me the installation plan,
the prep, and tell me what has to happen before we start?
