# Task for: food-and-beverage-director

I direct F&B at a 420-room full-service branded hotel: a signature
restaurant, a lobby bar, banquets, and in-room dining. We're at $14.2M in F&B
revenue year to date, but departmental profit is 24% against a 29% budget,
and the GM wants a recovery plan and a draft of next year's budget in three
weeks. By outlet: banquet labor is 6 points over budget since the new union
wage scale took effect in May; the restaurant is running 36% food cost; the
bar's pour cost is 24%; and in-room dining loses about $310K a year. The GM
wants to shut IRD entirely. My bar manager's fix for the bar is a weekend
bottomless-mimosa brunch and a two-for-one happy hour. The restaurant manager
says her food cost is high because banquets borrow product from her walk-in
and never transfer it on paper. Can you tell me which of these numbers are
real problems, what to do about IRD and the bar ideas, and how to structure
next year's budget?
