# Task for: food-safety-manager

I'm the food safety manager at a hotel with a 220-seat restaurant and a
banquet kitchen. Last week's routine inspection cited us for cooling:
20-quart stockpots of chicken stock and hotel pans of rice 4 inches deep
cooling in the walk-in, logged at 70°F after three hours. A reach-in on the
garde manger line was also holding at 45°F. The re-inspection is in 10
days. Separately, three guests from Saturday's 180-person wedding have
emailed the sales office about vomiting and diarrhea starting that night.
The GM wants to offer them a refund and keep it internal "until we know
more." And the executive chef wants to launch a sous-vide and cook-chill
program next month: vacuum-packed short rib and chicken breast, cooked,
chilled, and held up to 10 days. Can you help me fix the cooling problem
before re-inspection, tell me what we actually have to do about the wedding
illness reports, and update our HACCP plan for the sous-vide program?
