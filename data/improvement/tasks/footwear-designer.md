# Task for: footwear-designer

We're designing a safety work boot for warehouse and distribution workers
who stand and walk 10-hour shifts on sealed concrete, some near wet
loading docks. Target landed cost is $42 per pair, retail $119, launching
to a large retail customer in ten weeks. Requirements are a composite toe,
slip resistance, and electrical hazard protection. To hit cost, our
product manager wants to switch from the Goodyear welt construction on our
current boot to a cemented sole, and wants a knit upper for breathability.
Sizing is US men's 7 to 15, but the customer also wants wide widths and a
women's size run "from the same last." Marketing wants to print "meets
ASTM safety standards" on the box and hangtag for the retailer's catalog
deadline in three weeks, though we haven't sent anything to a test lab
yet. Can you lay out the design direction, construction, materials, and
what's realistic for the timeline?
