# Task for: forecasting-analyst

I need a weekly demand forecast for our 1,800-SKU garden-supply catalog for
next spring (weeks 5 through 26 of 2027) so our buyer can place orders with
overseas suppliers who have 14-week lead times, with POs due by November 15.
We have four years of weekly sales, but 2024 had a record wet spring and in
2025 we ran out of stock on our top 120 SKUs for about six weeks, so recorded
sales were way below real demand. We're also opening 12 new stores in March
and moving Easter promos, since Easter 2027 falls in late March. Last year's
forecast had a 38% MAPE at the SKU-week level and the buyer lost trust. The
CFO wants a single number per SKU "so we can just order it," and the buyer
wants to know how much safety stock to hold. What approach would you take,
what do you need from me, and what should I hand the buyer?
