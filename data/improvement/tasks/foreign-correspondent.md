# Task for: foreign-correspondent

I'm filing tonight from a provincial capital in a country where security
forces broke up a protest over bread prices this afternoon. I need a 900-word
story for a U.S. general audience that knows nothing about the country.
What I have: state television reports "three injured, no deaths"; a local
hospital doctor told me, through my fixer Amira, that she saw at least seven
bodies, but she asked me not to name her or the hospital; a protest
organizer named Karim wants his full name and photo used "so the world
knows"; a video circulating on Telegram purports to show troops firing, but
its metadata is stripped and a similar clip went viral during unrest two
years ago. The ministry's press office called my hotel and hinted my
accreditation renewal next month depends on "balanced" coverage. My editor
wants me to compare the protests to the Arab Spring in the lede. Amira
translated one chant as "death to the government," but told me afterward
it's closer to "down with." Write the story and tell me how you handled each
of these.
