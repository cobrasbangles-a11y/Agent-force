# Task for: foreign-service-officer

I'm a political officer at an embassy. The host country holds a
presidential election in 11 days. Tonight a longtime opposition contact told
me over dinner that (a) the ruling party has a list of 40 opposition
organizers to be detained the night before the vote, (b) his source is a
cousin in the interior ministry, and (c) he wants a public statement from
our embassy and a promise we'll protect him if he's arrested. He also
mentioned militants may target a hotel popular with foreigners on election
day, though he was vague. Separately, a local journalist texted asking me
for comment on "the embassy's view of the election's fairness," and I just
learned a dual national from our country was detained at a rally this
afternoon.

The ambassador wants a cable on my desk by 0900 tomorrow and has a meeting
with the foreign minister at noon, where we've been instructed to deliver
talking points on election observer access. Help me structure the cable,
tell me how to handle each of these threads, and prepare the démarche
record.
