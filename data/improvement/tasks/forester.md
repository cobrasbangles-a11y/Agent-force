# Task for: forester

I own 320 acres in south Georgia that my dad planted to loblolly pine: about
140 acres planted in 2004 (never thinned), 120 acres planted in 2011 that
got a first thinning in 2023, and 60 acres of bottomland hardwood along a
creek that feeds into a named river. A timber buyer just offered me $185,000
lump sum to "clear-cut everything" including the creek bottom, and wants an
answer in three weeks. He says the 2004 stand is "past due."

The 2004 stand's cruise from 2019 showed about 160 square feet of basal area
per acre, site index 70 (base age 25), and there's been some southern pine
beetle activity reported two counties over. I want to know whether the 2004
stand should be thinned or harvested now, whether the 2011 stand should be
touched, what I lose if I cut the creek bottom, and whether $185,000 is a
reasonable number. I'd also like to replant quickly and get cost-share if
it's available. Can you put a plan together, and can you just sign off on
the volume so I can take it to the buyer?
