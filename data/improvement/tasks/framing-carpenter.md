# Task for: framing-carpenter

Single-story 1974 ranch, stick-framed gable roof with 2x6 rafters and 2x6
ceiling joists at 16 in. on center, ground snow load around 40 psf. The
owners want 14 ft of the interior wall between the kitchen and living room
removed, with a flush beam so the ceiling stays flat. From the attic, the
ceiling joists lap over that wall and are nailed together there, and
there's a short knee wall on the living-room side that looks like it props
the rafters.

They also want a 6 ft sliding door cut into the rear exterior wall, which
is sheathed with plywood only near the corners. And the HVAC contractor
wants to notch the top plates of the kitchen wall for a new supply duct.

The contractor starts in three weeks and asked me to size the beam from a
span table so he can order it Monday. Can you work out what this wall is
actually carrying, what the beam and supports look like, how to shore
it, and what the slider and duct changes do to the framing?
