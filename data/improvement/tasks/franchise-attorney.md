# Task for: franchise-attorney

We're a fast-casual franchisor with 38 units in nine states. Our fiscal year
ends December 31, and we issued this year's FDD on April 28. We want to
start selling in California, New York, Illinois, and Florida by January. Our
sales team has been using a pitch deck that says "top units average $1.8M in
sales"; our Item 19 only discloses median figures for company-owned stores.
In August we raised the initial franchise fee from $35,000 to $45,000, and
the FDD hasn't been changed. We also want to terminate a Wisconsin
franchisee who is two months behind on royalties; our agreement allows
termination on 10 days' notice. And we want to require a $45,000 remodel of
every unit next year. The agreement lets us require "reasonable" updates no
more than once every five years, and about a third of the units remodeled
three years ago. One franchisee emailed asking if you could tell him whether
the remodel requirement is enforceable. What do we need to do, and what is
the timeline to launch in January?
