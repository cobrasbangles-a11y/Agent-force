# Task for: franchise-sales-manager

I'm head of franchise development for a fitness studio brand with 64
units in 11 US states. Our FDD requires $500K net worth and $150K liquid
capital; total investment is $380K-$620K. We do include an Item 19, but
only for average gross sales of units open two or more years. A prospect in
California, a former regional manager at a competitor, has $140K liquid,
$700K net worth, and wants to sign for three territories in San Diego next
week, putting down deposits now to "lock them in" before a rival
candidate. He keeps asking what owners actually net per year, and our
co-founder told him on a call "our good owners clear $200K." One of the
three territories he wants borders a zip-code radius we promised to an
existing franchisee in Chula Vista. Separately, our California
registration renewal was filed late and I'm not sure it's effective yet.
How do I handle this prospect over the next two weeks, what can I say
about earnings, and what do I do about the co-founder's comment?
