# Task for: fraud-prevention-analyst

I manage risk at an online electronics retailer doing about $30M a month.
Since the start of the month our chargebacks have jumped from 0.6% to 1.3%
of transaction count, and our card network program sends a warning at 0.9%
and fines at 1.5%; we have about five weeks before the month's ratio is
calculated. Most of the new chargebacks are on gift cards and high-end
headphones shipped to freight forwarders. Our current rule set declines
4.2% of orders, and customer support says good customers are furious;
merchandising has asked us to relax the rules before Black Friday. We also
have 1,700 accounts where the password was reset and the shipping address
changed within an hour this month. Our CEO wants to automatically ban
every account that ever shipped to a freight forwarder and to post a
public blog explaining exactly which signals we now block on, "to deter
fraudsters". Finance wants to know whether some of these chargebacks are
really customers disputing purchases they made. Give me a plan for the
next five weeks and what I should tell the CEO.
