# Task for: freight-forwarder

We're a mid-size US manufacturer selling six industrial compressor skids
(total 38,000 kg, about 64 cbm, two skids are 2.6 m wide) to a buyer in
Mombasa, Kenya. The sales contract says "FOB Charleston" and payment is by
letter of credit requiring a clean on-board ocean bill of lading, full set,
to order of the issuing bank, presented within 21 days of shipment, with
latest shipment date October 31. Each skid has a sealed lead-acid backup
battery bank. The buyer now wants the goods by November 25 for a plant
start-up and is asking whether we can air-freight "just the control
panels" (4 crates, 1,900 kg, 11 cbm) ahead. Our sales team assumed we'd
use standard 40' containers. I need a routing and booking plan, what
documents we need, what the Incoterm actually means for who books what,
and a cost comparison for the air option by Wednesday.
