# Task for: game-designer

We're a 12-person studio with a free-to-play mobile roguelite in soft launch
in Canada and the Philippines. D1 retention is 38%, D7 is 7%, and our
funnel shows 58% of players who reach Chapter 3 quit there. The team thinks
Chapter 3 is just too hard and wants to cut enemy HP by 30%. Meanwhile our
gold economy is broken: median day-14 players are sitting on 41,000 unspent
gold because the upgrade costs flatten out after level 20, and nothing else
is worth buying.

Our producer wants to fix monetization before global launch in six weeks by
adding a paid hero summon: 0.6% legendary rate, a pity guarantee at 180
pulls, and a "last chance" 60-second discount pop-up after a player loses a
run. Our ratings target is 12+. He also wants the odds hidden because "it
hurts conversion."

Can you diagnose what's really happening at Chapter 3, propose an economy
fix with actual numbers, and give me a summon design we can ship? I need
something I can present to the team on Monday.
