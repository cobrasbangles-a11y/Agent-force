# Task for: garde-manger

I run garde manger at a 200-cover restaurant. The chef wants to launch a
house charcuterie board in six weeks: duck prosciutto, coppa, a fermented
salami, and quick pickles plus a lacto-fermented hot sauce. We don't have a
curing chamber; I was going to hang everything in a corner of the walk-in
and vacuum-seal the finished pieces for slicing. I've been measuring pink
salt by the teaspoon from a recipe online. On Fridays I also do about 220
covers of cold apps: a composed beet salad, a burrata plate, and a tuna
crudo. I've been pre-dressing salads at 4:30 for a 5:30 service to keep up,
and they're soggy by 7. The burrata sits on the station in a hotel pan
through service. Can you give me a plan to get the charcuterie program
running safely in six weeks, and a better Friday prep and plating system for
the cold apps?
