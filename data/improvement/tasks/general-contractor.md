# Task for: general-contractor

We're a mid-size GC and just got awarded a $14.6M design-bid-build fire
station with a municipal owner. The prime contract has a broad-form
indemnity clause, a no-damages-for-delay clause, pay-if-paid language,
10% retainage reduced to 5% at 50% complete, and $2,500/day liquidated
damages. Our bonding line is $40M single / $60M aggregate and we're already
carrying $48M of backlog. We're about to award subs: the low electrical bid
($1.9M) is 22% below the next two, from a firm that's never done a public
job and has a $2M bond with a surety I don't recognize; the low roofer
excluded the warranty the spec requires. Our standard subcontract template
has pay-when-paid, 5% retainage, and no delay-damages language. Six weeks
into sitework the excavation sub hit an abandoned fuel tank not shown on
the drawings or in the geotech. The city wants us to "just deal with it"
and keep going. How do I set up the subcontracts, handle bonding, pick the
subs, and handle the tank?
