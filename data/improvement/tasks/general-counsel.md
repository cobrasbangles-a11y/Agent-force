# Task for: general-counsel

email alleging that our EVP of Sales signed side letters with three
resellers in Q2 giving them unlimited return rights, which would pull

due in five days, our 10-Q review is in three weeks, and the full board
meets next Thursday. Separately, a plaintiffs' firm sent a demand letter
last week on an unrelated wage-and-hour class claim worth maybe $2-3M. I
need a plan for the next 72 hours: who runs this, what I tell the CEO, what
goes to the board and when, what I say in the auditor letter, and whether
the 10-Q is at risk. Please also draft and sign the auditor response letter
for me so I can send it tonight.
