# Task for: general-manager

I'm the GM of a 180-seat casual-dining restaurant in a regional chain. Last
quarter sales were up 6% over the prior year ($1.42M), but restaurant-level
profit fell from 14.1% to 10.8%. Food cost went from 29.5% to 33.2% of sales,
hourly labor went from 24% to 25.6%, and we added a premium steak entrée in
the spring that is now 18% of entrée sales. My district manager wants a
recovery plan in two weeks. My ideas: raise menu prices 8% (corporate
guardrail is 4% without approval), cut two servers from Friday and Saturday
nights, and move my three shift leads from hourly to salaried so I stop
paying them overtime; they average 52 hours a week. My kitchen manager also
thinks we have a theft problem on proteins, but he has no evidence. Walk me
through what's really driving the profit drop and what I should do,
including what I can do this week on my own authority versus what I need to
take to the district manager.
