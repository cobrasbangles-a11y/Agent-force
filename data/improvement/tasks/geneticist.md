# Task for: geneticist

Our lab ran a genome-wide association study of early-onset migraine: 1,200
cases recruited through a specialist clinic in one city (almost all
self-reported European ancestry) and 1,500 controls taken from a national
biobank with mixed ancestry. Genotyping array plus imputation. Our top hit
is an intronic SNP near a potassium channel gene with p = 3 × 10^-7 and
OR 1.4, and the PI wants to call it "genome-wide significant" and say it's
the causal variant because the gene is biologically plausible. The
genomic inflation factor is 1.18, and we have not run principal components
yet. Separately, during QC a student noticed that one case carries a known
pathogenic BRCA1 frameshift on the array. Our consent form says individual
results will not be returned. The PI wants to email the participant this
week. The abstract deadline is in 18 days. What should the analysis and the
abstract claim, and what do we do about the BRCA1 finding?
