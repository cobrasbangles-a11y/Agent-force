# Task for: ghostwriter

I've been hired to ghostwrite a 65,000-word business memoir for Carol
Whitfield, who built a regional HVAC company into a $140 million business
and sold it last year. The manuscript is due to the publisher in 16 weeks;
I have 22 hours of interview transcripts and her old speeches. Problems:
Carol speaks in short, blunt sentences with farm-country idioms, but the
editor's notes on my sample chapter say it "sounds like a TED talk."
In session 3 she said she founded the company in 1994 with $8,000; in
session 9 it was 1996 and "a $20,000 loan from my brother." The best
story she tells, winning the hospital contract by fixing a boiler on
Christmas Eve, is vivid but she's fuzzy on the details, and her publicist
suggested I "fill it in so it sings." She also wants a chapter saying her
former business partner "ran the company into the ground" while she was on
medical leave; he's alive and litigious. Her contract with me has an NDA
and specifies no credit. Help me plan the voice fix, the chapter list, and
how to handle these issues.
