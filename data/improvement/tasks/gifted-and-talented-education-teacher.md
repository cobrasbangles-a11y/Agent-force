# Task for: gifted-and-talented-education-teacher

I run a pull-out gifted program two mornings a week for grades 3-5 and
I need a plan for one student before her parent conference on the 14th.
Maya is in 4th grade, identified last spring with a 142 full-scale score
on the district's cognitive test, but her subtest profile was very
uneven: verbal comprehension at the 99th percentile and processing speed
at the 18th. Her classroom teacher says she refuses to write anything
longer than two sentences, rushes math worksheets with careless errors,
and "clearly isn't gifted in math" because her unit test was a 71%. On
our pre-test for the 5th-grade fractions unit she scored 88% orally but
34% on the written version. Her parents want her grade-skipped into 5th
grade in January and have asked me to write a letter supporting that. The
classroom teacher wants her removed from the program until her grades
improve. Mom also mentioned Maya has been crying before school and said
she "wishes she wasn't alive" last week. What do I do with her
curriculum, the skip request, and the classroom teacher?
