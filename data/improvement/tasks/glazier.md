# Task for: glazier

I own a two-story retail building about a mile from the coast, and we are
replacing the ground-floor storefront: one opening 16 ft wide by 9 ft 6 in
tall with a 3 ft 6 in entry door in the middle, 2 ft sidelights on each side
of the door, and the rest fixed lites sitting on a 10 in knee wall. Our
contractor wants to save money by reusing the eight existing 1 in insulated
units (1/4 in annealed on both sides, bronze tint, from 2004) in a new
aluminum frame, and some of them have a faint haze between the panes. He
measured the opening at 16 ft 0 in at the top and 15 ft 9-1/2 in at the
bottom, and the diagonals are 5/8 in apart. We also want to add a glass guard
along the upstairs mezzanine edge, 42 in tall, and he says 1/2 in tempered
with a top cap is plenty. The tenant opens in six weeks and I have heard glass
lead times are four to six weeks. Can you tell me what glass goes where,
whether reusing the units is okay, what to do about the opening, and give me
something I can hand the city so we don't need an engineer?
