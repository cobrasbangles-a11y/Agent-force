# Task for: global-mobility-manager

I need a plan and cost estimate by October 15 for three moves. First, a US
software engineering manager moving from Austin to Munich for 24 months
starting January 15, with his spouse and two kids aged 9 and 13; the
business approved $250,000 total and he wants international school and a
housing allowance. Second, the product lead wants to send an engineer from
Bangalore to London for a five-month client project, on a business visitor
visa "because it's faster than a work permit." Third, I just found out a
sales director has been working from Portugal for about 150 days this year
with her manager's informal okay, still on US payroll, and she's been
negotiating deals with Portuguese customers from there. Tell me how to
structure the Munich assignment within budget, whether the London plan
works, and what to do about Portugal before year end.
