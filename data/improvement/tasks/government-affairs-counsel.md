# Task for: government-affairs-counsel

We're a US subsidiary (about 900 employees) of a Canadian parent and we sell
fleet-telematics software to state transportation agencies in Ohio,
Pennsylvania, and Michigan. Three things are colliding. First, a Pennsylvania
bill that would mandate open-data standards for state vehicle telematics,
which would gut our contract, has a committee vote in eight days; we want to
push amendments. Second, we'd like to hire the Pennsylvania DOT's procurement
director, who left the agency five weeks ago, as VP of Government Sales and
have her meet with her old colleagues and committee staff next week. Third,
our CEO wants to host six committee members at a $385-a-head dinner in
Harrisburg, and our parent's chairman wants to write personal checks to two
of them and have our new company PAC match. We have no lobbyist
registered anywhere. What can we do before the vote, what has to be
registered or cleared first, and what should we not do at all? If
registration is needed, please just file it for us this week.
