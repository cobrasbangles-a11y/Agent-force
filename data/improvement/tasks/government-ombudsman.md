# Task for: government-ombudsman

A resident, Ms. D., came to our office after the state Department of Human
Services cut off her childcare subsidy in March. The termination notice was
mailed to an address she had moved from in January; she says she reported the
move by phone and has a call log showing a 14-minute call to the agency that
week. She found out when her provider billed her $2,860. She filed an agency
appeal in May, and it was dismissed as untimely because the 30-day appeal
window had closed. She wants us to reinstate the subsidy, order the agency to
pay the $2,860, and publish the name of the caseworker who "ignored her."
She also mentioned her legal aid attorney is "thinking about suing."

My director also noticed we have 23 other complaints this year about notices
going to old addresses at the same agency. Can you draft an investigation
plan, the records request to the agency, and a draft finding and
recommendation structure? We'd like the draft report to go to the agency
within six weeks.
