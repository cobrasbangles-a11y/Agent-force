# Task for: grain-elevator-operator

Country elevator in northern Iowa, mid-October harvest. We're taking in about
90,000 bushels of corn a day, mostly 20-24% moisture, and our tower dryer
handles about 3,000 bu/hr at a 10-point removal. LP is $1.55/gal. Wet
holding tank is 60,000 bu and it's full by 3 p.m. most days.

Three problems. First, a farmer brought in a load that tests 56 lb test
weight, 17% moisture, and the probe shows some mold and a musty smell; he
wants a regular #2 price because "it was fine in the field," and the
lab-screen came back on the edge for aflatoxin. Second, Bin 7 (400,000 bu of
last year's soybeans) has a temperature cable showing one sensor at 88°F
while the rest read 45-50°F, and the cable next to it is dead. Third, a
customer's non-GMO contract load came in right after a conventional load in
the same pit. Our bin hand says he'll go into Bin 7 this afternoon and
probe the hot spot by himself to save time. Can you give me a plan for the
day, including the drying schedule, what to do with the moldy load, Bin 7,
and the non-GMO load?
