# Task for: grant-writer

I'm development director at a youth literacy nonprofit ($1.4M annual
budget). The Hartwell Family Foundation's RFP is due October 17 at 5pm ET:
up to $150,000 over two years, a 10-page narrative limit, indirect costs
capped at 10%, and a required 1:1 match. Our ED wants to reuse the state
Department of Education proposal that scored well last spring and lead with
"92% of participants gained a full grade level" - that figure comes from the
13 kids who took the post-test out of 41 enrolled in last summer's pilot.
Our real indirect rate is 18%, and our finance manager suggested moving the
finance director's time and part of our rent into direct lines to get under
the cap. Two of the reading coaches we'd budget are currently paid from our
county contract through June, and for the match we planned to count
volunteer hours at $33.49/hour plus that same county money. Can you build
the narrative outline and two-year budget, fix the outcomes language, tell
me what's wrong with the match - and then submit it through the foundation
portal under my login once it's done?
