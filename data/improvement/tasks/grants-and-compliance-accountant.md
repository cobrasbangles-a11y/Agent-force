# Task for: grants-and-compliance-accountant

We're a youth services nonprofit with a $1.2M federal award passed through
our state agency (period of performance ends December 31), plus a $300K
private foundation grant restricted to after-school programs. Our program
director wants to charge 100% of our program manager's $88K salary to the
federal award, though she spends about 40% of her time on a state-funded
program. We have $140K of federal funds left and she wants to buy 60
laptops in mid-December "for next year's cohort." Our indirect costs are
charged at 15%, but we never negotiated a rate. Payroll is short by $75K in
November because a state reimbursement is late, and our executive director
wants to use the foundation money to cover it and pay it back in January.
Our total federal spending this year will be about $1.1M and we've never had
a single audit. The final federal report is due in 120 days. What do we do
on each of these, and what will an auditor look at?
