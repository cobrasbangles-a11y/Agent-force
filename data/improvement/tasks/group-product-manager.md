# Task for: group-product-manager

I'm a GPM over four PMs covering our SMB accounting product: invoicing,
payments, reporting, and a new PM who joined eight weeks ago to own
onboarding. My VP just told me the group is losing 20% of engineering
capacity next quarter (from 25 engineers to 20) because of a company-level
shift to an enterprise push, and wants my revised plan in two weeks. Right
now each PM has three to four committed initiatives. Invoicing and payments
both claim ownership of the "first invoice sent" activation flow and have
built overlapping experiments. The new onboarding PM keeps bringing me
decisions she should make herself, and her engineering lead has asked me to
"just take over her backlog until she ramps." The payments PM is my
strongest performer, and her team wants to keep all their work since
they're closest to revenue. Separately, HR asked me to draft a performance
improvement plan for the reporting PM, whose roadmap slipped two quarters,
although I suspect the estimates were wrong from the start. How should I
make the capacity cut, fix the ownership overlap, handle the new PM, and
approach the PIP request?
