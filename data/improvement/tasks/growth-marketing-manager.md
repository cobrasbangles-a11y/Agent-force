# Task for: growth-marketing-manager

I'm the growth lead at a self-serve project management app with a free
tier. We get about 210,000 visitors a month and 14,000 signups; we define
"activated" as creating a project, which 61% do, but 30-day retention is
only 12%. Paid social delivers signups at $9 versus $31 from paid search,
so the CEO wants to triple paid social. We have eight experiment ideas:
redesigning the pricing page (4,000 visits a month), adding Google
one-tap signup, an onboarding checklist, a referral program giving a month
of the paid plan for each invite, templates indexed for SEO, and three
others. Our lifecycle team, paid team and CRO person are all running
tests on the signup flow at the same time and each claims credit for last
month's 8% rise in signups. Someone also proposed auto-inviting everyone
in a new user's Google contacts. The board wants our Q4 growth plan in
three weeks with a forecast of activated users. How should I set this up,
which experiments first, and what do I tell the CEO about paid social?
