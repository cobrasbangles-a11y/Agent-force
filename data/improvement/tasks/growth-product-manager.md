owning week-one activation, defined as creating and sharing one file. We
launched a new onboarding checklist test five days ago at a 50/50 split.

p = 0.04, and my CEO wants it shipped to 100% today. But the test was
sized for 14 days, the variant arm has 6% more users than control, and
three other experiments are running on the signup and editor flows at the
same time. The design team also wants to test a pre-checked "Start my
annual Pro trial" box on the upgrade screen that converts to a paid annual
plan after 14 days, and marketing wants to count "shared a file" even when
users share to themselves. I also have no long-term holdout, so nobody can
tell me whether the six "winning" tests we shipped this year actually
added up. What should I do with the current test, the two new proposals,
and our experimentation setup over the next quarter?
