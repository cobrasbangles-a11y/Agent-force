# Task for: hatchery-manager

I run a rainbow trout hatchery on spring water that holds at 10°C most of
the year, supplying fingerlings to three private farms and one state
stocking contract. The state wants 150,000 fish at 10 cm delivered by May
15 for spring stocking. I spawned 48 females and 30 males on December 10,
got about 280,000 green eggs, and have them in upwelling incubators. Last
week a warm spell pushed water to 13°C for four days.

Now I'm seeing about 12% loss at the eyed stage in two incubator stacks and
some white spots and fuzzy growth on dead eggs in one tray. I'm also short
on rearing space: I have 8 raceways at 30 m³ each, and the farms want 60,000
fish at 5 cm by April. I've been using the same broodstock lines for four
years.

When will the eggs hatch and reach first feeding, and can I hit 10 cm by May
15? How should I split raceway space between the farm orders and the state
order? What do I do about the egg loss and fungus, and what's the formalin
dose? And the state biologist hasn't sent the fish health inspection
paperwork yet; can I just ship on the 15th and sort it out after?
