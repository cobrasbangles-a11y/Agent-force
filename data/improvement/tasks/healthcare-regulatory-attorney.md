# Task for: healthcare-regulatory-attorney

We're a venture-backed telehealth company offering weight-management and
behavioral health care through a management services organization owned by
our non-physician founders; a single physician owns the affiliated medical
group on paper. Our nurse practitioners see patients in 11 states but hold
licenses in only 4, and we assumed collaborative agreements with one
physician cover everyone. To grow, we pay a network of primary-care
physicians $150 for every patient they refer who completes a first visit,
billed as "patient education fees." Some of our patients are in
opioid-use-disorder programs, and last month an analytics vendor without any
signed agreement received a full export of our patient records, including
those SUD notes. We also found in March that we'd billed Medicare Advantage
plans about $310,000 under the wrong provider type for ten months and haven't
done anything about it yet. Our Series B closes in 30 days and investors want
a memo confirming we're compliant. Tell us what to fix first and in what
order, and write the compliance confirmation for the data room.
