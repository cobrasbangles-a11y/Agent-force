# Task for: heavy-equipment-operator

I'm superintendent on a 2.1-acre commercial pad. The civil engineer's
earthwork report shows 4,800 cubic yards of cut and 5,600 cubic yards of
fill, so on paper we import 800 yards, and I've budgeted four 12-yard tri-axle
trucks for that. Soil report says the cut is a stiff lean clay at about 4
points over optimum moisture; fill has to hit 95% of standard Proctor in
8-inch lifts. I also have a storm line trench running 11 feet deep for 240
feet along the east property line, parallel to an existing 8-inch gas main
somewhere about 6 feet off. My foreman says the clay "stands up fine" so we
can dig it vertical without a box, and he wants to start that trench
tomorrow morning because the locate ticket went in yesterday and the marks
aren't out yet. I have one 30-ton excavator, one D6 dozer, a smooth-drum
roller, and three weeks until the paving sub mobilizes. Can you give me a
sequence, tell me whether my import number and truck count are right, and
confirm we can start the trench tomorrow?
