# Task for: herd-manager

220-cow spring-calving commercial beef herd in western Kansas. Preg-check
was last week; here's what came back. 31 cows open. Of the 189 bred, 22 are
late (projected to calve in the last 30 days of the 75-day season). Average
herd BCS is 5.2, but 18 of the first-calf heifers scored 4 or below, and 7
older cows dropped from 6 to 4 since weaning. Tag 318 was treated for foot
rot 12 days ago with a product with a 28-day meat withdrawal. Tag 142 has
had two calves weaned at the bottom 10% of the herd and has bad feet. Our
records show 234 cows on the inventory sheet, but the chute count says 220.

The owner wants to cull all 31 opens plus anything under BCS 4 on Monday's
sale because cull cow prices are strong. Pasture is short, and we're buying
hay at $180/ton. I need a cull list, a feeding plan for the thin cows and
heifers going into winter, and to know what to do with 318 and 142. And
what's wrong with the seven older cows that dropped condition; can you tell
me what to treat them with?
