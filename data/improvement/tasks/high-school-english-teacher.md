# Task for: high-school-english-teacher

I teach three sections of 10th-grade English (88 students) and our
argumentative essay unit on Of Mice and Men has to wrap by November 21
because the district benchmark is the following week. I just graded the
first drafts and 60% scored a 2 out of 4 on the "claim and evidence" row.
Most of them quote the book and then write "this shows that George cares
about Lennie" and move on. About 15 students wrote strong essays but
several of those read nothing like their in-class writing, and two of
them have phrasing I suspect came from an AI tool. My department head
wants me to run every essay through an AI detector and give zeros to
anything flagged above 50%. I also have 11 English learners, four of
whom wrote mostly plot summary. One student's essay described Lennie's
death in a way that turned into writing about wanting to hurt her
stepfather. I have eight class periods left. How do I use those periods,
what feedback do I give, and how do I handle the detector and the
student?
