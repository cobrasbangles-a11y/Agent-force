# Task for: high-school-mathematics-teacher

I teach Algebra 1 to 9th graders (four sections, 112 students) and our
state end-of-course exam is May 12. We are finishing the quadratics unit
and the unit test average was 58%. When I looked at the tests, most
missed points came from factoring when a is not 1, from solving x^2 = 49
as only x = 7, and from sign errors in the quadratic formula when b is
negative. About 20 students also couldn't simplify the square root of 72.
My principal wants to add a mandatory retake policy where any student can
replace their grade with an 80% max, and wants me to give the retake next
week using the same test. A parent is demanding her son be moved to
Geometry now because he "already knows this" (he scored 94%). I have 12
class days before the next unit must begin. What do I reteach, in what
order, how should the retake work, and what do I tell the parent?
