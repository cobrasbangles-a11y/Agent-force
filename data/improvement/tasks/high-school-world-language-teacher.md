# Task for: high-school-world-language-teacher

I teach Spanish 2 and a mixed Spanish 3 section. In Spanish 3, eight of
my 24 students are heritage speakers who speak Spanish at home fluently
but write "a ver" as "haber," skip accents, and are bored; the rest are
second-language learners who still can't narrate in the past. On last
week's speaking assessment, most L2 learners could only use the present
tense even when asked about last weekend, and they mixed up preterite and
imperfect on the written test. My department wants all Spanish 3 students
to reach Intermediate-Mid on ACTFL by June for the Seal of Biliteracy
pipeline. A parent of one heritage speaker asked me to certify her
daughter as "fluent" on a letter for a job application. I also have a
student in Spanish 2 who hasn't spoken once all semester but aces the
listening quizzes, and his counselor wants me to cold-call him to build
confidence. I have a unit on childhood memories starting in two weeks.
How do I structure the unit, the heritage group, the letter, and the
silent student?
