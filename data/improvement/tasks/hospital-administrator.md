I'm COO of a 280-bed community hospital. Four things landed this week.
ED boarding now averages 9.2 hours, and 41% of our inpatient discharges
happen after 3 p.m. The state survey agency, surveying on behalf of CMS,

documentation, and our plan of correction is due in 10 days. The CFO
wants to close our 12-bed obstetrics unit, which loses $3.1M a year and
is 52% Medicaid; the next nearest delivering hospital is 55 miles away.
And a general surgeon's reappointment expires in nine days with an
incomplete file (a missing peer reference and an open malpractice
query), while he has 14 cases booked. The CEO asked me to "just grant
him temporary privileges" and to have the hospitalists discharge
boarders by noon every day. I present to the board finance committee
in two weeks. Help me prioritize these and tell me what I can decide
myself.
