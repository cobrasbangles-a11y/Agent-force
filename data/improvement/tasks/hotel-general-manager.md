# Task for: hotel-general-manager

I'm GM of a 210-room branded full-service hotel. RevPAR is down 8% against
our comp set year to date while theirs is flat, and guest satisfaction
scores dropped mostly on "room not ready at check-in" and "slow restaurant
breakfast." Housekeeping is down six attendants, and the F&B director says
breakfast is slow because housekeeping keeps pulling his servers to strip
rooms. The owner wants to defer $1.4M of brand PIP work (corridor carpet and
bathroom refresh) that's due by March because cash is tight. One of our
two guest elevators has been out for nine days waiting on a part. Last
night a guest slipped on the pool deck, went to the ER, and her husband is
emailing about lawyers; my front desk manager wants to email back an apology
and offer to cover the hospital bill. I have an owner call in five days. Can
you help me figure out root causes, what to tell the owner about the PIP,
and how to handle the pool incident and the elevator?
