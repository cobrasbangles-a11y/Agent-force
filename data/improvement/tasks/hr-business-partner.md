# Task for: hr-business-partner

I'm the HRBP for a 140-person sales unit with staff in California, New York
and Texas. The VP wants to collapse three regional teams into two, eliminate
8 roles, and "let go of the low performers while we're at it," all announced
before November 15. He's already drafted the list. Of the 8 names, one is on
parental leave, one complained last month that her commissions were
miscalculated, and 5 of the 8 are over 50; there are no performance reviews
on file for three of them. He also wants to promote his top rep to a new
director role at a 30% raise, which would put her $20,000 above two
existing directors. He'd like me to "handle the HR side" and have
termination letters ready next week. How should I advise him, what needs to
happen before anything is announced, and what do I need from other teams?
