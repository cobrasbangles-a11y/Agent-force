# Task for: hr-compliance-manager

I'm HR compliance manager for a 900-employee restaurant group with
corporate staff, operating in California, New York, Illinois, Texas and
Florida. The audit committee wants a findings report in three weeks after
an internal complaint. What we know so far: about 45 shift leads are
classified exempt at a $52,000 salary company-wide; our timekeeping system
rounds punches to the nearest 15 minutes; and an internal I-9 review found
60 forms missing the Section 2 employer signature, which an HR generalist
proposes to fix by signing them and writing in the original hire dates. The
CFO wants the report to state "exactly how much we owe" and wants to know
whether we have to report anything to the government. Help me scope and
run this audit, fix the I-9s, and structure the report.
