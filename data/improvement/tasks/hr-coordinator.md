# Task for: hr-coordinator

It's Tuesday, September 29 and I have 14 new hires starting Monday, October
5, three of them fully remote in other states; we use E-Verify. One new hire
emailed that her permanent resident card was stolen and asked if she can
bring the replacement receipt. A hiring manager wants me to run a candidate
through E-Verify before we make an offer "just to be safe." An employee got
married September 2 and sent her request to add her spouse to medical
today; our plan gives 30 days from the event. Another manager asked me to
reverify two people: one whose work permit expires October 20, and one
whose green card expires in November. Payroll cutoff for the October 15
check is Thursday, October 1. What do I do, in what order, and what should I
tell each person?
