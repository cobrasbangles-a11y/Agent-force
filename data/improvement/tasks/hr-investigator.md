# Task for: hr-investigator

Our general counsel has asked me to run an investigation. A senior sales
engineer (female, 11 years with us) filed a written complaint on September
22 alleging that her director, over the last eight months, made repeated
comments about her appearance at client dinners, sent her late-night
personal messages on his personal phone, and cut her out of two deals after
she stopped replying. She says a colleague saw one incident in Denver in
May. The director's boss, our VP of Sales, wants to sit in on the
interviews "to keep things fair," and is pushing to finish in five business
days because the director is up for a regional promotion on October 10.
The complainant has asked that the director not be told her name. HR
already moved her onto a different reporting line, which cut her
commission accounts from 14 to 9. Can you set up the investigation plan,
tell me what's wrong with the current setup, and draft the interview
outline for the director? GC wants the plan by Thursday.
