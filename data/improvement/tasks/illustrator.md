# Task for: illustrator

We're a personal-finance app and need 30 illustrations in six weeks: 12
onboarding screens, 10 empty states, and 8 marketing hero images. Budget is
$18,000 total. Our CEO loves the style of one specific living illustrator on
Instagram and wants ours to look "basically like hers" but we can't afford
to hire her. The characters need to show a diverse range of users,
including older adults and a wheelchair user, without looking like a stock
diversity checklist.

The app has a dark mode, so everything has to work on both a white and a
near-black background. Our motion team wants to animate the onboarding set
in Lottie later, so they've asked for "clean SVGs." To save money, our
marketing lead suggested you do the first 10 by hand and we generate the
other 20 with an AI image tool trained on those, and we want full exclusive
ownership of everything.

Can you propose how to scope and schedule this, define the style so it's
ours and not a copy, and tell me what's realistic about the AI plan and the
ownership question?
