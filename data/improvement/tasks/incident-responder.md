# Task for: incident-responder

I'm the IT director at a 700-person regional manufacturer, and I'm running
our response. At 5am we found ransomware notes on 60 servers, including
the ERP and both file servers; our backups are on a NAS that was also
encrypted, but we have 11-day-old offsite tapes. The shipping floor is
down and the COO says every day costs about $400K. Our EDR shows the same
compromised domain-admin account was used on 40 other hosts over the past
two weeks. The COO wants the ERP restored from tape onto the same servers
today and the plant back up by tomorrow morning. The CFO is asking whether
we should just pay the ransom, which demands 30 BTC within 72 hours. Our
cyber insurer's hotline says they'll appoint breach counsel and a forensics
firm within four hours and not to change anything until then. A plant
manager has already started wiping workstations to reimage them. I need a
sequenced plan for the next 72 hours: what to do in the first two hours,
recovery order, what I tell leadership and staff, and how to handle the
ransom question.
