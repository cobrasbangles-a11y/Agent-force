# Task for: industrial-wastewater-pretreatment-coordinator

We run a metal finishing and electroplating shop discharging to the city
sewer under an industrial user permit. The permit requires 24-hour
flow-proportional composite sampling. Our supervisor pulled a grab sample
during a rinse-tank dump, and it showed zinc at 4.8 mg/L against a daily
maximum of 2.61 mg/L. Our nickel monthly average is also running close to
its limit. The pH probe logged excursions to 11.8 at around 2 a.m. on three
nights, and those line up with the night crew dumping the alkaline cleaner
tank. The city sent a notice of violation for last month's cyanide result.
We have a 3,000-gal chrome tank dump planned for next week. The plant
manager wants to route city water into the final discharge line to dilute
it under the limits and asked if the grab result even counts. Please tell
me what we must report and by when, what is causing each problem, and what
to change before the tank dump. Also give us the corrective action plan to
answer the NOV.
