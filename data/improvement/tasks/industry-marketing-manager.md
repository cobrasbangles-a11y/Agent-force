# Task for: industry-marketing-manager

I've just been made industry marketing manager for financial services at a
document automation and e-signature company. Leadership wants a banking
and credit union campaign live in eight weeks with a $150,000 budget and
$5M of pipeline expected in two quarters, although our average bank deal
takes nine to twelve months. Our horizontal messaging is about "saving
time on paperwork." Sales wants the campaign to say we're "SOC 2 and
FFIEC compliant" and "approved by regulators"; we have a SOC 2 Type II
report but nothing else I know of. Our strongest case study is a
healthcare network that cut contract turnaround by 70%; our only banking
customer is a small credit union that saved about 30% on loan document
processing and hasn't agreed to be named. Product mentioned that a core
banking integration everyone asks about is "on the roadmap." I need the
research plan, the vertical messaging, the proof strategy, and realistic
targets I can take back to leadership. Can you also write the landing page
headline and the ad copy?
