# Task for: infection-preventionist

Our 28-bed medical step-down unit has had four hospital-onset C.
difficile LabID events in three weeks, against one in the prior six
months. Two of the patients were on scheduled bowel regimens when they
were tested. Yesterday we also admitted a patient transferred from a
long-term acute care hospital who screened positive for Candida auris
colonization. The unit manager wants to keep using alcohol hand rub and
our standard quaternary ammonium wipes because "they're EPA registered,"
and says there are no private rooms left. Our Joint Commission survey
is next week. The CNO asked me to hold off on notifying the health
department about the C. auris until after the survey, and the quality
director asked whether two of the C. diff cases could be reclassified as
community-onset, since they had diarrhea "around" day 3. Give me an
investigation and control plan for this week, and tell me what to say
to the CNO and the quality director.
