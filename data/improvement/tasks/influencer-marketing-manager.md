# Task for: influencer-marketing-manager

I manage influencer marketing for a skincare brand launching a vitamin C
serum in the US, UK and Germany. Budget is $120,000 for a six-week launch
starting in a month. The founder wants to spend $70,000 of it on one
creator with 2.1M followers whose engagement rate is 0.4% and who posted a
competitor's serum eight weeks ago. Our agency shortlisted 30 micro
creators (20K to 150K followers), several of whom are 16 or 17. Our draft
brief tells creators to say the serum "clears acne and reverses sun damage
in 14 days" and to put #ad at the end of their hashtags. The founder also
wants to run the best-performing creator videos as paid ads for a year,
but the draft contracts only mention organic posts, and he wants
creators paid only if they hit a sales target. We'll track with discount
codes. I need the creator selection approach, what's wrong with the brief
and contracts, the payment structure, and how we measure the launch. Can
you also write the script creators should read word for word?
