# Task for: information-designer

I'm the communications lead at a county health department. Our board meets
Thursday and I need a one-page infographic, in English and Spanish, on
overdose deaths. Our county (population 182,000) had 42 deaths in 2019 and
61 in 2023; the neighboring county (455,000) had 98 and 121. 2024 data is
preliminary and shows 49 so far. My director wants a bar chart with the
axis starting at 40 so the 2024 drop "pops," and a headline saying our
naloxone program caused the decline.

We also want a ZIP-code map of deaths. Several ZIPs have between one and
four deaths, and a couple of rural ZIPs have fewer than 2,000 residents. The
page will be posted on our website, so it has to work for screen reader
users, and the director wants the ZIP colors to go from green to red.

Can you design the layout, choose the charts, and write the headline and
alt text? I also need to know which of the director's requests I should
push back on and how to explain why.
