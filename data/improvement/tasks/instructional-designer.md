# Task for: instructional-designer

Our hospital system needs a new blood-transfusion safety course for about
2,400 nurses, and the chief nursing officer wants it live by March 1 as a
single 45-minute self-paced e-learning module with a 10-question
multiple-choice quiz at the end, 80% to pass. The trigger was two
near-miss events last year where the two-person bedside verification of
patient identity and blood product was skipped under time pressure. The
SME, a transfusion nurse specialist, sent a 90-slide deck organized by
blood component type, with most of the content on immunology and
component storage temperatures. The compliance team says the course
must "meet Joint Commission requirements," and the CNO asked me to
confirm in writing that it will. We also have 300 night-shift nurses who
can't attend daytime sessions and an LMS that only tracks completion,
not quiz item data. Can you design this course, tell me what's wrong
with the current plan, and tell me what I should say to the CNO?
