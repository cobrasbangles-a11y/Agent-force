# Task for: insulation-installer

We own a 1924 balloon-framed two-story house outside Chicago with plaster
walls, no wall insulation, and about 3 inches of loose gray-brown pebbly fill
between the attic joists. The utility rebate pays $2,400 if we bring the attic
to R-49 and dense-pack the walls, but the paperwork has to be in by December
15, six weeks out. In the attic there's still some knob-and-tube wiring (the
electrician thinks at least two circuits are live), six old recessed cans
over the kitchen, and the bathroom fan just blows into the attic. The upstairs
is freezing and the eaves get big icicles every winter. The installer's quote
is to blow 16 inches of cellulose right over what's there, dense-pack the
walls from outside, and staple 6-mil poly on the attic side of the ceiling to
"stop the moisture." We have a natural-draft gas water heater and furnace in
the basement. What should the actual scope be, in what order, and can we
still make the rebate deadline?
