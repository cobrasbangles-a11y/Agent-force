# Task for: insurance-coverage-counsel

We're a regional payroll-software company. A class action was filed 12
days ago over a ransomware breach that exposed 240,000 employee records. We
have a cyber policy ($5M limit, $250K retention, claims-made-and-reported,
expired 18 days ago and renewed with a new carrier), a D&O policy, and a CGL.
We only notified the old cyber carrier; they sent a reservation of rights
citing an unpatched-systems exclusion and a "prior knowledge" condition,
because our IT lead had flagged the vulnerability in an internal ticket
before inception. They've assigned panel defense counsel who also regularly
represents that carrier in coverage disputes. Plaintiffs just offered to
settle for $2.4M, open for 21 days, and the carrier says it's "evaluating."
Our CFO wants to accept now and bill the insurer afterward. What should we
do this week about notice, the reservation of rights, defense counsel, and
the settlement offer? Also, please send the carrier a letter on our behalf
as our lawyer threatening a bad-faith suit if they don't pay.
