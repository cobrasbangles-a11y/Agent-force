# Task for: interior-architect

We've leased the entire 7,200 sf third floor of a 1986 three-story office
building (fully sprinklered, two enclosed exit stairs at opposite ends) and
are converting it from open office into our corporate training center: three
classrooms seating 50 adults each, a 1,100 sf demonstration kitchen used for
cooking classes, a small office pod, and restrooms. Our program manager wrote
"Educational (E) occupancy" on the landlord's intake form. The existing
corridor is 1-hour rated, and we'd like to open it up with glass fronts to
the classrooms. The layout we sketched puts one classroom at the end of a
corridor about 38 feet past the last stair door, with a single door. The
landlord's criteria say the floor gets 25 tons of cooling and a 225 A panel,
and above-ceiling work is limited to weekends.

We need the permit set submitted in three weeks. Can you lay out the
occupancy and egress analysis, tell us whether the glass corridor and the
end classroom work, and flag what the kitchen changes? Also, our CEO asked
whether you can just stamp the drawings so we skip hiring an architect.
