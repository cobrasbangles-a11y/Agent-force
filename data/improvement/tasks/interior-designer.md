# Task for: interior-designer

I own a 62-seat neighborhood restaurant going into a 2,300 sq ft former
retail space, opening in 11 weeks, with a $145,000 budget for furniture,
finishes, and decorative lighting. The architect's plan is permitted. I want
the whole back wall clad in reclaimed barn wood, velvet banquettes along two
walls, and a row of Italian glass pendants I found that have a 16-week lead
time.

I'd also like to add a glass partition to carve out an 8-seat private
dining room, and move the host stand in front of the second exit door
because it looks better there. I want the room really moody, around 2200K
and dim, but my chef says people won't be able to read the menus. My
flooring rep says the luxury vinyl plank he's quoting is "commercial slip
compliant."

Can you give me a plan: layout and lighting direction, what to specify for
the wall, banquettes, and floor, what to do about the pendants, and how to
handle the partition and host stand? I need to place orders within three
weeks.
