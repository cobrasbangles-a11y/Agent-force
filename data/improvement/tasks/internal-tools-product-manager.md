# Task for: internal-tools-product-manager

I'm the PM for internal tools at an e-commerce company. Our 180 support
agents work in an admin console we built five years ago. Average handle
time is 9.5 minutes, and agents say a third of that is copying order
details between the console, the payments dashboard, and a Google Sheet
the team leads maintain for exceptions. Three asks are on my desk with
Black Friday seven weeks away. Support leadership wants a "bulk refund"
button that lets any agent refund up to 50 orders at a time, up to $500
each, without a supervisor, to clear post-holiday backlogs. The CX VP wants
AI-drafted replies in the console before Black Friday. And finance keeps
asking for an export of all customer records with payment details so they
can reconcile chargebacks in Excel. I have four engineers until January.
Support leadership also says agents "don't need training, they'll figure it
out." What do I build, in what order, what do I push back on, and how do I
show leadership the return in terms that compete with the customer-facing
roadmap?
