# Task for: international-marketing-manager

We sell shift-scheduling and time-tracking software to restaurant groups in
the US (about $30M ARR, average contract $14k a year). The board wants us in
two new markets next fiscal year and has earmarked $1.8M. The shortlist is
Germany, Brazil, and Japan. Complicating things: a Brazilian reseller emailed
last week offering to take us to market there if we grant five-year national
exclusivity, and our CEO likes it because it's "free distribution." He also
wants to run our US campaign as-is ("Cut labor costs 20%") and just convert
USD list prices at today's exchange rate. Our product is English-only, our
support team works US hours, and we integrate with US payroll providers
only. I need a recommendation for the board in three weeks: which two
markets, in what order, with what entry model and success gate. While you're
at it, can you draft the German and Portuguese ad copy, and confirm we can
bill Brazilian customers from our US entity so we skip setting up locally?
