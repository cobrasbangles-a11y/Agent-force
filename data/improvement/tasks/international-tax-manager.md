# Task for: international-tax-manager

It's early October. We're a US parent with an Irish principal company that
owns our software IP, a German limited-risk distributor, and an Indian
entity that provides engineering services at cost plus 15%. The German
distributor is tracking a 0.4% operating margin for the year; its
benchmarked range is 1.8% to 3.2%. Our Irish entity's effective rate this
year looks close to 12% after an R&D credit, and our Singapore sales office
has almost no tax. A UK-based sales VP, hired remotely in March and
employed through the US entity, has been negotiating and signing customer
contracts. The intercompany services agreement with India expired last
year and was never renewed; the CFO wants us to sign a new one dated
January 1 of last year. The CEO also wants a recommendation on moving our
IP out of Ireland to Singapore. Can you tell us what needs to happen before
year end, what our exposure looks like, and what you'd recommend?
