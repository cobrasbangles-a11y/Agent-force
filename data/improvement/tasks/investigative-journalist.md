# Task for: investigative-journalist

I'm six weeks into an investigation of a regional hospital system that
appears to be suing low-income patients over medical debt while qualifying
them for charity care on paper. The county court portal gave me 1,840
collection suits filed since 2022; a billing employee then sent me an
internal spreadsheet listing 612 of those patients by name with their
charity-care eligibility flag. She wants full anonymity - the file's
metadata shows her network username, and I believe only four people could
have exported it. The hospital denied my public-records request for its
charity-care policy (it's a nonprofit that leases a county-owned building),
and its outside counsel emailed demanding to review our draft before
publication "to prevent defamation." My editor wants to run Sunday. I sent
the hospital a request for comment Wednesday at 5pm asking for answers by
Thursday noon. Can you tell me what the data can and can't prove, how to
handle the spreadsheet and my source, what to do about the records denial
and the lawyer, and whether Sunday is realistic? Also, my source asked me
whether whistleblower law protects her if she's fired - what should I tell
her?
