inch on Saturday. The canal district is on a 7-day rotation, and our turn
is Tuesday. Can you build me a schedule for the next 14 days, tell me
which fields get priority, and whether I'll run out of allocation before
corn reaches dent? Also, the owner asked if we can run the wells a bit over
the allocation this year and "make it up next year." And Pivot 6 keeps
