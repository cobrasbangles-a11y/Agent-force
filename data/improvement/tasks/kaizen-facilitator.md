# Task for: kaizen-facilitator

We want to run a five-day kaizen event starting in three weeks on
changeover of our 400-ton stamping press. Changeover currently averages 94
minutes and the plant manager wants it under 30 so we can run smaller
batches. He has already told the team leads the answer is "quick-change
dies and pre-staged tooling," and he'd like the event to confirm that and
wrap up by Wednesday so the team can get back to production. Maintenance
says they can only spare one technician part time. Two of the operators
have told the team lead that the fastest changeovers happen when people
skip the full lockout and just hit the e-stop while swapping die clamps.
There's also a separate problem: scrap runs about 6% for the first 200
parts after every changeover, and nobody knows why; the plant manager wants
that fixed in the same event. Help me set this event up: scope, team,
preparation, the five-day plan, and how I handle the plant manager.
