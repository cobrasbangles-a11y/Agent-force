# Task for: kindergarten-teacher

It's early November and I have 24 kindergartners; my first-quarter report
cards and parent conferences are due November 20. My fall screener
results: 9 children know fewer than 10 letter sounds, 5 are already
reading CVC words, and 6 are Spanish-dominant English learners, three of
whom score very low in English but whose parents say they know their
letters in Spanish. One boy, Eli, can count to 50 but can't tell me how
many bears are in a pile of 7, can't hold a pencil with more than a fist
grip, and has had three meltdowns this week during transitions that ended
with him hitting classmates. His parents want him held back now. Another
girl, Ava, came in with a bruise on her upper arm and said "Daddy got
mad"; her mom told me it happened at the playground. My principal wants
me to cut center time to add 30 more minutes of whole-group phonics. How
do I set up literacy groups, what goes on report cards, and what do I do
about Eli and Ava?
