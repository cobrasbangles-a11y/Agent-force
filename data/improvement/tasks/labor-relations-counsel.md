# Task for: labor-relations-counsel

We're a unionized food-packaging manufacturer, 420 bargaining-unit employees
at our Ohio plant; the CBA expires in 45 days. At the last session our VP of
Operations told the union "we simply can't afford" their 4% wage ask, and
the union has now requested three years of plant-level financials. We've
also decided to close one of our five lines and subcontract that work,
eliminating about 60 bargaining-unit jobs, and leadership wants to announce
it with the final offer. At our non-union Indiana warehouse, an organizing
drive started last week and a shift supervisor has been asking employees
whether they signed cards, and the site manager wants to announce a $1.50
raise next Monday. If the union doesn't accept our final offer at
expiration, the CEO wants to implement it the next day and hire permanent
replacements if they strike. Map out our risks and a plan for the next 45
days, and can you sit in at the table with us Thursday as our negotiator?
