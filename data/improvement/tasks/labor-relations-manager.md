# Task for: labor-relations-manager

I'm the labor relations manager at a 1,200-employee US auto parts plant;
780 hourly workers are covered by a CBA with the union that expires next
June. Operations wants to move the second shift from 3:00-11:30 p.m. to
2:00-10:30 p.m. starting November 3; the contract sets shift hours "as
established" and is silent on changing them. A supervisor fired a
14-year forklift operator yesterday after a near-miss, without an
interview, and the steward has already filed a grievance; our Step 1
response is due in five working days. The union also sent an information
request for two years of overtime distribution records and discipline for
all forklift operators, due in ten days, and plant management wants to say
no because "they're fishing for bargaining." Finally, our CFO wants a cost
estimate for a 3.5% annual wage increase over a three-year deal for next
year's bargaining. What should I do this week, and what do I tell
operations and the plant manager?
