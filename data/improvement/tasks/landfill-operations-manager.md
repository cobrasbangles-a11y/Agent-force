four loads a day. We're also being offered 300 tons a week of wet wastewater
biosolids at a good tipping fee. I use 6 inches of soil as daily cover. I
need, by Friday: what our real utilization factor means for closure date,
whether switching cover would help and by how much, what to do about the
leachate head, whether to take the biosolids, and a straight answer on the
Cell 7 starter lift.
