# Task for: landscape-architect

We're resubmitting the site plan for a 2-acre medical office building in two
weeks (USDA zone 6b, heavy clay). Three things are stuck. First, the owner
wants to save three 30-inch-diameter white oaks along the north property
line, but the civil plan cuts the grade about 18 inches for parking
starting roughly 12 feet from the trunks. Second, the civil sized a 4,000 sf
bioretention cell near the entrance; the owner thinks it's ugly and too big
and asked us to shrink it to 2,500 sf and plant it with shade trees and
turf. Third, to gain parking the owner wants a 5-foot segmental block wall
along the east side and a 2.5:1 mowed turf slope on the south.

The city requires 10% interior parking-lot landscaping, one street tree per
40 feet of frontage (we have 310 feet), and an irrigation water budget. Can
you tell us what to do about the oaks, whether we can resize the
bioretention, how to handle the wall and slope, and give us a plant palette
and irrigation approach for the resubmittal?
