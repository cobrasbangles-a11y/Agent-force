# Task for: lean-six-sigma-black-belt

I'm the plant quality lead at a snack manufacturer. Our 454 g (16 oz) bags
average 468 g off Line 3, and finance says that giveaway costs us about
$410,000 a year. The plant manager wants to drop the filler target to 456 g
next month. I have six months of checkweigher data (every bag, about 2.1
million records) plus 30 bags a shift weighed on the QC lab scale, and the
two don't agree: the checkweigher reads about 3 g heavier on average. The
histogram of the checkweigher data looks skewed with a long low tail, and
operators adjust the filler whenever they see a light bag. Last week a
state weights-and-measures inspector pulled a lot from a retailer and
flagged it for review, though it passed. Can you tell me whether 456 g is
safe, what capability we really have, what's causing the variation, and
how to set this up as a proper project? I need something for the plant
manager by the end of the month.
