# Task for: leave-of-absence-specialist

I administer leave for a 2,300-employee company with sites in New York,
New Jersey, and Texas. Three cases this week. (1) A New Jersey warehouse
associate with 10 months of service and 1,100 hours worked says she needs
six weeks to care for her husband after surgery. (2) A Texas customer
service rep has intermittent federal family leave certified for migraines
at 1-2 episodes per month lasting up to a day; he has taken seven days off
in the last three weeks, and his manager wants to write him up and asked
me what his diagnosis is. (3) A New York analyst has used her full 12
weeks of federal leave for cancer treatment, which ends October 20; her
oncologist says she needs four more weeks, and her director says the
department "can't hold the job any longer" and wants to post it. Her
short-term disability claim is still open with the carrier. What do I do
on each case, what notices go out when, and what do I tell each manager?
