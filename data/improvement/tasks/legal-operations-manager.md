# Task for: legal-operations-manager

I'm the GC of a 3,000-person logistics company. We're at month 9 with
$6.2M of outside counsel spend against a $7M annual budget, 18% over the
run rate, and one patent case going to trial in February is the main driver.
The trial firm's invoices show block-billed entries, four partners on
routine status calls, and 11% of time on "file review" by first-year
associates. We use 40 firms and I want a proposal to cut to a panel of 8
with better rates by January. The CFO needs Q3 legal accruals and a Q4
forecast by Friday, and our e-billing tool doesn't capture unbilled work in
progress. Our head of sales ops also asked whether we should just settle the
patent case to hit budget, since settlement "looks like" about $3M. Give me
a plan: the accrual approach, what to do about the trial firm's bills before
trial, the panel convergence proposal, and your recommendation on settling
the patent case.
