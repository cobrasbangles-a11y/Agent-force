# Task for: legislative-aide

I'm the legislative aide for a second-term state representative. Her bill
HB 1182, which requires insurers to cover hearing aids for children under 18,
is set for a House Health Committee hearing Thursday at 9 a.m. She also has
an Appropriations subcommittee markup at 9:30 a.m. in another building, where
she's carrying an amendment for a rural clinic. The chair gives sponsors three
minutes. We have 11 cosponsors and need a majority of the 13-member committee;
by my count five are yes, three are no, and five are unknown. Committee
passage deadline for House bills is in nine days.

Our best story is a constituent whose son was denied coverage. She also has
an open dispute with her insurer that she wants the rep to "fix." The
communications director wants to send a mailer about the hearing on official
letterhead to 8,000 households, closing with "keep Rep. Alvarez working for
you in November."

Can you build Thursday morning's schedule plan, the three-minute testimony,
talking points, and a vote-count/tracking plan through the deadline? Tell me
what to do about the constituent and the mailer.
