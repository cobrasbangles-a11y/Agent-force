# Task for: legislative-policy-analyst

My senator sits on the Senate Finance Committee, which hears SB 407 on
Tuesday. The bill raises the state earned income tax credit from 10% to 20%
of the federal credit and makes it refundable, effective for tax year 2027.
The Department of Revenue's fiscal note puts the cost at $142 million a year.
An advocacy group supporting the bill says it will cost $88 million and
"pays for itself" through higher work participation, citing a study of one
city's pilot program. The senator is inclined to vote yes and wants a memo
"making the case" she can hand to two undecided colleagues. A lobbyist for
the state retailers' association told her the refundable piece violates the
state constitution's uniformity clause. We're also at the committee
reporting deadline: if Finance doesn't act Tuesday, the bill is dead for the
session unless it rides on the budget bill.

Can you draft the briefing memo by Monday noon, with the fiscal picture
reconciled, the questions she should ask the Revenue witness, and what to do
about the constitutional point?
