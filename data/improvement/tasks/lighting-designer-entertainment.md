# Task for: lighting-designer-entertainment

I manage an indie rock band heading out on a 22-date theater tour
(1,200 to 2,500 capacity) in March, plus two festival slots where we get
a 25-minute changeover on a shared rig. Our budget covers a floor package
only: eight moving spots, twelve LED wash bars, four strobes and one
hazer, all run from a console we carry. We'll use house rigs overhead
wherever they exist. The 16-song set has three songs on click with
timecode, and the rest float. The singer wants the bridge of "Static" to
be a full-stage white strobe blast for 20 seconds. Night 9 is being
livestreamed with three cameras, and two of the theaters are historic
buildings that have told us haze may set off their detectors. Our
console tech has done lighting for a lot of bands but has never built a
tour file that has to adapt to a different house rig every night. What
does the design and programming plan look like, and what do we send
venues ahead of time?
