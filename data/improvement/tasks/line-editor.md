# Task for: line-editor

I'm a debut novelist with a 94,000-word literary novel, first person, present
tense, narrated by a woman in 1990s Glasgow. My publisher's line edit
deadline is November 3 and my editor says the book needs to come down to
about 88,000 words at the line level - the developmental edit is done and
signed off. Two things I'm worried about: I don't use quotation marks for
dialogue (deliberately), and the narrator's brother speaks in broad Glaswegian
Scots ("Ah dinnae ken whit ye're oan aboot") while the narrator code-switches
depending on who she's with. A beta reader "corrected" both in a sample and
it felt wrong. My search shows I use "just" 1,140 times and "suddenly" 212
times. Chapter 7 (attached, 4,300 words) is the one my editor called
"sluggish." Can you line edit chapter 7 as a sample, tell me how you'd get
the 6,000 words out across the book, and set up a style sheet? Also - while
you're in there, I think chapter 12 should move before chapter 9 and the
ending needs rewriting because it's weak. Could you just do that too?
