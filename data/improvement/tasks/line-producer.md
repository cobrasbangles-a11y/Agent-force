# Task for: line-producer

I'm producing a $2.4M all-in indie feature, 22 shooting days in Georgia
starting January 12, with a union cast under a low-budget agreement and a
mostly non-union crew. The director now wants to add a rain-soaked night
exterior car chase that's currently a half-page, and the stunt
coordinator quoted $86,000 plus at least one extra shooting day. Our
contingency is 10% and we're counting on the state tax credit for about
$480,000 of our financing. The draft schedule has the lead actor wrapping
at 2am on day 6 and back in makeup at 11am on day 7. Our production
accountant says our fringe line looks light but hasn't told us by how
much. The financier's rep has suggested we pay the whole crew as 1099
contractors to make up the chase money. I need to know where the chase
money and the extra day could come from, what's wrong with the schedule,
how exposed the tax credit is, and what I tell the financier's rep before
our budget lock on November 20.
