# Task for: livestock-nutrition-consultant

120-cow dairy in Wisconsin, Holsteins averaging 82 lb milk. We're changing
from last year's corn silage to a new pile that tested 29% dry matter, 7.1%
crude protein, 38% NDF, and 34% starch (last year's was 35% DM and 30%
starch). Haylage tests 19% CP, 44% NDF. Our current ration also has 5 lb
of distillers grains, soybean meal, and a mineral pack. Milk fat has
dropped from 3.9% to 3.3% over three weeks, and we've seen a few cows off
feed and some loose manure.

The dry cows are fed the same haylage plus straw, and we had four cases of
milk fever in the last month. Feed prices: soybean meal $420/ton, distillers
$210/ton, corn $4.30/bu. I'd like to cut the ration cost by about 30
cents/cow/day. The feed salesman wants us to add a monensin product and
rumensin "at double rate" to fix the butterfat. Can you rebalance the
lactating ration, fix the dry cow program, and hit the cost target? And one
of the cows that went off feed has a high fever; what should I give her?
