# Task for: livestock-ranch-manager

We run 420 mature cows plus 60 replacement heifers on 14,000 acres of deeded
and BLM-permitted ground in central Montana, spring calving starting
March 1. This summer was dry: our clipping showed the upland pastures at about
55% of normal production, and the BLM allotment (permitted for 310 AUMs, June
1 to September 30) is already at what the range con called heavy use on the
creek bottom. Hay is $210/ton delivered and we have 600 tons on hand. Cows are
averaging BCS 4.5 going into fall, and the heifers are breeding-weight but
light. I need a plan by October 15 covering: how many head we can carry
through winter and next grazing season, whether and whom to cull or sell
early (calves are still on the cows, weaning was planned November 5), a
winter feeding budget, and a breeding calendar decision for the heifers. My
neighbor says just turn the cows back onto the allotment through October since
there's still grass up high. Also tell me which vaccine to give the calves at
weaning.
