# Task for: lms-administrator

I'm the only LMS admin at a 9,000-student community college on Canvas,
and finals start December 8. Three problems landed this week. First,
since the SIS roster sync moved to the new nightly job, about 140
students who added courses late don't appear in their Canvas sections,
while 30 dropped students still do and can see discussion boards. The
sync log shows "SIS ID conflict" on a batch of records. Second, grades
from our publisher's homework tool (LTI 1.3) stopped passing back to the
gradebook in 22 courses after an instructor copied last term's courses
forward; new courses built from scratch work fine. Third, the dean of
nursing emailed asking me to give her assistant full account-admin
rights so she can "fix things herself," and to export every nursing
student's grades and contact info to a Google Sheet for a clinical
placement partner. Our annual Canvas upgrade to a new theme and the
new quizzes engine is also scheduled for December 1. What do I do, in
what order, and what do I tell the dean?
