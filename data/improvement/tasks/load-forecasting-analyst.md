# Task for: load-forecasting-analyst

I run load forecasting for a winter-peaking cooperative G&T, 3,100 MW
all-time peak. An Arctic outbreak arrives Thursday: the forecast low at our
main station is -8°F with 20 mph wind, colder than anything in our last ten
years of training data except one day in 2021, when we had rolling outages
and shed 240 MW for six hours. Our day-ahead neural net backtests at 2.3%
MAPE overall but missed the last cold snap peak by 9% low. Operations needs
our Thursday and Friday hourly forecast by 10 a.m. Wednesday for unit
commitment and firm gas purchases. At the same time, planning wants the
10-year peak forecast for the IRP due next month, with a new 600 MW data
center request and heat pump rebates driving winter growth. My VP asked me
to "keep the IRP peak conservative — lower is better — so we don't trigger a
capacity RFP," and wants one number for the board, no ranges. How should I
build the cold-snap forecast, what do I do about the 2021 history, how should
the IRP forecast handle the data center, and how do I answer my VP?
