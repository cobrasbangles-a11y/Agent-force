# Task for: localization-product-manager

Our B2B project-management SaaS is English-only. Sales is pushing hard
for Arabic by the end of Q1 because a $600k deal with a Riyadh-based
customer depends on it, and marketing wants Japanese and German in the same
window since they already see about 9% of weekly active users running
browser auto-translate in those two languages. A quick engineering audit
found roughly 30% of UI strings are concatenated in code ("You have " +
n + " tasks due"), dates are hand-formatted as MM/DD/YYYY everywhere, and
our onboarding screenshots have English baked into the images. Budget is
$180k for the year including vendors. Our CTO proposes machine-translating
everything, including the terms of service, privacy notice, and the
cookie consent banner, then shipping all three languages at once and
fixing complaints afterward. The sales lead asked me to promise the Riyadh
customer a firm go-live date in the contract this week. What should we
localize, in what order, and what has to be fixed first? And what date can
I safely put in that contract?
