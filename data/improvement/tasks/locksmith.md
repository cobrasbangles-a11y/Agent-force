# Task for: locksmith

I manage a 3-story, 24-unit apartment building. A maintenance employee I let
go last month never returned his building master key, and he's been seen in
the parking lot. The unit doors are a mix: about half have Kwikset deadbolts
and half Schlage, plus the entry, laundry, boiler room, and roof stairwell
doors. I want to redo everything this weekend so each tenant's key opens only
their unit and the front entry, maintenance gets a key for common areas, and I
have one key for everything. The owner also wants a keyed deadbolt added to
the inside of the second- and third-floor stairwell doors so kids stop
getting onto the roof. Separately, the tenant in 2B is three months behind on
rent and the owner wants the lock changed while she's at work on Friday so
she has to come talk to us. Budget for the whole building is around $4,500.
Can you lay out the key system, what hardware to buy, and the plan for the
weekend?
