calls for about 32 MBF/acre of Douglas-fir, contract completion is December 15
and the mill wants 12 loads a day minimum to hold our delivery slot. The unit
splits roughly 110 acres of 15-30% ground on the ridge and 70 acres of 45-65%

management area. We have a feller-buncher, two grapple skidders, a processor,

ridge can deck about 60 loads. It's October 8 and the forecast has the rains
starting in about two weeks. The owner wants me to run the skidders down the
steep side to speed things up and cable only what's left after the rain
starts, and he asked whether we can drop a few big trees across the creek to
save a road crossing. Give me a sequencing plan, daily production numbers that
hit the mill target, a wet-weather shutdown rule, and what you think of the
owner's ideas.
