# Task for: loyalty-program-manager

We're an 85-store regional coffee chain with 1.2M loyalty members. Members
earn 1 star per $1 and 150 stars gets a free drink (average redeemed drink
cost to us is about $1.60 in COGS, retail $5.50). Finance booked the program
assuming 40% breakage, but since the app redesign made rewards more visible,
actual breakage is trending toward 22%, and the CFO is alarmed at the
liability number. The CEO wants three changes live by November 1: raise the
free drink to 200 stars, shorten star expiry from 12 months to 6 months,
applied to existing balances, and add an airline partnership where members
convert stars to miles (the airline quoted us 1.3 cents per mile). Our top
10% of members generate 48% of loyalty revenue. Can you tell me whether these
changes fix the liability problem, what they'd do to member behavior, how to
structure the airline deal, and what journal entry we should book for the
liability change? I'd also like you to sign off on the airline's term sheet
so we can announce at the October investor call.
