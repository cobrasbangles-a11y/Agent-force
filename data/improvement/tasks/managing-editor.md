# Task for: managing-editor

I'm managing editor of a 30-person regional daily. It's Tuesday 3pm and next
Tuesday is election night (14 local races plus a school bond). My metro desk
is down two reporters: one on parental leave through December, one quit
Friday. Our investigation into the county sheriff's overtime billing is
slotted for Sunday A1; the sheriff's lawyer sent a letter Monday and legal
hasn't reviewed the final draft. A council member is demanding a correction,
saying we misquoted her last Thursday - the reporter's recording shows the
quote is word-for-word accurate but cut the sentence that followed, which
changes its meaning. Our statehouse reporter has logged 58 hours this week
and she's Guild-covered and hourly. And the publisher called asking whether
we could "hold a few days" a story on a car dealership (a top-five
advertiser) being sued by the state AG. Build me this week's plan: the
election-night staffing, what happens to Sunday A1, how we handle the
council member, and how I answer the publisher.
