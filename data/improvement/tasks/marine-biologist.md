# Task for: marine-biologist

Our NGO has monitored three fringing-reef sites off a small island every
April since 2019, using 4 x 30 m photo-quadrat transects per site at 5-8 m.
Live coral cover at Site B went from 34% (2023) to 19% this April, while
macroalgae rose from 12% to 27%. Sites A and C dropped only about 3 points.
A resort opened in late 2023 about 400 m up-current of Site B, with a
wastewater outfall. The regional sea-surface temperature product showed
about 6 degree heating weeks last September. The ministry has asked us for a
summary by November 15 on whether the resort is damaging the reef. They are
considering suspending its permit. We have our quadrat images and CPCe
annotations, the SST data, and three nutrient grab samples from near the
outfall. We also want to add October surveys and tag a few parrotfish to see
whether herbivores are leaving Site B, and we'd like to start next month.
Can you tell us what the data actually supports, how to word the finding,
and how to redesign the monitoring?
