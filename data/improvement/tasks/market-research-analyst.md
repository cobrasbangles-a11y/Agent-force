# Task for: market-research-analyst

We're a startup with a blood test that screens dogs for early signs of
cancer, sold through veterinary clinics at $250 retail. We're raising a
Series A in ten days and need a US and UK market size for the deck. Our
founder wants to open with "a $150B global pet industry" and take 1% of it.
We've also drafted a survey for a consumer panel of 2,000 US adults, budget
$15k, with the headline question: "Would you pay $250 for a test that could
save your dog's life?" A pilot with 60 respondents came back 71% yes, and
we'd like to use that to project adoption. Our 40 pilot clinics ordered 900
tests in six months. Investors keep asking whether vets or owners are the
real buyer. Can you give us a defensible market size, fix the survey before
it fields Friday, and tell us how to present the adoption number? Investors
want one clean TAM figure, not a range. Also, if you can, pull insights from
our clinic ordering portal's usage logs to show engagement trends.
