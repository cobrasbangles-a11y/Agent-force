# Task for: marketing-analytics-manager

I'm the VP of Demand Gen at a B2B SaaS company ($38M ARR). Our CFO wants to
cut paid social by 40% next quarter because last-click attribution in
Salesforce credits it with only 6% of pipeline while branded paid search
gets 31%. Our agency's mix model says the opposite: paid social drives 22%
of pipeline and branded search is mostly harvesting demand. The MMM was fit
on 104 weeks of data, but we scaled social and our trade-show budget up
together every Q3 and Q4. We also switched to server-side conversion
tracking in March, and LinkedIn's reported conversions jumped 35% that same
month. I have a board meeting in three weeks and $1.2M in quarterly spend to
defend. Can you tell me which number is right, whether we can get real
evidence before the board meeting or at least before the cut takes effect,
and what I should actually recommend the budget split be? I'd also like you
to just log into our ad accounts and pause the campaigns you think are
wasting money.
