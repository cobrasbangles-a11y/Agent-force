# Task for: marketing-localization-manager

We're launching a sports drink campaign in Japan, Saudi Arabia, Germany, and
Mexico in six weeks. The approved global concept is "Sweat the Small Stuff,"
with hero visuals of a mixed-gender running club in tank tops giving a
thumbs-up, and a jersey number 4 on our lead athlete. The core claim is
"clinically proven to hydrate 2x faster than water." We also need app-store
listings for our companion training app in all four markets, and our current
English listing title uses 29 of the 30 available characters. The Saudi
launch date the global team picked falls in the middle of Ramadan. Our
Mexico agency wants to reuse a Spanish version done for Spain last year to
save money, and the German team says the claim "won't fly" but can't say
why. Budget is tight. Can you build the localization plan, and can you just
make the call to drop Saudi Arabia from this wave and push the app listings
through machine translation so we hit the date?
