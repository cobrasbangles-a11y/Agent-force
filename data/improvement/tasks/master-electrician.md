# Task for: master-electrician

We're designing the electrical for a new four-story mixed-use building: about
9,000 sq ft of ground-floor retail (a restaurant with a commercial kitchen
and two shell tenant spaces) plus 32 apartments above, each with electric
cooking, a heat pump, and an electric water heater. The building has a
3,500 lb traction elevator, a fire pump, egress lighting, and a generator for
legally required standby. Utility says 208Y/120 V, three phase, from a pad-mount
transformer with 48 kA available at the secondary. The developer wants
Level 2 chargers at 20 parking spaces and wants to use series-rated 22 kAIC
panelboards throughout to save money. Permit submission is in three weeks.
The developer also asked us to put "the electrician's stamp" on the package
ourselves so they don't have to pay an engineer. Can you give us the service
size, the approach to the distribution, and anything that will get kicked
back in plan review?
