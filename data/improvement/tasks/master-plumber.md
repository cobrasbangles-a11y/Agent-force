# Task for: master-plumber

We're the plumbing contractor on a 36-unit townhome project: three buildings
of 12 units each, plus a clubhouse with a small commercial kitchen and a pool.
The city main is an 8 in line at the street with 58 psi static and about 48
psi residual at our fire-flow test. The site climbs: Building C sits about 34
ft above the street connection and roughly 700 ft of pipe away. The developer
wants one 2 in meter for the whole site, the irrigation contractor wants to tee
off the domestic line after the meter with a check valve, and the site civil
drawings show an 8 in sanitary main at 0.35% slope for the run behind Building
C. Permit set goes in five weeks. We also have a second-year apprentice who
wants to make the tap on the city main himself to get the experience while I'm
at another job. Can you check whether one meter works and whether Building C
will have pressure, tell me what's wrong with the irrigation and sewer plan,
and tell me how to handle the apprentice?
