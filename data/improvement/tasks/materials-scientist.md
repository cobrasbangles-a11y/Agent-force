# Task for: materials-scientist

We make 6061-T6 aluminum brackets for agricultural sprayer booms. A field
batch is cracking at the weld toe after roughly 8 months, and we've had 11
returns out of about 2,400 shipped. Our supplier's mill certs show yield
strength of 285 MPa, which is well above the 240 MPa minimum. Our incoming
tensile coupons also pass. Last year we switched from GMAW with 4043 filler
to 5356 filler, and we moved welding to a new vendor at the same time. Two
fracture surfaces show beach marks and a flat region near the toe, and a
third looks rougher and is covered in white deposit (the sprayers carry
liquid fertilizer). Our customer wants a root-cause statement and a
corrective action by the end of the month. Our purchasing manager wants you
to confirm the brackets are fine because the tensile data passes, so we
can keep shipping the current inventory. What's likely happening, what
analysis do we need, and what can we responsibly tell the customer now?
