our device "reduces post-op complications," which isn't in our labeling.
My Vendormate credentialing expired last week, and one surgeon wants me
in the OR Thursday for a case with a sample unit. Last month, a nurse
mentioned a device tip that separated during a case. Build me a plan for the
VAC, the trial, and these surgeon requests.
