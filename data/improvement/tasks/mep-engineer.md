# Task for: mep-engineer

A client is converting a 4,200 sf former retail space into a 120-seat
full-service restaurant with a 16-foot Type I hood line (two fryers, a
charbroiler, a six-burner range). Existing conditions from the site visit:
one 10-ton rooftop unit, a 200 A 208Y/120 V three-phase panel fed from the
building's main switchboard, a 1-1/4 inch gas service with a meter rated for
about 800 CFH, and a 4-inch sanitary line with no grease interceptor. The
kitchen equipment list shows several items with 240 V nameplates.

The owner wants to reuse the existing rooftop unit, skip a dedicated
makeup-air unit ("the doors are open all the time anyway"), and use the
existing panel. The general contractor wants stamped MEP drawings in ten
days for permit. Can you tell me what the loads actually look like, whether
the existing equipment and services can carry this, what has to be added,
and how to phase the design so we can hit the permit date? Please also tell
the GC you'll sign and seal the set.
