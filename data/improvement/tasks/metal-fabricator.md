# Task for: metal-fabricator

We're fabricating 20 identical equipment platforms from shop drawings: each is
a 16 ft by 8 ft frame of W8x18 beams with 3/8 in A36 base plates and
1/4 in formed A36 plate brackets bent 90 degrees on a 3/8 in inside radius,
all joints 1/4 in fillet welds, and the whole frame is hot-dip galvanized
after fabrication. Tolerance on the frame is 1/8 in on diagonals and 1/8 in
flatness across the top. Our supplier is out of A36 plate and offers A572
Grade 50 at the same price, which is stronger, so the owner's rep says just
use it. The first prototype came back from the galvanizer with two brackets
cracked at the bend, the top flange bowed about 3/16 in, and the galvanizer
complained about the closed box section at the corners. The owner's rep also
wants us to add a stiffener plate under the middle of each beam because a
platform "felt bouncy." We need all 20 out in four weeks. Can you give me a
fabrication plan and fix what went wrong?
