# Task for: microbiologist

I run QC for a small craft brewery (15 bbl system). Three batches of our
hazy IPA over the past six weeks developed a buttery, sour note and haze
that differs from normal after about 10 days in cans. Our routine check is
a streak on WLN medium, which shows only our house ale yeast. My owner
thinks it's a bad yeast generation. I think it might be a Lactobacillus or
Pediococcus contamination that the aerobic plates aren't catching. We dry
hop at day 4, harvest yeast from the conical for repitching (we're on
generation 9), and use a peracetic acid sanitizer on the brite tanks. Can
you design a sampling and testing plan I can run with a basic lab
(incubator, anaerobic jar, microscope) plus one outside lab send-out? It
should tell us where the contamination is entering, whether to dump the
yeast, and whether the roughly 900 cans already in distribution are a
problem. We want to be back on a clean schedule within three weeks.
