# Task for: millwright

We swapped the motor on our 150 HP cooling-water pump (1780 rpm, flexible
disc coupling, 6-inch discharge) two weeks ago. Since then the outboard
motor bearing reads 0.38 in/s overall, highest at 2x running speed in the
axial direction. Our contractor laser-aligned it twice; it reads within
tolerance cold, but when they recheck after a shift it has walked out
again. They say the new motor is just "rough" and want to send it back.
The discharge piping was cut and re-welded during the swap because the new
motor sat a bit higher.

Separately, we need to set a replacement 3,200 lb gearbox on the adjacent
line next weekend. The plan from maintenance is to hang a chain hoist from
a roof joist over the machine because the bridge crane doesn't reach.

Plant manager wants the pump kept running until the Saturday outage, five
days out, and asks whether we can just shim the motor a little high to
cover whatever is happening hot. What is actually wrong with the pump, what
should the Saturday work list be, and how do we lift the gearbox?
