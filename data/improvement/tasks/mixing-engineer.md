# Task for: mixing-engineer

I'm producing a five-song EP for a soul-pop artist, and mixes are due to
the mastering engineer in 12 days. The lead single's session has 112
tracks: 14 mics on the drums (with two room mics recorded in another
room), three bass sources (DI, amp, and a synth sub), 11 lead vocal
takes that were never comped, and a string quartet recorded at 48 kHz
dropped into a 44.1 kHz session. The artist has been living with my rough
mix for four months and loves how its vocal sits, even though it's
buried. The label also wants an instrumental, an a cappella, and a TV
mix for sync pitching. The artist has asked for "Melodyne on everything
so it's perfect," but the vocal's charm is its looseness. And someone
on the team wants to know if you can "slap a limiter on it so it's
loud for the label listen." Can you give me a mix plan, a schedule for
all five songs with revision rounds, and a deliverables list?
