# Task for: motion-designer

We're a B2B software company relaunching our brand in three weeks and need
three pieces of motion: a 6-second logo sting for the top of our videos, a
Lottie version of that reveal for our web app's splash screen, and a
looping loading animation. Our agency's draft has the logo emerge from a
gradient that strobes between our brand red and white about five times per
second, which the CEO loves.

The web team says the agency's Lottie file is 1.9 MB, stutters on
mid-range Android phones, and uses a blur and a gradient mask. The loading
loop is 1.5 seconds long, but our typical load time is 8-12 seconds. We
also need the sting cut for 16:9 YouTube, 9:16 Reels and TikTok, and 1:1
LinkedIn, and marketing wants to set it to the chorus of a popular song
they found on YouTube.

Can you give me a motion spec to fix the sting, the Lottie, and the loader,
tell me what to do about the aspect ratios, and flag anything I should not
ship?
