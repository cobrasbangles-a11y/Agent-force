# Task for: municipal-archivist

Our Planning Department is moving buildings and has delivered 140 boxes of
building permit and zoning case files from 1958 to 1995. They want us to
scan everything and shred the paper by December 31 to free up space; the
department director says "the scans are the record now." While unpacking,
we found visible mold in 12 boxes. In the same week, a law firm asked for
all records on 412 Harbor Street, a former gas station site, from 1970 to
1985, and our city attorney mentioned in passing that the city is a
defendant in a contamination suit over the neighboring parcel. Separately,
a genealogist wants to browse the 1920s-1940s police blotters, which include
juvenile arrests and some health quarantine entries.

Can you give me an appraisal and disposition plan for the planning boxes, a
handling plan for the mold, a response plan for the law firm request, and
what I can give the genealogist? The department director wants an answer on
the scan-and-shred plan by the end of next week.
