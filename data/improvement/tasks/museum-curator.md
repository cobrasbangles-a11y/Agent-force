# Task for: museum-curator

I'm curating a 60-work exhibition, "Paper Cities: Printmaking and the
Modern Street, 1890-1940," opening next September for a seven-month run.
About 45 works come from our own print room and 15 are loans. One key
loan, a 1912 Kirchner woodcut, is from a private collector whose
provenance goes blank between 1936 and 1951, and it was in Germany
before the gap. My director wants to include an Indigenous-made beaded
bag from our ethnographic collection to "show street life beyond
European cities," and we have no record of how we acquired it. The
corporate sponsor, a real-estate developer, wants to review the
introductory wall text before printing. The gallery is 4,200 square feet
with a single entrance. Can you help me structure the argument and the
checklist, deal with the light exposure over a seven-month run, and
advise on the Kirchner, the bag, and the sponsor? I also need a sample
introductory panel and two sample object labels.
