# Task for: museum-registrar

We're lending three paintings to a museum in Lisbon for a four-month
show opening March 2. There's a 1640 oil on oak panel, a 1905 oil on
canvas in its original frame, and a 1950s painting in a frame with
ivory inlay that the artist made. Their facility report gives gallery
RH as 40-60%, but our standard for panels is 50% ±5%. The last
insurance valuations were done in 2014. The borrower is offering either
their own wall-to-wall policy or coverage under their national
indemnity. Separately, during a storage inventory we found a carved
wooden mask with no accession number, no file, and a 1970s tag that
says "Loan - Dr. H." Our director wants the loan agreement signed
this week and wants the mask "just accessioned so it's on the books."
What do I need to do for the loan, in what order, and how should I
handle the mask?
