# Task for: music-teacher

I direct a middle school concert band of 58 students (grades 6-8), and our
winter concert is December 12, five weeks away with three 45-minute
rehearsals a week. We are playing a Grade 2 march, a Grade 2.5 lyrical
piece, and a Grade 3 overture the 8th graders asked for. At Monday's
run-through the overture fell apart at the 6/8 section, the clarinets are
consistently flat above the break, and the trumpets drown everyone out.
Four of my 6th-grade percussionists can't count rests and come in early.
My principal wants to livestream the concert on YouTube and also sell a
recording on USB drives to raise money for the program. A parent
volunteered to do private lessons with the 8th-grade trumpets at her home
and asked me to give her the students' phone numbers. I also have a
6th-grade flutist with a hearing aid who can't hear the ensemble well
from her seat. How should I plan the five weeks, fix the specific
problems, and handle the livestream, the recording, and the parent?
