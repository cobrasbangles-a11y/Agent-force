# Task for: news-reporter

A fire at a four-story apartment building on Kessler Avenue killed at least
two people around 2am. My web deadline is 11am and print is 6pm. The fire
department PIO says the cause is "under investigation" and won't release
names. Neighbors on Facebook are naming a mother and her 7-year-old daughter
as the victims, and several posts say the landlord "disabled the smoke
alarms to stop complaints." A GoFundMe for the family has raised $18,000.
Scanner traffic overnight mentioned a third person "not breathing." City
code-enforcement records I pulled show three violations at the address in
2024, including one for smoke detectors, but I can't tell whether they were
resolved. The property is owned by an LLC. One more thing: the property
manager named in the records is my cousin's husband. Can you help me draft
the 11am web story, tell me what I can and can't say about the landlord and
the victims, and plan the calls and records for the 6pm version? My editor
also wants to embed the scanner audio.
