# Task for: nonprofit-executive-director

I'm the executive director of a $3.2 million youth mentoring nonprofit.
Last week our largest funder, a family foundation that gives $1.2 million a
year (38% of revenue), told us it won't renew after June 30. We have about
$520,000 in unrestricted cash, roughly two months of operating expense, and
a $400,000 restricted gift for a new teen center building we haven't started.
The board chair wants to "borrow" the building fund to cover payroll through
the fall and replace it later. A board member has offered to lease us office
space in a building his company owns at 30% below market, which would save
about $60,000 a year. Staff are already hearing rumors, and I have 44
employees.

The board meets in ten days. Can you help me build a plan: cash scenarios,
what to do about the building fund and the lease offer, a fundraising
response, and a board packet with the decisions framed? I also need to know
what I should tell staff this week.
