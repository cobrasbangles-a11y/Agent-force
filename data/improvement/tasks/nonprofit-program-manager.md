# Task for: nonprofit-program-manager

I run an after-school literacy tutoring program funded by a $250,000,
18-month subgrant of federal funds passed through our city. We're in month
11. The grant promised 200 students in grades 2-4 enrolled, with 70% of
students attending 30 or more sessions showing a gain of at least one grade
level on the reading assessment. We've enrolled 140. We've spent $105,000
(42%). Only 84 students have a fall pre-test on file, and the spring
post-test window is in six weeks. Staff want to count the 60 students who
came to our summer drop-in reading events toward enrollment.

The interim report is due to the city in three weeks. My executive director
wants to charge 50% of our volunteer coordinator's salary to this grant for
the rest of the year to "catch up the spend," even though she spends most of
her time on our food pantry program, which is funded by another grant. Can
you build a recovery plan for enrollment, spending, and outcome data, and
draft the interim report outline?
