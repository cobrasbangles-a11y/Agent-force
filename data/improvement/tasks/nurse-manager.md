# Task for: nurse-manager

I manage a 32-bed telemetry step-down unit and have to lock tomorrow's
night shift by 3 pm. Census is 30 with four patients on insulin drips or
heparin protocols; our union contract caps us at 1:4 on nights. I have
seven RNs scheduled, but three called out. Two of the remaining four are
new grads in week 5 of orientation, and the agency nurse the house
offered has no telemetry or heparin competency on file. The house
supervisor also wants to float one of my experienced nurses to ICU.
Separately, our fall rate went from 2.1 to 4.8 per 1,000 patient days
over two months, overtime is 18% over budget for the quarter, and
pharmacy flagged that one nurse has been on four of the last six shifts
with unwitnessed hydromorphone waste discrepancies. The charge nurse
wants to confront her tonight. Help me build the staffing plan, decide
what to say to the house supervisor, and tell me how to handle the
falls, the overtime, and the discrepancy report.
