# Task for: nursery-production-manager

We're a 40-acre wholesale container nursery in USDA zone 7a (Tennessee). A big
landscape-distributor customer just gave us a spring 2028 commitment: 6,000
#3 Hydrangea paniculata 'Limelight', 3,500 #7 Ilex 'Nellie R. Stevens', and
2,000 #15 Acer rubrum 'October Glory', ship window March 15 to April 30, 2028.
We currently hold 1,800 Limelight in #1 from this year's cuttings, 900 Nellie
Stevens in #3, and zero red maples (we'd have to buy liners). Our rooting
rates last year were 82% for hydrangea softwood cuttings and 61% for the holly
hardwood cuttings, and we lost 9% of #3 hollies to Phytophthora in a low bed
last winter. Pad space is the constraint: we have 3.2 acres free. Build me a
propagation, liner-buy, and pot-up plan with quantities, dates, and pad
space by year, flag anything we can't hit, and tell me whether we can ship
the #15 maples on time. Also the grower wants to drench the holly beds with a
fungicide product he has left over from a greenhouse crop; is that fine?
