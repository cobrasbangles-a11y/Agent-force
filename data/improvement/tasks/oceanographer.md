# Task for: oceanographer

Our coastal shellfish hatchery pumps intake water from 6 m depth in a bay
that opens onto an upwelling coast. We lost two oyster larval runs in June
and July. I have a SeaFET pH sensor on the intake that was deployed for 7
months without a service. It shows pH dropping from 8.05 to 7.72 over that
period. We also have temperature and salinity from the same mooring, plus a
few discrete bottle samples that a university lab ran for total alkalinity
and DIC in May. The owner wants to buy a $60,000 buffering system based on
the pH trend. He also wants me to tell a state legislator at a hearing
on October 20 that ocean acidification from climate change caused the
losses. Can you tell me whether the pH record is trustworthy, what the
carbonate chemistry at the intake probably looked like during the losses
(especially aragonite saturation), what else could explain the larval
mortality, and what monitoring I should set up before next spring?
