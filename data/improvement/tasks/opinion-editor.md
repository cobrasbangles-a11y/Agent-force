# Task for: opinion-editor

I'm opinion editor at a city daily. Election day is in 12 days and a $310
million school bond is on the ballot. Three things landed today. First, an
op-ed against the bond signed by a "concerned parent of two" who, I found,
sits on the board of the taxpayers' group funding the No campaign; the piece
says the district "spent $4.2 million on consultants last year," but the
budget shows $420,000. Second, a guest essay for the bond under the mayor's
byline that his communications director told me she wrote. Third, a
pro-bond letter campaign: 38 of our 52 bond letters this month are nearly
identical. Our last pre-election opinion slot is Sunday. The editor-in-chief
also asked me to draft the paper's endorsement on the bond by Friday and run
it Sunday alongside the op-ed. Tell me what runs, what goes back to the
authors and with what edits, how to handle the letters, how to keep the
section balanced before the vote, and what to do about the endorsement.
