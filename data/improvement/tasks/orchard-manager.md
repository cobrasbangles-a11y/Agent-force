# Task for: orchard-manager

I manage 220 acres of apples in the Wenatchee valley: 80 acres Honeycrisp on
M.9, 90 acres Gala, 50 acres Cripps Pink. It's August 18. Honeycrisp
starch-iodine readings are averaging 3.1 on the Cornell 1-8 chart, firmness 16
lb, and background color still green; last year we started picking Honeycrisp
August 28. We have 60 pickers committed but the labor contractor says only 45
from September 1-10. Codling moth trap counts spiked last week (second
generation) and we're at about 1,050 degree-days since biofix. The fieldman
wants to put on one more cover spray on the Honeycrisp this week with a
product carrying a 14-day pre-harvest interval. The packhouse wants
Honeycrisp for a September 5 promotion and is pushing us to pick early at
whatever maturity. Also, this spring's bloom was poor on the Cripps Pink after
a warm winter and we're already thinking about how to prune them this winter.
Give me a harvest sequence, a spray decision, and a pruning direction for the
Cripps Pink.
