# Task for: organic-chemist

I'm a medicinal chemist at a small biotech. We need 150 g of a
biaryl amide intermediate for a tox study in 8 weeks. The current route is
a Suzuki coupling of an aryl bromide bearing a free secondary amine with a
boronic acid (Pd(PPh3)4, K2CO3, dioxane/water, 90 °C), then HATU amide
coupling. At 500 mg we got 78% on the Suzuki and 85% on the amide. At 20 g
the Suzuki dropped to 41%, with a lot of protodeboronated side product and
a dark crude. The product also retains 900 ppm Pd, and our tox group's
limit is 20 ppm. Our NMR of the final compound shows a doubled set of
peaks in the amide region, which my manager thinks is an impurity. He
wants me to repeat the column. Can you propose changes to get the Suzuki
back up, a Pd removal strategy, a view on the doubled peaks, and a
realistic plan for the 150 g campaign in our kilo lab (a 20 L jacketed
reactor)? We have to order materials by Friday.
