# Task for: packaging-designer

We make single-ingredient dog treats and are redesigning our line of ten
flavors ahead of a regional grocery rollout. Files go to the printer in four
weeks. The line is a stand-up pouch printed flexo on kraft-laminated film
plus a small folding carton printed offset. Our brand color is a bright
Pantone orange, and on the kraft pouch mockup it came back muddy brown.

Marketing wants these on the front: "Human-Grade," "All Natural," "Vet
Recommended," "Made in USA" (our beef is sourced from Uruguay and processed
here), and "Compostable Pouch" because the film supplier says it is. Our
current back panel is so crowded the UPC was shrunk to 70% and printed in
our dark red to match the palette.

Shoppers also can't tell flavors apart: all ten pouches look nearly
identical from the aisle. Can you give me a front-panel hierarchy, a flavor
system, fixes for the color and barcode, and a read on which claims we can
print? I need something to take to our printer and our co-packer next week.
