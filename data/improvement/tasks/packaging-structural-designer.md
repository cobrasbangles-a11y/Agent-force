# Task for: packaging-structural-designer

We sell a 4.2 kg drip coffee maker with a glass carafe. Today it ships in a
printed retail carton with EPS foam end caps, and for e-commerce we put
that carton inside a brown overbox with air pillows. We're seeing a 3.8%
damage rate online, almost all broken carafes, and a major marketplace is
pushing us to ship in our own container with no overbox. Sustainability also
wants the EPS replaced with molded pulp.

Retail pallets are stacked four cartons high and double-stacked in the
warehouse, and our Gulf Coast warehouse runs around 85% relative humidity in
summer, with 60 to 120 days of dwell. The current carton is 32 ECT
single-wall C-flute, and our supplier's quote for the new design has to stay
under $3.10 a unit at 50,000 units a year.

Can you propose a structure that works for both retail and
ship-in-own-container, do the stacking math, and tell our buyer whether we
can say it's "certified" for the marketplace program in the sales deck next
month?
