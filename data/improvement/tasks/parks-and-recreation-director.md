# Task for: parks-and-recreation-director

I'm the parks director for a city of 64,000 with a $1.8 million capital
budget for next year. The requests on my desk are: resurfacing and pump
replacement at our 1968 outdoor pool ($1.1 million; the pump failed twice
last summer); replacing surfacing under three playgrounds our certified
inspector flagged as below the required fall-height depth ($210,000); eight
new pickleball courts that one council member wants in his district
($640,000); and accessible routes to two parks named in an accessibility
complaint filed last month ($380,000). One pickleball site is on land the
city developed decades ago with a federal outdoor recreation grant. The youth
soccer league also wants exclusive Saturday rights to all six lighted fields,
and staff want to raise adult league fees 15% to cover field maintenance.

The council work session is in three weeks. Can you give me a ranked capital
list with the reasoning, what to do about the playgrounds right now, a
position on the soccer request and the fee increase, and talking points for
the council member?
