# Task for: particle-physicist

I'm a postdoc on an LHC experiment doing a search for a narrow dimuon
resonance between 12 and 70 GeV in 140 fb^-1 of Run 2 data. We unblinded
last week. There's a local excess at 28.4 GeV of 3.6 sigma, with a mass
resolution there of about 0.4 GeV. Our background model is a fourth-order
Bernstein polynomial fit to the full spectrum. A student notes that a
fifth-order polynomial reduces the excess to 3.1 sigma. My convener wants
to tighten the muon isolation cut, which would push the local significance
to 4.2 sigma, and to present at a conference in 3 weeks as "evidence". I
need to know what the global significance is likely to be, how to handle
the background function choice properly, what cross-checks to run before
the collaboration review, and how to answer the isolation-cut suggestion.
Also, how should we word this if it goes into a conference talk?
