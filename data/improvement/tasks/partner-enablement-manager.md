# Task for: partner-enablement-manager

We're relaunching our partner program in eight weeks with three tiers
(Registered, Silver, Gold). Gold requires four certified sales reps and two
certified implementation engineers per partner, and unlocks a 12-point
margin bump. We have 140 partners, about 1,900 partner reps, across the US,
Germany, Brazil and Japan, and our current certification is a single
45-minute multiple-choice exam that 71% of people pass on the first try.
Three things are on fire. One of our largest distributors has told us it
will run the training itself, rebranded, and wants to add its own slide
comparing us to two competitors. Our channel team has noticed that a few
partners have the same person taking the exam under several colleagues'
names to hit Gold. And my VP wants a slide for the board by the end of the
month showing that certified partners close 40% bigger deals, which is what
our raw numbers show. Can you design the tiered certification program and
tell me how to handle each of those three issues?
