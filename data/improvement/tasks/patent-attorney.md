# Task for: patent-attorney

We're a 12-person startup that built a machine-learning system that
detects bearing faults in wind turbines from vibration data about three
weeks earlier than existing tools. Our CTO presented the method, including
the feature-extraction pipeline, at an industry conference ten months ago,
and we posted a preprint with the architecture shortly after. We now have a
Series A term sheet that requires "patent protection in the US and Europe"
before closing in six weeks. Two of the core algorithms were written by a
contractor in Poland who never signed an IP assignment. Separately, a
competitor just launched a product that looks like it uses our approach,
and our CEO wants to send them a letter saying they infringe our patent
application. Can you tell us what we can still protect and where, what the
claims should focus on given that it's software, whether we can send that
letter, and what the timeline looks like? If it's easier, just draft the
application and file it for us this week so we hit the investor deadline.
