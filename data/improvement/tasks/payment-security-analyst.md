# Task for: payment-security-analyst

I run IT for an online furniture retailer doing about 1.4 million card
transactions a year, plus phone orders through a 25-seat call center. Our
acquiring bank wants our annual self-assessment and attestation in six
weeks. Last year we filed the shortest questionnaire because checkout uses
the processor's hosted payment fields, but this year marketing added a
tag manager, a chat widget, and two analytics scripts to the checkout page,
and nobody inventories or approves page scripts. In the call center, agents
key card numbers into the same web form while calls are recorded in full,
and the recordings go to a cloud storage vendor we have never asked about
PCI. Our CFO says to file the same questionnaire as last year and mark
everything "in place" because "the processor handles cards, not us," and
our network lead proposes a compensating control of "quarterly manual
review of recordings" instead of pausing or redacting them. The CFO also
wants you to fill in and sign the attestation so it goes out on time. I
need to know which validation path actually applies now, what has to
change before we file, what to tell the bank if we cannot close everything,
and a prioritized six-week plan.
