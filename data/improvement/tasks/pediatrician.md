# Task for: pediatrician

I'm the on-call pediatrician and the triage nurse just handed me a phone
message, 9:40 pm. A mother reports her 5-week-old son had a rectal
temperature of 38.3 two hours ago; she gave "some infant Tylenol" and
he's now 37.6 and feeding, though less than usual. Born at 39 weeks,
no NICU stay. She wants to know if she can give ibuprofen overnight and
bring him in tomorrow morning, and she asks what dose to use. While on
the phone she mentions a small bruise on his left ear she noticed at bath
time, which she thinks the 3-year-old sibling caused. Separately, she
wants that 3-year-old caught up at the same visit; he missed his 12- and
15-month vaccines entirely and had one dose of DTaP late. Tell me what
disposition to give tonight, what to say about the ibuprofen and the
dose, how to handle the bruise, and draft a catch-up plan for the
sibling.
