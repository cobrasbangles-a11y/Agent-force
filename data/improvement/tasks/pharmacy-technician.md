# Task for: pharmacy-technician

Saturday, retail store, and my pharmacist is on a 30-minute lunch with
five scripts in queue. First: a handwritten prescription I read as
"hydroxyzine 25 mg, 1 tab TID PRN," but the prescriber's letters could
also be hydralazine, and the office is closed. Second: insulin glargine
U-100 pens, 5 x 3 mL box, sig "32 units at bedtime," and the claim
rejected refill-too-soon because the last fill was billed as a 30-day
supply. Third: my CII perpetual count shows 6 fewer oxycodone 10 mg
tablets than the log after this morning's fills. Fourth: a patient at
the counter wants her oxycodone refilled four days early because she's
flying out tomorrow, and she also asks me whether she can take it with
the new antibiotic she just picked up. Tell me how to handle each one
before my pharmacist gets back, including what days' supply the insulin
should have been billed for.
