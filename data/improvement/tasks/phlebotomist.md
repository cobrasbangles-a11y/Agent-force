# Task for: phlebotomist

Outpatient draw station, 7:45 am, courier leaves at 8:15. Next patient
has orders for two sets of blood cultures, PT/INR, CMP, CBC, lactate, and
ammonia. She had a left mastectomy with lymph node removal and has a
dialysis fistula in her right forearm. Her veins are poor, and the only
thing I can feel is a small vein on the back of her right hand, below
the fistula. She tells me she fainted during her last draw. When I
checked in her requisition, her name matched another patient's in the
system with a different date of birth, and she forgot her ID. My
supervisor told me to "just get it done, we're behind." Walk me through
what to do in order: identification, whether and where I can draw, the
order of draw if I use a butterfly, handling for the lactate and
ammonia, and what to do if she starts to faint.
