me a call script for the call we keep getting this week: customers whose
bill jumped from about $79 to $134 because their 12-month promo expired,

someone pay." Please give me the verification sequence, the talk track,
how to handle the outage part, the non-account-holder case, cancellation
requests, and when to escalate.
