# Task for: physical-security-consultant

I'm operations director for a contract electronics manufacturer: one
180,000 sq ft plant, 420 staff over three shifts, and a locked cage holding
about $2 million of high-value processors at any time. Over the past five
months $140,000 of chips went missing from the cage, and badge logs show
nothing unusual, although staff admit the cage door is often propped during
shift change. Our largest customer's supplier audit is in eight weeks and
requires controlled access and 90-day video retention for the cage; we
currently keep 14 days. My budget is $180,000 this year. Facilities wants to
chain the two rear emergency exits shut at night because people use them to
slip out for smoke breaks, and our plant manager wants to cut the overnight
guard post to pay for cameras. HR has a specific second-shift technician
they suspect and asked whether you could set up hidden cameras in the
locker room and run a covert test to catch him. I need a prioritized plan I
can defend to the customer's auditor, a spend split between procedure,
hardware, and staffing, and your position on the exits, the guard post, and
HR's request.
