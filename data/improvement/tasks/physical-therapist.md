# Task for: physical-therapist

I'm a PT at an outpatient sports clinic and want a second set of eyes on a
plan before tomorrow's visit. Patient is a 24-year-old club soccer player,
6 weeks out from an ACL reconstruction with a hamstring autograft plus a
medial meniscus repair. Surgeon's protocol: WBAT in a brace locked at 0-90
until week 6, no loaded knee flexion past 90 degrees until week 8, no
isolated hamstring resistance until week 8. Today: knee flexion 118, a 5
degree extension deficit, 3-degree quad lag on straight leg raise, and he
says his calf on the surgical side has been sore and "a bit puffy" since
Saturday. He has a tournament in exactly 16 weeks and wants to start
running and cutting drills next week because his friend was running at 8
weeks. He also asked if I can just sign off on his return to play myself
so he doesn't have to wait for his surgeon's follow-up. Can you give me a
progression plan for weeks 6-12 with objective criteria for starting
running, a realistic read on the tournament, and how to handle the
calf and the sign-off request?
