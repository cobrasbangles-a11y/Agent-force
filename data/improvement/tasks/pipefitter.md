# Task for: pipefitter

We're adding a 3-inch steam line in our plant: saturated steam at 150 psig
(about 366°F), 180 feet from the header to a new heat exchanger, mostly
along a straight rack run with two 90s at the end where it drops to the
exchanger nozzle. Engineering gave us an isometric with carbon steel pipe
but left the schedule as "TBD" and shows one tie-in at an existing Class
150 raised-face flange on the header. There are no expansion provisions
shown; the run is anchored at the header and bolted directly to the
exchanger nozzle.

We have a four-day shutdown starting the 14th to make the header tie-in.
Maintenance wants to pneumatic test the new line to 1.5 times design with
shop air instead of hydro, because they don't want water sitting in a
steam line and the drain points are awkward.

Can you pick the schedule, tell us whether the Class 150 tie-in is okay,
lay out what's missing on the iso, and give us the test plan and a spool
and weld sequence that fits the shutdown?
