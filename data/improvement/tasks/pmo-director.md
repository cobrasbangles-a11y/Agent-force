with 42 active projects. The latest portfolio report shows 31 green, 8

engineering directors are fighting over the same eight firmware engineers,
who are booked at about 140% across five projects. And the COO asked me to
personally take over as project manager on the ERP program since its PM
just resigned. I need a 90-day plan for the executive team in two weeks.
What should I put in it?
