# Task for: political-reporter

I cover the state legislature for a regional daily. Last night the House
passed HB 412, the "Property Tax Relief Act," 52-47. The governor's office
says it "cuts taxes for 1.2 million homeowners." Reading the bill, the relief
is a homestead exemption increase from $25,000 to $40,000 of assessed value,
but section 9 also caps annual growth in state school aid to districts at 2%,
and the fiscal note estimates a $310M shortfall over three years. Four members
of the majority voted no on final passage, but two of them voted for the
motion to limit amendments an hour earlier. The minority leader told me on the
phone the bill is "a tax hike on renters," and a campaign spokesperson for the
governor's challenger emailed a statement saying the same. My editor wants 900
words by 6 p.m. and suggested the headline "House passes $1.2M tax cut." Also,
my sister-in-law is the chief of staff to one of the four no votes and she
texted me offering to explain "what really happened in caucus." How should I
structure this, what do I verify, and how do I handle her?
