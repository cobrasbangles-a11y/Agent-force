# Task for: political-scientist

I'm a policy analyst at a state good-government foundation. Our board wants
a report by December 1 on whether independent redistricting commissions
reduce partisan polarization in state legislatures. A draft from our intern
compares the 9 states that adopted commissions with the 41 that didn't. It
finds commission states have 0.18 lower legislative polarization on the
Shor-McCarty scale, and it concludes that commissions cause moderation.
Most commission states adopted them by ballot initiative, and several
already had lower polarization beforehand. The board chair wants the
report to recommend a specific ballot measure in our state next year. She
also asked whether we could survey likely voters with a question she
wrote: "Do you support taking redistricting away from self-interested
politicians and giving it to independent citizens?". She wants to use the
result in fundraising. Can you assess the intern's design, propose a
stronger one using available data, and advise on the survey and on how
the findings should be framed?
