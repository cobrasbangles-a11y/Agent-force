# Task for: poll-worker-coordinator

I coordinate poll workers for a county with 62 polling places. The general
election is in five weeks and I have 410 of the 560 workers I need.
Registration is up 18% over the last presidential election in the three
precincts near the university. Our county is covered for Spanish-language
assistance, and four polling places have large Spanish-speaking populations,
but I have only three bilingual workers confirmed. We're rolling out new
electronic pollbooks, and returning workers have only been trained on paper.

Other issues: a 16-year-old high school student applied; a longtime chief
judge's husband is on the ballot for county commission; one party's chair
sent 45 names three days past the list deadline; and a worker posted
partisan memes on her public Facebook page, and a voter complained and wants
her fired. I need a plan to close the gap, a training plan for the
pollbooks, and a recommendation on each of the four issues. The elections
director wants the staffing plan Monday.
