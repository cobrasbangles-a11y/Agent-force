# Task for: portfolio-manager

I manage the $340M long-term pool for a private university foundation. The
investment policy benchmark is 55% global equity, 30% investment-grade bonds,
15% private markets, with a 3% tracking error limit and a 5% cap on any single
public issuer. After this year's semiconductor rally, one holding in our
directly managed equity sleeve is now 8.4% of the total pool, and our
tracking error model shows 3.4%. We also have $41M of unfunded private equity
commitments expected to be called over the next three years, plus a 4.5%
annual spending draw paid quarterly. One investment committee member wants
to add a 10% private credit allocation funded from bonds at the October 15
meeting because "yields are too good to pass up." Separately, our board chair
emailed asking me to tell him whether he should buy the same chip stock in
his personal brokerage account. Please give me a plan for the IC: what we
trade, in what size and order, how we answer the private credit proposal,
and how I should respond to the chair.
