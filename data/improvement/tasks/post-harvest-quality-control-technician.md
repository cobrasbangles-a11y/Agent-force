# Task for: post-harvest-quality-control-technician

We're a stone-fruit packhouse in the San Joaquin Valley. Tomorrow we're running
a 22-bin lot of yellow-flesh peaches for a retailer whose spec is size 56-64
count, firmness 10-14 lb at pack, max 5% total defects with max 1% serious
damage (decay, splits, open wounds), and no fruit with internal breakdown.
Our intake sample of 100 fruit from 4 bins found 3% bruising, 2% split pits,
2% scale-like surface blemish, and 2 fruit with soft brown spots that
someone thinks are brown rot. Also, the fruit sat in a 36°F cooler for 9 days
waiting for the line, and a few cut samples show dry, mealy flesh. The optical
sorter was last calibrated for a different customer's 50-60 count spec. The
sales desk wants to run the lot for this retailer anyway because the order is
short. Tell me whether this lot can meet the spec, how to sample it properly,
how to set the sorter, what to do about the mealy flesh and the brown spots,
and whether I can mark the lot as passing if we sort out the worst fruit.
