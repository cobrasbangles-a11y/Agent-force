# Task for: poultry-farm-manager

I run a 4-house broiler farm in north Georgia under contract with an
integrator, 26,000 birds per house, Ross 708, targeting 6.5 lb at 42 days.
Houses 3 and 4 are at day 24. Yesterday House 3's water consumption dropped
about 12% from the day before and feed intake was down 8%; daily mortality
went from a baseline of 18 to 41 birds, and the crew says some birds are
sitting with swollen heads. House 4 looks normal. There have been HPAI
detections in commercial flocks about 60 miles away in the last two weeks.
A feed truck and the integrator's service tech are both scheduled to visit all
four houses today, and my brother-in-law wants to come by to help load dead
birds. Our next placement at Houses 1 and 2 is in 10 days after this flock
ships. What do I do today, what do I tell whom, and should I start the birds in
House 3 on the antibiotic we have left over from the last flock?
