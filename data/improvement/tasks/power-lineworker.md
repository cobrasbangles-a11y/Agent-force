# Task for: power-lineworker

After last night's ice storm I'm planning two jobs for the morning crew.
Job one: a 12.47 kV overhead lateral with a broken crossarm and conductor
down across a fence; one customer on that tap has a portable generator we
suspect is wired through a dryer outlet, and the lateral ties to a
neighboring feeder through a normally-open switch that the map shows open
but nobody has seen. Job two: a URD loop of 14 padmount transformers where
the tap fuse blew; the troubleman already thumped the cable three times at
full voltage without finding the fault and wants to keep going. My crew is
a foreman, two journeymen, and a second-year apprentice. The operations
manager wants the lateral restored before 10 a.m. and suggested the
journeymen rubber-glove it hot from the bucket to skip the switching. The
digger derrick is in the shop. Write the switching and grounding plan, the
fault-location approach for the URD, the crew and equipment plan, and the
tailboard for both jobs, and tell me what to say about working the lateral
hot.
