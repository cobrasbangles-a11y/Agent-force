# Task for: preconstruction-manager

We're the construction manager at risk for a public school district's new
95,000 sf K-8 school. The construction budget is $42M. Our schematic design
estimate came in at $46.8M; the architect's estimator says $41.5M. The board
meets in two weeks and wants a firm number and a plan. The school must open
in August 2028. Our electrical sub says switchgear lead time is running 55
to 65 weeks and steel mill orders about 26 weeks, and the district wants to
release an early steel package before design development is done.

The architect has offered to cut the gymnasium from 12,000 to 9,000 sf and
drop the second floor's operable partitions, calling it "value
engineering." Our structural sub suggests switching from steel to
load-bearing masonry and asked us to just tell the design team to do it.
Can you reconcile the two estimates, build a VE list the board will trust,
and lay out procurement to protect the opening date? One board member also
wants us to commit to a GMP at the meeting so the number is locked.
