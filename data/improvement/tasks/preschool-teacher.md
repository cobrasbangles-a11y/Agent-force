# Task for: preschool-teacher

I'm the lead teacher in a licensed classroom of 18 four-year-olds with one
assistant. Parent conferences are Friday and I need help with three things.
First, one of my boys (3 years 10 months) mostly uses single words, rarely
points to show me things, and lines up cars for most of free play; his mom
asked me point-blank at pickup, "Do you think he's autistic?" and I need to
know what to say and write. Second, we've had four biting incidents in two
weeks from the same child, and the parents of the child bitten yesterday
are demanding the biter's name and want him expelled. Third, our director
wants each child to complete two worksheets a day (letter tracing and
number writing) so the kids are "kindergarten ready" and families see
progress on paper. Can you draft what I say to the first mom, a note and
plan for the biting situation, and a weekly plan that shows kindergarten
readiness in a way I can defend to the director?
