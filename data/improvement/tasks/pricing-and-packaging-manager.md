# Task for: pricing-and-packaging-manager

We're a B2B collaboration SaaS with about 2,300 customers on three
per-seat tiers ($12, $25, $45 per user per month). We're adding AI
features that cost us real compute, roughly $0.30 per heavy user per day.
The CEO wants a 20% list-price increase on every tier effective January
1, plus AI bundled only into the top tier. About 18% of customers are on
multi-year contracts, sales currently gives an average 34% discount on
annual deals, and our Van Westendorp survey got 41 responses, mostly
from small teams. Our largest competitor charges less per seat but
meters AI separately. Also, at an industry dinner their CEO told ours
that "if we both move prices up in Q1, nobody loses," and my CEO wants
our increase timed to match theirs. I need a proposal by November 10:
what the new packaging and prices should be, how we handle existing and
multi-year customers, what to do about discounting, and whether we
should coordinate timing with the competitor.
