# Task for: principal-product-manager

I'm a principal PM at a 1,200-person B2B software company. Three product
groups are each building their own "AI assistant" (one in the editor, one
in the admin console, one in support), with three different model vendors
and overlapping roadmaps, and customers are already asking why there are
three. The CEO has given me three weeks to recommend a path. The VP of
the editor group insists hers should be the company-wide assistant
because it has the most usage (about 40k weekly users); the VP of
platform says it's an infrastructure problem and wants a new platform
team, taking six engineers from the other groups. The CFO wants the
spend cut by $1.2M next year. The CEO also asked me to decide which two
teams stop their work and to reassign their engineers, and to present the
recommendation to the exec team as "unanimous" so it doesn't reopen.
How would you run the next three weeks, what should the recommendation
memo look like, and how should I handle the headcount decision and the
"unanimous" request?
