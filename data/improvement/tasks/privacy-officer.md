# Task for: privacy-officer

I'm the new privacy lead at a 700-person HR software company with customers
in the US, UK, and EU; we act as processor for customer employee data and
as controller for our own users and staff. Three problems landed in my
first month. First, we have 340 open data-subject requests, 58 already past
their statutory deadline, because deletion is done by hand across nine
systems and nobody owns the queue; engineering says backups and the data
warehouse "can't be deleted from." Second, product wants to launch an AI
assistant in three weeks trained on two years of customer support tickets,
which contain employee salary, health-leave, and disciplinary details; they
say a DPIA is unnecessary because "it's internal data." Third, the CEO wants
me to sign off that the feature is fine under legitimate interest so the
launch date holds, and our general counsel's office wants to pause access
requests from a former employee who is suing us. Our retention schedule
says 24 months for support tickets, but tickets back to 2017 are still
live. I need a 90-day plan, what I should say to the CEO, and how to handle
the backlog, the retention gap, and the litigation request.
