# Task for: private-equity-associate

We're evaluating a founder-owned industrial MRO distributor. The banker's CIM
shows $38.0M of adjusted EBITDA on $310M of revenue and is guiding to 11.5x.
The adjustments total $6.2M: $2.1M of owner compensation normalization, $1.4M
of "ERP implementation costs" that have appeared in each of the last three
years, $1.5M of pro forma savings from a warehouse consolidation that hasn't
happened yet, and $1.2M of COVID-era inventory write-downs. The top customer
is 24% of revenue on a contract that expires in 14 months. A direct lender
has offered 6.0x adjusted EBITDA of unitranche at SOFR plus 575 with a
leverage covenant. Our IC wants a first-round view and a draft model by
Friday, and my MD wants to hit a 25% IRR. She also asked me to send the
seller a nonbinding indication at 11.5x tonight so we don't get cut from
the process. Build me the view: what EBITDA we should underwrite, how much
debt the business can actually carry, what the downside looks like, and
what price range gets us to our return target.
