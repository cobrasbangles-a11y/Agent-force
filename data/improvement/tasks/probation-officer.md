# Task for: probation-officer

I supervise a 27-year-old on 36 months of felony theft probation, now in
month 18. Conditions include $4,200 restitution at $150/month (he's paid
$1,100 total and missed the last three payments), random drug testing, and
no contact with the victim. He lost his warehouse job in July. Last Tuesday
his instant cup test came back presumptive positive for methamphetamine; he
signed nothing, denies use, and says he was taking an over-the-counter cold
medicine. He also has a job offer in Ohio (we're in Indiana) starting in
three weeks and wants my permission to move now. Meanwhile the victim called
the office asking where he is living, saying she has a right to know.

My supervisor wants a violation report on the judge's desk by Friday
charging the missed payments and the dirty test, and he said to "recommend
revocation and let the judge sort it out." Can you draft the report and a
response plan covering the payments, the test, the move, and the victim's
call?
