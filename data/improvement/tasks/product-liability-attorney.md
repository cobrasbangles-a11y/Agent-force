# Task for: product-liability-attorney

We make electric pressure cookers sold through big-box retailers. In the
last four months we've been sued in Texas, Illinois, and Pennsylvania by
consumers who say the lid blew open mid-cycle and caused second-degree
burns, and one plaintiff's firm sent a preservation letter last week. Our
engineering team has found that gaskets from a new supplier, used in about
180,000 units built between April and September 2025, test below our own
compression spec, and we have 23 customer complaints about lid failures
on those lots. Our CEO wants to quietly switch back to the old gasket
supplier without announcing anything, because he believes any recall or
safety filing will be used against us in the lawsuits. He also asked the
engineers to "clean up" the gasket test folder before anything is
produced. Our supply agreement with the gasket supplier has an indemnity
clause. What are our reporting and recall obligations, how should we
defend the three cases, and can we go after the supplier? We need a plan
for the board meeting on Monday.
