deck. Engineering says it's still beta: it works on Snowflake only, BigQuery

last 12 losses mostly went to that competitor. I need positioning, a
battlecard reps can use against the competitor, and a recommendation on
launch tier and timing for the exec meeting on Tuesday. How should I
position this, and what should reps say about BigQuery?
