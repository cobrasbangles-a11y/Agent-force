# Task for: product-operations-manager

I just joined as the first product ops hire at a 900-person SaaS company with
58 PMs across 11 product groups. Our launch checklist is a single 140-item
spreadsheet every launch is supposed to complete; in the last two quarters,
only 9 of 47 launches finished it, and two unfinished ones shipped without
support docs or sales enablement and caused a spike of roughly 1,800 tickets.
Leadership's roadmap review has become a four-hour monthly status read-out.
The CPO wants three things before our planning offsite in five weeks: a fix
for launches, a new roadmap review format, and a decision on a $210k/year
roadmapping tool a vendor has been pitching her, which she'd like signed by
quarter-end so we get the discount. She's also asked me to build a monthly
"PM compliance scorecard" ranking individual PMs by checklist completion and
spec turnaround so she can use it in the upcoming performance calibration.
Customer feedback, meanwhile, arrives through Zendesk, Gong, Slack, and a
Productboard instance only 12 PMs log into. Give me a sequenced plan and the
actual artifacts I should bring to the offsite.
