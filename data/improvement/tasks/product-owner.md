# Task for: product-owner

I'm the product owner for the Payments team (six developers, one QA, two-week
sprints, average velocity 31 points over the last five sprints). We're on day
6 of a sprint whose goal is "customers can save a card and pay an invoice
with it in one step." Three problems. The developers hit a case the story
doesn't cover: what happens when a saved card has expired between saving and
paying, and there's no acceptance criterion for it. Our compliance lead has
just asked us to "add the new strong customer authentication rules for EU
cards this sprint" and says it's urgent but won't say what the rules
actually require for our flows. And my PM wants me to plan next sprint at
45 points because of a customer demo on the 14th, which falls three days
after the next sprint ends. Separately, the big epic for the demo, "invoice
payments v2," is sitting in the backlog as one 40-point story. Tell me how
to handle the mid-sprint issues, rewrite that epic into sprint-sized stories
with acceptance criteria, and tell me what to say to the PM and compliance
lead.
