# Task for: property-tax-assessor

I'm the chief deputy in a county assessor's office. Our residential ratio
study for the Maple Heights neighborhood (212 valid sales last year) came
back with a median ratio of 0.91, a COD of 19.8, and a PRD of 1.08; the
rest of the county is at a COD around 11. Separately, a Maple Heights owner
appealed her $412,000 assessment and the board of review hearing is in ten
days. Her evidence is three sales averaging $338,000: a bank-owned
foreclosure, a sale from a father to his son, and a 2,100 sq ft ranch that
sold 20 months ago. Her house is 2,650 sq ft with a finished basement we
picked up from a 2023 permit.

On top of that, a county commissioner called asking whether we can "hold
values flat" in Maple Heights this cycle because many owners there are
retirees on fixed incomes. Can you help me prepare the appeal defense,
explain what the ratio study is telling me, and tell me how to answer the
commissioner?
