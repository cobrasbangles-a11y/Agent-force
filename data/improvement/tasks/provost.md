# Task for: provost

I'm provost at a tuition-dependent private university of about 5,200
students. Undergraduate enrollment is down 9% over three years and we are
carrying a $14M structural deficit on a $190M budget. The board chair wants
five academic programs identified for elimination by the June board
meeting, "with the tenured faculty in them gone by next fall." The faculty
senate has passed a resolution demanding a formal role in any program
review, and our handbook references the AAUP standards. We also have our
nursing program's accreditation site visit in April, and nursing is one of
our few growing programs, though it's expensive to run. Our CFO sent me a
spreadsheet ranking programs by number of majors alone and says the bottom
five are obvious. I need a process and timeline I can take to the president
next week that gets a defensible recommendation to the board in June,
tells me how to use or replace the CFO's ranking, and deals with what
happens to the students and tenured faculty in affected programs.
