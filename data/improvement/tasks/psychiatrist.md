# Task for: psychiatrist

Consult-liaison request from the surgical floor, and they want an answer
within the hour. 34-year-old man, post-op day 3 after an open fracture
repair, on sertraline 150 mg daily for years. Since surgery he's been
getting tramadol for pain, and yesterday ID started linezolid for a wound
infection. Over the last 12 hours he's become agitated, diaphoretic,
tremulous, with temperature 38.6 C, heart rate 124, dilated pupils, and
the night nurse describes "jerking in his legs" worse in the lower
extremities. He received haloperidol 5 mg IV overnight for agitation and
the primary team wants to give more plus add scheduled lorazepam. He's
now saying he wants to leave against medical advice to "get away from
the noise." The surgical team asks three things: is this delirium or a
psychiatric problem, can we keep giving haloperidol, and can you declare
he lacks capacity so we can hold him. Please give me a consult note
draft and the recommendations I should call back to the team.
