# Task for: public-housing-program-administrator

Our housing authority has 2,300 households on the Housing Choice Voucher
waitlist and funding to issue about 85 vouchers before year end. Three
things need decisions before our board meeting in two weeks.

First, an income-matching report shows a current voucher tenant had $9,600
in wages last year she never reported; her rent portion should have been
about $240/month higher for ten months. Her case manager wants to terminate
her assistance this week. Second, a mother with a child who uses a
wheelchair found the only accessible unit near the child's school, but its
rent is 14% above our payment standard and she's asking us to cover it.
Third, a board member emailed asking us to "look at" moving a constituent,
number 1,140 on the list, into this round because her lease ends next
month.

Can you lay out how we should handle each one, what the tenant owes and our
options on the unreported income, and a defensible order for pulling the
next 85 names?
