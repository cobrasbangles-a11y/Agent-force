# Task for: public-librarian

I manage a mid-sized branch. Three things landed this week. A parents'
group filed 14 reconsideration forms at once, all on young-adult titles,
with a 300-signature petition demanding the books be off the shelf before
the library board meets on the 21st. Most forms say "see attached list"
and at least five cite passages from a website rather than the book itself.

Our materials budget for next year is being cut 15%, from $38,000 to about
$32,300, and I have to submit allocations by the end of the month. Our data
shows Spanish-language circulation up 40% over two years and adult DVD
circulation down 35%, while large print holds steady.

Also, yesterday a sheriff's deputy came to the desk asking for the checkout
history of a specific patron, saying it was for an investigation and
"it'll just save everyone time." My clerk told him to come back today. Can
you help me with the reconsiderations, the budget allocation, and what we
say when the deputy returns?
