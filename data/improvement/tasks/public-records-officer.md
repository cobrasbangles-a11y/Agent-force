# Task for: public-records-officer

I'm the records officer for a city of about 60,000. Eight business days
ago a local reporter requested (1) all emails and texts between the police
chief and any council member about the March 14 use-of-force incident, from
March 1 through August 31, and (2) the body-camera footage from the three
responding officers. Our statute gives ten business days to respond or
invoke an extension.

The chief tells me some of the texts are on his personal phone and "aren't
city records." The footage shows a 15-year-old bystander's face and a
neighbor's front door with the house number visible. The internal affairs
review closed in July, but the chief wants everything withheld under the
active-investigation exemption anyway. IT estimates 3,800 emails hit the
search terms, and I figure 40 hours of review. Our fee schedule allows $25
per hour after the first two hours.

What do I send the reporter by the deadline, how do I handle the personal
phone and the video, and what do I tell the chief?
