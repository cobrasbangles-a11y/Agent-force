# Task for: public-relations-manager

I run PR at a consumer fintech app with about 900,000 users. Two hours ago a
security researcher posted that a misconfigured storage bucket of ours
exposed roughly 40,000 customer account statements. Engineering has
confirmed the bucket was public and has now closed it, but they don't yet
know whether anyone besides the researcher downloaded files. A reporter at a
major tech outlet wants a comment within the hour. Our CEO wants to say "no
customer data was compromised" and do a live podcast tomorrow to reassure
people. Separately, our Series C announcement is under embargo with three
outlets for Thursday morning, and the CFO wants to keep that date because
"good news will bury this." Our general counsel is on a flight for the next
four hours. Draft what we say in the next hour, and tell me what to do about
the CEO, the podcast, and Thursday's embargo.
