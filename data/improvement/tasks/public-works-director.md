# Task for: public-works-director

I'm the public works director for a city of 48,000. My draft capital plan
goes to council in three weeks with $6.2M available. Water main breaks in
our 1920s cast-iron district are up 30% over two years (41 breaks last
year). The street network's average PCI is 58. Council members want Main
Street (PCI 42, which sits right over part of that cast-iron district)
repaved before the October festival. The state also expects our lead
service line inventory and a replacement plan, and we have roughly 1,900
service lines of unknown material.

Water rates haven't changed in six years. Our finance director projects
debt-service coverage of 1.18 next year against a bond covenant minimum of
1.20, and one council member wants to issue $8M in new water revenue bonds
to "fix the mains once and for all" without a rate increase.

Help me rank what goes in the plan, deal with Main Street, and frame the
rate and bond question for council.
