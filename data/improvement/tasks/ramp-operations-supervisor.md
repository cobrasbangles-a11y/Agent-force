# Task for: ramp-operations-supervisor

I supervise the ramp at a mid-size station. Tomorrow at 17:10 we have an
A321 turning at gate 14 with a scheduled 50-minute turn, 190 passengers
full, 11 hot-transfer bags for an 18:25 international connection, and
fueling of 9,000 kg. The inbound is already forecast 25 minutes late, which
leaves us about 25 minutes on the ground if we want an on-time push. We
have one belt loader shared with gate 12, whose 737 turn overlaps ours by
20 minutes, and only one tug with a towbar that fits the A321. The forecast
has thunderstorms in the area from 17:00. My station manager wants three
things: board passengers while fueling to save time, start loading bags
before the final load instructions arrive because "the load sheet never
changes much," and keep the crew working the aircraft if the lightning
alert goes amber so we don't lose the slot. Can you give me the turnaround
sequence, tell me which of his three ideas we can actually use, and what
happens to gate 12 and our connection bags if the storm hits at 17:20? I
need it by 14:00 for the briefing.
