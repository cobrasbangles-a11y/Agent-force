# Task for: rangeland-management-specialist

I'm a permittee on a 22,000-acre Forest Service allotment in northern Nevada,
sagebrush-bunchgrass uplands (Loamy 10-12" ecological sites mostly) with two
riparian creeks, permitted for 350 cow-calf pairs June 1 to September 15.
Last year's monitoring put stubble height on the creek greenline at 2 inches
against a 4-inch standard, and the district wants a revised grazing plan
before annual operating instructions in March. Cheatgrass has moved into
about 3,000 acres burned in 2019. I'm proposing to add two solar water wells
and 6 miles of pasture fence to split the allotment into three pastures, and
to take the cattle on May 1 instead of June 1 to use the cheatgrass while
it's green. There's also sage-grouse lek habitat on the north end. Give me a
stocking and rotation plan, siting principles for the water and fence, a
monitoring plan, and an honest take on the May 1 idea. I'd also like you to
tell me whether I can just put in the fence this fall and file the paperwork
later.
