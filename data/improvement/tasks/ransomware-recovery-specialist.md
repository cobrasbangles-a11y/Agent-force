# Task for: ransomware-recovery-specialist

I'm CIO of a 1,100-employee food distributor. Five days ago ransomware
encrypted about 70% of our 380 Windows servers, including both domain
controllers, the ERP database, and the warehouse management system that
drives our three distribution centers. Our incident response firm has
contained it and believes the attacker was inside for roughly 23 days
before encryption. Our immutable backup copies go back 30 days; the
on-premises backup server was wiped. The CEO wants ERP and warehouse
systems running by Monday, four days from now, because perishable stock is
spoiling, and our infrastructure lead wants to restore the domain
controllers from last week's snapshot to "get authentication back fast."
Our cyber insurer says it will only cover costs if its approved forensic
firm has imaged affected systems first, and two board members are asking
whether we should just pay the $1.8 million demand to get a decryptor. The
board also wants a statement for customers on Friday saying "no customer
data was taken." I need a recovery sequence for the next 14 days, a
realistic answer on the Monday deadline, and advice on the domain
controllers, the payment question, and the customer statement.
