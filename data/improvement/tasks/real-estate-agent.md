# Task for: real-estate-agent

I'm a buyer's agent. My clients are pre-approved for a conventional loan
up to $620,000 with 10% down, and they have about $35,000 in savings beyond
the down payment and closing costs. They love a 1972 ranch listed at
$599,000; offers are due Sunday at 6 pm and the listing agent says there
are five. The seller disclosure says "no known water issues," but photos
from the 2019 listing show ceiling stains in the basement family room. My
clients want to waive inspection and appraisal and write an escalation
clause up to $665,000. They also want to send a letter with a family photo.

The listing agent called and offered to represent my clients too if they
switch, which he says would "give them an edge." My clients also asked me
whether the neighborhood is safe and "what kind of people" live there.
Can you tell me how to structure a competitive offer that doesn't expose
them to a gap they can't cover, what to do about the water stains, how to
answer their neighborhood question, and whether the escalation clause
language their cousin found online is enforceable?
