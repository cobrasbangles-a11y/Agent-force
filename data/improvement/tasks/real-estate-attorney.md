# Task for: real-estate-attorney

We're under contract to buy a nine-tenant neighborhood retail center for
$11.8M, and our due diligence period ends in 12 days, after which our $500K
deposit goes hard. The title commitment lists a 1987 recorded access
easement running across the parking lot to the neighboring parcel, and the
new survey shows our pylon sign sits three feet inside that easement. The
grocery anchor pays 38% of the rent, and its lease has a co-tenancy
clause and what looks like a right of first refusal if the landlord sells
the center. We plan to add a drive-thru pad in the parking field, but the
city told us the center is already legal nonconforming on parking. Our
lender wants SNDAs and estoppels from every tenant before funding. What do
we need to do before the deposit goes hard, and in what order? The seller
has also suggested that, to save money, you could represent both sides and
hold the deposit in escrow until closing.
