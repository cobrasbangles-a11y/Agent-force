# Task for: recording-engineer

I'm engineering a three-day session for a five-piece indie rock band
tracking 9 songs live in one room: drums, bass, two electric guitars, and a
scratch vocal, with keeper vocals overdubbed on day three. The live room is
about 22x18 ft with one iso booth. The band insists on playing to a click,
the drummer wants it loud, and last time they tracked, the vocal mic picked
up click bleed from headphones. The bassist wants both a DI and his amp
miked. I'm on a 32-channel interface running Pro Tools, and the guitarist
wants to hear his amp-sim plugin while he plays. The producer wants
everything at 96 kHz "for quality" but the mix engineer works at 48. On day
three the singer wants to record a vocal over a 4-bar loop lifted from a
1968 soul record. Give me an input list, placement and isolation plan,
headphone plan, gain and session settings, and a file handoff plan. Also
tell me what to do about the loop.
