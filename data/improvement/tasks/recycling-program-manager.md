# Task for: recycling-program-manager

I run curbside recycling for a city of 85,000 households, single-stream,
every other week. Our MRF's last composition audit put our inbound
contamination at 24%; the contract charges $85 a ton for residue above 15%
and we send 1,900 tons a month. The contract renews in 90 days and the MRF
is proposing to switch us from a fixed $40/ton processing fee to a fee
indexed to its average commodity value. Council wants to "add all plastics
#1-7 and keep glass in the cart" to hit a 50% diversion goal by 2028; we're
at 36% by our own calculation. A new state packaging EPR law starts funding
programs next year but the reporting rules aren't final. Two routes have
missed-pickup rates triple the others since we added 1,200 homes without new
trucks. A council member also wants our mailer to say "everything you put in
the blue cart gets recycled." I need a contamination plan, advice on the
accepted-list proposal, how to evaluate the contract offer, the route fix,
and what to say about the mailer, before the council work session in three
weeks.
