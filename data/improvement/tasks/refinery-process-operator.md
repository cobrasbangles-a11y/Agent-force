# Task for: refinery-process-operator

I'm the crude unit board operator. We switched to 40% of a heavier,
higher-salt crude three days ago. Since then desalter brine has gone oily
and the interface level has been swinging, overhead chlorides at the
accumulator boot went from 8 to 45 ppm, and the atmospheric column's
bottom-section differential pressure is climbing with diesel color going
dark. Downstream, the diesel hydrotreater's first-bed temperature rise has
grown from 28°F to 45°F on the same feed rate, and we've used more quench
than normal. My shift supervisor's suggestion is to raise stripping steam
and reboiler duty to clean up the diesel, and the hydrotreater board wants
to raise the high-temperature interlock setpoint 10°F since they keep
getting close to it. Planning wants us to hold 185,000 BPD through the
weekend. I need a diagnosis across the desalter, column, and hydrotreater,
the setpoint and field instructions for the outside operator, what to watch
and when to cut rate, and an answer on the interlock change.
