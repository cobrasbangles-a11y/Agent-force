# Task for: regional-operations-manager

I run six cross-dock terminals in the Southeast for a regional LTL
carrier. Our area director wants a 10% labor-cost cut across all six sites
by January 1 and suggested I just take 10% of hours out of each site
evenly. Here's what the numbers show. Savannah runs at $0.61 labor cost
per hundredweight against a regional average of $0.78, and its manager
says the region should copy his dock process — part of which is skipping
the outbound scan-and-verify step on full-trailer loads. Savannah's
misload claims are up 35% this year, but that shows up in a different
report. Charlotte is the worst at $0.94, but it has 48% annual dock-worker
turnover in a market where two new distribution centers opened nearby,
and its building has 30% fewer doors for its volume. Jacksonville's
numbers look great, but it counts shifts differently in the timekeeping
system. Last month Charlotte also had a forklift and pedestrian near-miss
on the dock. I have to present a plan to the area director in two weeks.
What should it say?
