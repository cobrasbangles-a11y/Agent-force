# Task for: registered-architect

A client bought a two-story 1968 office building, about 9,800 sq ft per
floor, wood-framed, unsprinklered, one interior stair plus one exterior
steel stair, no elevator. They want a 180-seat restaurant with a bar on
the ground floor (roughly 5,200 sq ft of dining) and a coworking space on
the second floor, opening in March. Their contractor told them "it's
already a commercial building so there's no change of use" and that the
existing stairs and restrooms are fine. The client asks three things: can
we avoid adding sprinklers, do we need an elevator for the second floor,
and can I just stamp the contractor's layout so they can pull permits by
November 15? The jurisdiction adopted a recent edition of the model
building and existing building codes with local amendments I haven't
pulled yet. Give me the code analysis I should run first, where the
likely showstoppers are, a realistic document and permit plan for the
March date, and how to respond to the client on the stamp request.
