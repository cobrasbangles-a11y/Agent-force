# Task for: registrar

Commencement is in three weeks and I have four problems on my desk. One: a
graduating senior's degree audit shows 118 of 120 credits because a
transfer course from a community college (Intro to Statistics, 3 credits)
posted as a general elective instead of satisfying our Quantitative
Reasoning requirement; she says her advisor told her it would count. Two:
the athletic director emailed asking me to change a student-athlete's F in
a fall course to a W so he stays eligible, and the instructor hasn't been
contacted. Three: the mother of a 20-year-old student called demanding his
grades, saying she pays his tuition and claims him as a dependent. Four: an
alumnus with a $2,300 unpaid balance needs an official transcript for a job
offer, and our policy has always been to withhold transcripts until
balances are paid. We also received a subpoena from a law firm for a
current student's records. Tell me how to handle each one, in what order,
and what I should document.
