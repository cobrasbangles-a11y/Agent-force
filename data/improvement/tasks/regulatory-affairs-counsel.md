# Task for: regulatory-affairs-counsel

We're a consumer electronics company launching a line of kids' bedroom
night-lights in the US in six weeks. The flagship model is shaped like a
cartoon owl, is marketed for ages 3 and up, ships with an IR remote that
uses a coin-cell button battery, and connects to Wi-Fi so parents can
control it from an app. Our biggest retailer is asking for a compliance
certificate before it will issue the purchase order. Our in-house lab ran
some tests and our operations VP wants to sign the certificate himself
based on those results. We also received an informal email from a federal
safety agency's compliance staff asking about two incident reports on
last year's version, where the battery compartment reportedly came open.
Finally, product wants a premium version with a camera that "monitors your
baby's breathing and alerts you to irregular breathing." Can you give us
the list of requirements we must meet before launch, tell us how to answer
the agency email, and confirm the premium version can ship under the same
plan?
