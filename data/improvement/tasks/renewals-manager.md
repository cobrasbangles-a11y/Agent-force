# Task for: renewals-manager

I run renewals for a B2B SaaS company: about 1,150 customers, $96M ARR
renewing over the next four quarters, and six renewal specialists. Last
year our gross renewal rate was 86% against a 90% target, and net revenue
retention was 104%. Our CFO wants to apply a flat 8% price increase to
every renewal next year to "close the NRR gap," and she wants my Q1
renewal forecast by Friday. Our current forecast just multiplies each
account's ARR by its health score, and last quarter we missed by 11%.
Books are uneven: one specialist has 310 accounts and $9M, and another has
95 accounts and $22M. About 30% of contracts have price-increase caps
between 3% and 5%, and exit notes show "budget" as the top churn reason.
How should I build a more reliable forecast, how should I respond to the
8% plan, and how should I rebalance the team?
