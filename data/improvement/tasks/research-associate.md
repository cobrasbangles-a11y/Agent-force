normalized to GAPDH. The qPCR used a single reference gene we never checked
for stability under knockdown.

The PI wants the figure, a statistical comparison, and a draft methods
section by Friday for a manuscript going to a journal that requires full
uncropped blots and reagent identifiers. Please tell me what the data
actually support right now, what to redo and in what order in the time we
