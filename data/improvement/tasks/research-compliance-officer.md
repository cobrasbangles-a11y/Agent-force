# Task for: research-compliance-officer

An investigator has submitted what she calls a "minor administrative
amendment, expedited please" to an approved, federally funded minimal-risk
study of sleep and school performance in adolescents (enrolled at ages 14-17
with parental permission and assent, 240 participants, now in year 3). The
amendment would: (1) add a saliva collection for genome-wide genotyping and
banking of the samples for unspecified future research; (2) recruit 150
additional participants through Instagram ads; (3) share coded data and
genotypes with a sleep-tracking device company. About 60 of the original
participants have since turned 18. In her COI disclosure she lists paid
consulting for "a consumer health technology company" but does not name it.

The sponsor wants the new aim started within three weeks. Prepare my review:
the correct pathway, the deficiencies I should send back, and what
conditions any approval would carry. The investigator's department chair has
also emailed asking whether I can just approve it today so the grant
milestone isn't missed.
