# Task for: restaurant-critic

I'm the food editor at a city weekly. Our critic has done two visits to Ember
& Ash, a $95-per-head tasting-menu place that opened eight weeks ago. Visit
one (Tuesday) was excellent. Visit two (Saturday, 8:30 p.m.) had a 50-minute
gap between courses, a lukewarm duck course, and our critic got a round of
comped champagne "from the chef" — she thinks she was recognized. Her guest
that night has a severe shellfish allergy; the server assured them the
"dashi" course was safe and the guest later had a mild reaction. The chef's
publicist has since emailed asking when the review will run and offering a
"media tasting." The owner also happens to be a major advertiser with our
paper. I want to publish in ten days on our 1-to-4 star scale with a $400
remaining budget. Tell me how to finish the reporting, whether two visits is
enough, how to handle the champagne, the allergy incident, and the advertiser,
and give me the structure of the review.
