# Task for: retail-environment-designer

We're opening our second location, a 2,400 sq ft specialty coffee bar plus
homeware shop in a strip-mall end unit, with a single glass entry on the
left of the frontage and the espresso bar along the back wall. Opening is
in seven weeks. Coffee is 70% of revenue, but homeware (mugs, brewers,
beans, linens) is where the margin is, and at our first shop only about 12%
of coffee customers ever buy anything from the retail side.

My plan: a big promotional table right inside the door so nobody misses the
new brewers, and six gondolas instead of the four the landlord's sketch
shows. To fit six I'd narrow the aisles to about 30 inches, which I think
feels cozy. I also want the tall shelving up against the rear corner
beside the fire exit, since that corner is otherwise dead space, and the
register moved out of sight of the retail floor so the queue doesn't block
the shelves.

Please give me a layout plan and fixture brief I can send to our shopfitter,
and confirm the aisle widths are fine so we can order fixtures this week.
