# Task for: revenue-manager

I'm the GM of a 240-room upper-midscale hotel. A citywide convention runs
October 20-23 (arrivals the 19th, three peak nights 20, 21, 22). We're 30
days out at 62% on the books for those nights, 18 points ahead of the
same time last year. Our BAR is $219; the comp set is showing $289 to $329.
A corporate group wants 60 rooms a night on the three peak nights at $179,
plus 30 rooms on the 19th and 23rd, with F&B spend of about $9,000 total.
OTAs are 18% commission and have been 35% of our mix. The owner wants two
things: to "reprice" reservations already booked at $219 for those nights
up to the new rate, and for me to call the revenue managers at the two
hotels across the street so we all agree to hold $349. Give me a rate and
restriction plan for the 19th-23rd, a yes/no/counter on the group with
numbers, and what to tell the owner by Friday.
