# Task for: roofer

Two-story house built in 1998. There's a brown stain on the first-floor
living room ceiling, about 3 feet out from the exterior wall, directly
under the spot where the lower porch roof meets the second-story siding.
It shows up after wind-driven rain, not every rain. The main roof is
architectural shingles at 6:12, 17 years old; the back porch roof is also
shingled but only about 2:12, and it has some curled tabs and a soft spot
near the gutter.

We got a quote for $18,400 to replace everything. Another contractor said
he could lay new shingles right over the old ones for about half that and
be done before Thanksgiving. An insurance adjuster is coming next week, and
a neighbor told us to have the roofer write it up as hail damage so the
policy pays for the new roof.

What is most likely causing the leak, does the whole roof really need
replacing, can we lay over, and what should the scope and write-up look
like?
