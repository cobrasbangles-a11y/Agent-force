# Task for: route-optimization-analyst

We run 14 delivery vehicles out of one depot: 10 cargo vans (about 3,500 lb
payload, 450 cubic feet) and 4 box trucks at 26,000 lb GVWR with a 13'6"
body height. Daily volume is about 420 stops, 80 of them with commercial
receiving windows (mostly 7-10am), and we have a new grocery customer
starting next month adding 35 stops that need a refrigerated box truck and
must be delivered before 9am. Our ops director wants the new plan to run on
12 vehicles instead of 14, using the 2-minute average service time from
last year's sales deck and drive times from a consumer maps app, and he
suggested we widen some of the receiving windows "by an hour, nobody
checks." Drivers work 10-hour shifts and the box-truck drivers keep paper
logs. There are two low-clearance rail underpasses (12'4") near our
biggest cluster. Can you tell me by Friday whether 12 vehicles is realistic,
what the route set should look like, and what data I need to pull before we
commit to anything with the grocery customer?
