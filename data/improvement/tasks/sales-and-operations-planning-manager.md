# Task for: sales-and-operations-planning-manager

I run S&OP for a household cleaning products maker with three product
families. For Q4, sales has put in a forecast 18% above last year for the
liquid detergent family because of a big-box promotion. Our 6-month
forecast MAPE for that family is 14%, which everyone says is fine, but
actuals have come in below forecast in all six months, by an average of
11%. Supply says the plan fits because the plant is only at 82%
utilization overall, but the liquid filling line, which the detergent
family shares with fabric softener, would need 118% of its available
hours in November. A co-packer can take 20% of the volume at $0.38 more
per unit, with 8 weeks' lead time to start. The sales VP wants the
forecast locked and published Friday without an executive meeting because
"we already agreed," and the CFO wants the plan to show we hit the annual
operating plan revenue number. Our frozen horizon is 6 weeks. Can you put
together this month's S&OP package and tell me how to handle the Friday
deadline?
