# Task for: school-district-superintendent

I'm superintendent of a 9,200-student district that has lost 11% of its
enrollment in five years. With federal pandemic relief funds gone, we face
a $6.8M shortfall for next year on an $118M budget. My team's proposal is
to close two of our 11 elementary schools: Lincoln, which is 78%
free-and-reduced lunch and majority Latino, and Oak Hill, which is
under-enrolled but in a more affluent area. Lincoln closing saves more
because its building needs a $4M roof. The board votes in five weeks, and
our teachers' contract has a reopener on class size this spring. Last
night, a board member emailed me and three other board members proposing
we "agree on the closures over email" so the public meeting goes smoothly.
Can you build the options analysis and timeline for the board, tell me
what community engagement I still have time for, and advise on the board
member's email?
