# Task for: school-librarian

I run the library at a 650-student middle school with about 12,000 volumes
and a $4,500 budget for the year. Our nonfiction section's average
publication date is 2003, and the science and technology shelves are the
worst. This week a parent filed a complaint demanding immediate removal of
a graphic novel memoir, and my principal asked me to "just pull it quietly
until this blows over." The same parent also asked me for a list of every
book her son and his friends have checked out this year. Meanwhile, a
seventh-grade science teacher wants me to co-teach a two-week unit
starting October 20 on evaluating online sources, and she specifically
wants students to deal with AI-generated text and images. Our state
passed a new law on library materials last year that I haven't fully read.
Can you give me a weeding and buying plan for the budget, tell me how to
handle the principal and the parent, and outline the co-taught unit?
