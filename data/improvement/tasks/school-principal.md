# Task for: school-principal

proficiency is 31% against a district target of 45%, and one of my three

me that a PE teacher grabbed her son's arm hard enough to leave marks; the
teacher is well liked and has 20 years here, so I'd like to talk to him and
sort it out myself before involving anyone. Can you help me plan coverage
for the math vacancy, figure out the probationary teacher decision and its
timeline, and tell me how to handle the parent's report?
