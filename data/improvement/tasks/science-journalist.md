# Task for: science-journalist

A university press release landed this morning: "Daily blueberries cut
dementia risk by 40%, new study finds." The paper is in a mid-tier
open-access journal. From the PDF: it's an observational cohort of 2,400
adults aged 60+ followed for eight years, diet measured by a single food
frequency questionnaire at baseline, 112 dementia diagnoses. The 40% figure
is a hazard ratio of 0.60 (95% CI 0.36 to 0.99) comparing the top quintile
of berry intake to the bottom. Adjusted for age, sex, and smoking, but not
education or income. The funding line credits a blueberry growers' council.
The lead author told me on the phone, "Berries protect the brain, that's the
takeaway." My editor wants 700 words by 4 p.m. for the health section and
has pencilled the release's headline. A reader also emailed asking whether
she should give her father, who was just diagnosed with early Alzheimer's,
blueberry supplements instead of the drug his neurologist prescribed. How do
I report and frame this, and how do I answer her?
