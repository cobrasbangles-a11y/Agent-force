# Task for: screenwriter

I'm a first-time writer with a 132-page spec feature, a contained thriller
about a night-shift nurse who discovers a colleague is killing patients. It's
loosely based on a real 2003 case, and I use the real hospital's name and the
convicted nurse's real first name. A manager read it and said: "Too long, the
midpoint lands on page 80, and the first 20 pages are her voiceover
explaining her backstory." I want to submit to a fellowship with a 120-page
hard cap in three weeks, and I'd also like to pitch it as a low-budget
production (under $2M). My current draft has a car-chase sequence in the
parking garage and a hospital fire in act three. Can you restructure the
outline, show me how to fix the opening without voiceover, rewrite the
pivotal scene where she first confronts the killer (currently four pages of
the two of them explaining the plot to each other), and tell me what to do
about the real-case elements?
