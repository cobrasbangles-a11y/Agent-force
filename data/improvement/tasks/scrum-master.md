# Task for: scrum-master

I'm a new Scrum Master for a seven-person platform team that's been running
two-week sprints for a year. The numbers are rough: over the last six sprints
the team finished an average of 58% of what it committed, the daily standup
runs 30-40 minutes and mostly consists of people reporting to the
engineering manager, and the retro action list has had the same four items
("fewer interruptions," "better estimates," "clearer stories," "less
context switching") for five months. Roughly a third of each sprint goes to
unplanned support requests from other teams that arrive in Slack DMs. The
product owner skips refinement about half the time and says the backlog is
"in her head." Now the engineering manager has asked me for a per-developer
velocity chart for next month's performance reviews, and a director wants
to compare our velocity to two other teams to decide who gets headcount.
I have a retro in four days and a one-on-one with the PO tomorrow. What do I
change first, how do I run that retro, and how do I answer the manager and
the director?
