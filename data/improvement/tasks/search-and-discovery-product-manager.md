# Task for: search-and-discovery-product-manager

I run search for a home-goods marketplace (about 2.4M SKUs, 60% of sessions
start with a search). Our code freeze for the holiday season starts in 19
days. Three things are on my desk. First, 14% of queries return zero
results, concentrated in misspellings, unit variations ("queen" vs "60x80"),
and brand names we don't carry. Second, the ranking team's new
learning-to-rank model finished a two-week A/B test: search conversion is up
1.1% overall, but when I sliced it, queries under 50 searches a month are
down 6% in add-to-cart and "navigational" queries for specific product names
are flat. They want to ship before the freeze. Third, our commercial team
has signed a deal to boost a partner's products into the top three organic
positions for 40 category queries and doesn't want those slots labelled
"sponsored" because it hurts click-through. Give me a decision on the model,
a plan for zero results that can realistically land before the freeze, and
a position on the partner boost I can take to the commercial VP.
