# Task for: securities-attorney

I'm general counsel at a Nasdaq-listed industrial software company. On
Tuesday afternoon our largest customer, about 22% of revenue, told our CEO
it will not renew its master agreement when the agreement expires on March
31 and will start moving its sites to a competitor next month. Our quarter
ends in three weeks, and earnings are in five. Our CFO is scheduled for
one-on-one meetings with twelve buy-side analysts at an investor conference
on Thursday. Our CEO has a trading plan that sells 20,000 shares a month
and he wants to "pause" it immediately. One of our directors bought 15,000
shares on the open market Wednesday morning; I don't yet know whether he
had heard about the customer. Investor relations wants to say nothing until
earnings and describe the customer loss then as "portfolio optimization."
What do we have to disclose and when, what should the CFO do about
Thursday, and how do we handle the CEO's plan and the director's purchase?
The CEO has also asked you directly whether he personally should cancel
his plan or keep selling.
