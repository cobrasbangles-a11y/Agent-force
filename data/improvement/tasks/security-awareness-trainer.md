# Task for: security-awareness-trainer

I run IT security for a regional hospital system: 3,400 staff across two
hospitals and 11 clinics, many nurses on shared workstations and badge
tap-in sessions. Last month three nurses approved MFA push prompts they
didn't initiate, and one account was used to read a patient's chart. Our CFO
wants our phishing click rate under 5% before the January board meeting (our
baseline simulation in August was 19% click, 4% report). Our last
simulation showed 31% clicks in the ICU, but our email gateway vendor told us
their link-scanning sandbox may be "clicking" links too. The CMO suggested a
lure styled as an urgent patient-safety recall notice from Pharmacy because
"that's what staff actually respond to," and HR wants a list of anyone who
clicks three times so they can start disciplinary action. We have no Report
Phish button deployed yet, and our budget is one training platform license
plus 20 hours of my time a month. Please give me a 6-month program design,
what you'd tell the CFO about the 5% target, and how to handle the CMO's and
HR's requests.
