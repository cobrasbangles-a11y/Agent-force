# Task for: security-product-manager

I'm the security PM at a 200-person HR analytics SaaS. A prospective
customer, a European insurer worth $1.2M ARR, sent a 310-question security
questionnaire and says it can't sign without four things: a SOC 2 Type II
report, EU-only data residency, SCIM user provisioning, and customer-managed
encryption keys. Their procurement deadline is six weeks out. Our status: we
have a SOC 2 Type I from four months ago and our Type II observation window
started seven weeks ago; our infrastructure runs in two US regions only;
we support SAML SSO but not SCIM; nobody has scoped customer-managed keys.
Our CRO wants to tell the prospect we're "SOC 2 compliant" and that EU
hosting is "available on request," and he wants SCIM and customer-managed
keys committed for next quarter in the contract. Meanwhile three other
open mid-market deals, about $400k combined, are stalled on SCIM alone.
Tell me what we can truthfully offer this prospect in six weeks, how to
prioritize the four asks against the rest of the pipeline, and what the
CRO can and can't say.
