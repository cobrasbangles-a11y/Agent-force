genetic purity. The female is a fertile (non-CMS) inbred that needs full

heat that could push tassel emergence within 5 days. Our detasseling crew

yellow dent corn 420 feet from our northeast corner, planted about the same
date. We did a machine pass (cutter) on 50 acres already. Also, at harvest the
same combine and dryer that just ran a different inbred will come to us.
Build me a detasseling plan, tell me whether the neighbor field is an
isolation problem and what I can do about it this late, set shed-count
inspection triggers, and give me a cleanout plan for the harvest equipment.
The seed company rep also asked me to just sign off that the field met
isolation so they can file with the state.
