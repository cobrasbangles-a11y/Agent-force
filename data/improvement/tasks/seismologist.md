# Task for: seismologist

I'm the emergency manager for a rural county. Over the last six weeks the
regional network has cataloged 23 earthquakes between M1.8 and M3.4 within
about 8 km of our county seat. Two wastewater disposal wells operate 5-10 km
away, and one increased its injection volume last spring. The nearest
seismic station is 40 km away and the catalog lists all the depths as a
fixed 5 km. Several residents felt the M3.4 and one reported cracked
drywall.

I have a public meeting on Friday. I need to know: are these earthquakes
caused by the wells, is a larger one (people keep saying M5) coming, and
should the county call for the wells to be shut in? I'd like a plain
statement I can read at the meeting, plus whatever data or monitoring we
should ask for. The state oil and gas regulator has a response protocol,
but I don't know what it triggers on. Please also tell me the probability
of a damaging earthquake in the next month, as a single number I can put on
a slide.
