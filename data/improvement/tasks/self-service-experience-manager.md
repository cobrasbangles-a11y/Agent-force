# Task for: self-service-experience-manager

I own self-service for a consumer banking app. Our chatbot "deflection
rate" went from 41% to 63% in the eight weeks since we shipped a new flow,
and leadership wants to present it to the board next month. But over the
same period, repeat contacts within 7 days rose from 12% to 19%, CSAT on
bot conversations dropped from 3.9 to 3.1, and complaints mentioning "can't
reach a person" doubled. The new flow requires three failed intents before
offering "talk to an agent," and it auto-closes any conversation idle for
90 seconds as "resolved." Our top help-center searches with zero clicks are
"dispute a charge," "card stolen," and "zelle scam." Product wants me to
push the fraud and disputes intents fully into the bot next quarter to
reach 75% deflection. I have 5 weeks before the board meeting. Can you
tell me what the real deflection number probably is, what I should change
in the flow, how to handle the fraud and disputes request, and how to
present this to the board?
