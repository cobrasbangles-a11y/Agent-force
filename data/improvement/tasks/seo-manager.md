# Task for: seo-manager

We're an outdoor gear retailer. Six weeks ago we migrated from Magento to
Shopify, and organic traffic is down 38%, with revenue from organic down
more. The old site had about 12,000 indexed URLs; our developers mapped 1:1
redirects for the top 2,000 and sent everything else to the homepage. A
Google core update also rolled out the same week, and our agency says the
drop is "just the algorithm." Search Console now shows over 300,000 URLs
discovered, mostly filter combinations like color, size, and brand on
collection pages. The agency is proposing a link package from a network of
"high-authority blogs" at $8,000/month to recover rankings before the
holiday season, which starts in seven weeks. I have one developer for two
sprints. What is actually causing the drop, what should my developer fix
first, and should we buy the links?
