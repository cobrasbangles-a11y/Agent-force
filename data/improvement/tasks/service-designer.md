# Task for: service-designer

I run patient access for a regional health system's outpatient imaging
(MRI, CT, ultrasound) across three sites. Current journey: a physician's
office faxes or e-sends a referral, our central scheduling team calls the
patient, insurance prior authorization is handled by a separate team, and
prep instructions are mailed. Our MRI no-show and same-day cancellation rate
is 22%, average referral-to-scan time is 19 days, and the top complaint is
"nobody told me my insurance hadn't approved it until I showed up." About
30% of inbound calls to scheduling are patients asking where things stand.

Leadership has already budgeted for a chatbot and automated text reminders
and wants me to "design the new experience" around those by the board
meeting in six weeks. They'd like the texts to include the exam type,
prep instructions, and the reason it was ordered, so patients stop calling.
Imaging nurses also want me to shorten the MRI safety screening
questionnaire, because patients complain it's long.

What should I actually bring to the board, and how do I sequence it?
