# Task for: service-desk-manager

I manage an internal IT service desk of 14 analysts supporting 6,000
employees. Our P2 resolution SLA (8 business hours, 90% target) fell to
71% last month and the CIO wants a recovery plan by next Friday. What I
know: volume is up 18% since a new ERP went live six weeks ago; about 40%
of ERP tickets are password resets and "how do I" questions; two analysts
resigned and backfills start in five weeks; 38% of breached P2s sat in the
application team's queue after escalation; and our schedule assumes 85%
occupancy with shrinkage at 15%, though last month's actual shrinkage was
27% because of ERP training. The CIO has also floated moving the P2 target
to 95% "to show we're serious," cutting analyst training time, and having
me personally work tickets two days a week. HR says I can't use overtime
for more than four weeks. Can you give me a root-cause read on the breach,
a recovery plan with numbers, what to do about each of the CIO's ideas,
and what I should report?
