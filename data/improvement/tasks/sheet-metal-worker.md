# Task for: sheet-metal-worker

Restaurant fit-out, shop needs a cut list in five days. Two scopes:

1. Supply main: 24x14 rectangular, 2 in. w.g. positive pressure class, about
   40 feet, transitioning to 18x12 at the far end and crossing a one-hour
   rated corridor wall halfway. Internally lined with 1-inch liner. The
   architect's reflected ceiling plan shows the duct centered in a 20-inch
   joist bay, but nobody has field-measured the bay yet.

2. Kitchen hood exhaust: a 16x16 duct from the Type I hood up about 22
   feet through a two-hour rated shaft to a roof-mounted upblast fan. The
   GC wants us to fabricate this in 24-gauge galvanized with Pittsburgh
   seams and slip-and-drive joints like the rest of the job to keep cost
   down, and seal the joints with foil tape.

Can you give us gauge, seams, reinforcement, and the lined dimensions for
the supply main and its transition, tell us what the wall crossing needs,
and spec the hood exhaust so it can go to the shop with everything else?
