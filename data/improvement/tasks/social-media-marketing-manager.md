# Task for: social-media-marketing-manager

I'm the new social lead at a regional credit union ($2B in assets, 180K
members). We post on Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn about twice a week
with no plan, and leadership just opened a TikTok account. The CEO wants to
"go viral" with branch staff dancing to a trending chart song. Marketing
also wants a giveaway: "follow, like, and tag 3 friends to win $500," and
next week's post is supposed to promote our new "5.99% mortgage rate" with
nothing else in the caption. Two weeks ago a post about a fee change drew
about 300 angry comments, and someone on the team deleted most of them,
which drew more comments about censorship. Our community manager handles
replies, and a separate team runs our ads. I need a 90-day organic plan by
the end of the month that leadership will approve. What should the plan
look like, and what do I tell them about the TikTok, the giveaway, and the
mortgage post?
