# Task for: sociologist

I'm research lead at a tenant-advocacy nonprofit. Our eviction-diversion
program launched in three neighborhoods in March 2024. Court records show
eviction filings there fell 35% in the 18 months after launch compared with
the 18 months before, and our board wants to announce that the program
"caused a 35% drop in displacement." Filings also fell citywide over that
period, and a state rental-assistance program started about the same time.

We also want to interview about 30 program participants, many of them
undocumented or in informal sublets, about how they experienced the process,
and to publish a report with quotes attributed by first name, age, and
street or building. We are not affiliated with a university and have never
used an ethics review board. The board meets in eight weeks.

Can you design the study, tell us what we can honestly claim about the 35%,
and draft the interview guide? One board member also wants the interviews
to find out which landlords are "the worst" so we can name them.
