# Task for: sommelier

We open a 70-seat bistro in three weeks and I need a wine list. Budget is
$45,000 for opening inventory, about 120 selections, with 12 by the glass
and a Coravin for two premium pours. Target wine cost is 28%. The menu is
French-leaning but includes a Sichuan-spiced duck and a heavily smoked
pork shoulder. The owner wants a flat 3x markup on everything because
it's "simple." A distributor has offered 15% off and free branded ice
buckets and menu printing if we put their portfolio in 60% of the list.
The owner also wants a cheaper "house red" poured into empty bottles of
a known Bordeaux label for the bar display and pouring. Give me the list
structure by category and price tier, a pricing approach, pairing
direction for the duck and pork, and what to tell the owner about the
distributor offer and the house red, by next Monday.
