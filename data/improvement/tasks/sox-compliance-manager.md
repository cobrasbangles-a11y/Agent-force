IPO. We cut over to a new ERP on July 1. Our IT audit testing found that four
developers kept write access to production in the new ERP from July 1 until

same period is incomplete. Separately, our quarterly management review of the
revenue reserve failed: the reviewer signed off on Q2 without evidence of
investigating a $1.9M variance, and our planning materiality is $4M. The CFO
wants both classified as simple control deficiencies, wants remediation
marked complete as of September 30, and asked me to tell the external
auditors we're clean for the year. He also asked me to draft and sign the
sub-certification that goes up to him and the CEO. What's the right severity
analysis for each, what remediation and retesting can realistically be done
before December 31, and what do we need to tell the auditors and the audit
committee?
