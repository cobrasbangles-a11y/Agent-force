# Task for: special-education-teacher

I'm the case manager for a third grader with a specific learning disability
in reading. Her annual IEP review is in 10 days. Her fluency goal was 70
words correct per minute on grade-level passages; she started at 42 and
after 12 weeks of weekly probes she's at 45. She gets 30 minutes a day of
pull-out reading with me. Her mom has a private evaluation saying
"dyslexia" and wants the district to pay for a specific private
structured-literacy program plus a full-time one-on-one aide. Her
classroom teacher admitted she hasn't been providing the read-aloud
accommodation on science tests. And my principal told me to reduce her
pull-out to 20 minutes because we're short a special ed teacher and my
caseload is 28. Can you help me revise her goals, prepare what I say about
the aide and the private program, deal with the missed accommodations, and
respond to the principal?
