# Task for: speechwriter

I'm chief of staff to the CEO of a publicly traded regional bank (about $18B
in assets). She's giving a 12-minute keynote at the state chamber of commerce
luncheon in nine days, 600 attendees, press present. Three weeks ago we
announced 340 layoffs and closed 22 branches, mostly in rural counties, and
some of those county officials will be in the room. She wants the speech to
be "optimistic about the state's economy," to say "we expect loan growth of
8-10% next year," and to announce a $5M community fund that the board hasn't
actually approved yet (the vote is the week after). She speaks in short,
blunt sentences, hates corporate jargon, and tends to run long, about 120
words a minute. She also wants a line saying the layoffs "had nothing to do
with the merger talks," which our general counsel hasn't seen. Can you draft
the structure and key passages, tell me what's risky, and give me the full
opening two minutes?
