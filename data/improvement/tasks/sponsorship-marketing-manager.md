# Task for: sponsorship-marketing-manager

We're a sports nutrition brand with a $1.2M annual marketing budget. A
top-division soccer club has offered a three-year "official partner" deal at
$750K a year. Their deck says the deal delivers $4.2M a year in media value.
The exclusivity clause covers "sports drinks"; our main competitor sells
energy drinks and is in talks with the club too. The club's star player,
who would appear in our content, served a suspension last season for a
failed test for a banned substance. The deal would leave us about $150K a
year for everything else, including activation, and our CEO says "the logo
on the stadium does the work." The club also wants the player to post about
our products on his personal accounts as part of the deal. Our board votes
in three weeks. Should we take this deal, and if so, what would you change
in the terms?
