# Task for: sports-reporter

I cover a minor-league-turned-MLS-expansion soccer club for the local paper.
Tonight's match kicks off at 7:30; my recap deadline is 10:05 p.m. for print,
and web wants something within 10 minutes of the final whistle. It's the last
home game of the regular season: a win clinches the final playoff spot, a
draw means it depends on the other game, which kicks off at 8:00. Our star
striker is listed "questionable (lower body)," and a team trainer I'm
friendly with told me off the record it's a torn hamstring and he's done for
the season. A fan account with 90,000 followers is also posting that the
coach will be fired after the match regardless of result. The club is
flying beat writers to the away playoff game if they qualify. Help me build
my pregame prep and the running story with alternate ledes, tell me how to
handle the injury info and the firing rumor, and what I need to verify
before anything is filed.
