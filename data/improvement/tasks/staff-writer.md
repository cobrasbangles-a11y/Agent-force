# Task for: staff-writer

I'm a staff writer at a digital outlet and my editor gave me four weeks to
report a 4,000-word feature on the boom in "ghost kitchens" in our metro
area. Everyone has done the "delivery-only restaurants are weird" story. I
have one strong lead: a former employee of a ghost kitchen operator (about 30
virtual brands out of two warehouses) who says the company runs the same
frozen food under a dozen different brand names and that two kitchens failed
health inspections last year. She'll only talk if she isn't named. I also
found state inspection records, but they list the operator's LLC, not the
brand names. My editor wants the pitch memo Monday and asked whether I can
"lead with the Ponzi-style angle" since investors pulled out last month. I
also know the operator's head of marketing socially, we were in the same
MFA program. Help me sharpen the pitch, build the reporting plan and
timeline, and figure out what I can actually say.
