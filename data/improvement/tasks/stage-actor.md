# Task for: stage-actor

I've been cast as Hotspur in a regional production of Henry IV, Part 1. We
have four weeks of rehearsal, the director wants us off-book by the end of
week two (10 rehearsal days from now), and I have about 420 lines. The
director's early note is "Hotspur is all rage, play him hot from the first
line," but in 1.3 he spends a long speech defending himself to the king and
I think the rage has to build. The production is using a cut script and I
keep wanting to paraphrase the verse to make it sound natural. There's a
sword fight with Hal in 5.4 and a scene with Lady Percy in 2.3 where the
director said "just wrestle her onto the bed, you two figure it out." I
also have a slight lisp that shows up on fast lines. Help me break down
1.3 and 2.3, build a memorization plan to hit the deadline, and tell me how
to handle the director's note and the staging in 2.3.
