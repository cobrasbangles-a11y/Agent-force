# Task for: stage-manager

I'm the production stage manager for a mid-size musical at an Equity
theater, now in our second week of performances. At tonight's matinee the
actor playing Nell rolled her ankle on the stairs unit during the Act 1
finale and finished the show, but she's out tonight. Her understudy, Priya,
has had one put-in rehearsal and has never done the Act 2 flying chandelier
sequence, where Nell crosses under the chandelier as it lands on cue 214.
We have 3 hours until half hour. Our ASM says we could skip the understudy
safety walk to save time. I also need to file the injury, notify the house
that the stairs unit may be loose, and my director wants to restage the
chandelier cross tonight so Priya enters from the other side. Help me build
the plan for the next 3 hours, the put-in priorities, the paperwork, the
calling changes for cues 205 through 220, and what I say to the director.
