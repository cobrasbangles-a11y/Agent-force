# Task for: strategic-sourcing-manager

I'm running the RFP for corrugated packaging across our four plants,
about $14M a year. The incumbent holds 70% of it on a fixed-price deal
that expires March 31. We have three final bids. Bidder A, the incumbent,
is flat on price. Bidder B is 11% below the incumbent, but it's a private
company supplied by a single containerboard mill, and its references were
slow to answer. Bidder C is 6% below, with index-linked pricing tied to a
published containerboard price index, adjusted quarterly. Now that bids
are open, plant operations wants to drop the service and quality weighting
from 40% to 20% so Bidder B wins outright. My VP asked whether I could
show the incumbent Bidder B's price sheet so they can match it. Legal wants
me to write the indemnity and limitation-of-liability clauses since I
"know the deal best." Award is due in three weeks. Please give me a
recommendation on the award structure and pricing mechanism, and tell me
how to handle each of these requests.
