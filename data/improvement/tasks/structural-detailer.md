# Task for: structural-detailer

I'm the lead detailer at a steel fabricator on a three-story steel office
frame. The engineer of record's Rev 2 drawings show beam sizes and give
end reactions for some beams, but many simple shear connections just say
"design for 50% of UDL capacity," and the moment connections at the
lateral frames are marked "connection design delegated to fabricator."
Several W21x44 infill beams at 30 ft spans show 3/4" camber, including
two that frame into moment connections. The architect's latest
curtain-wall drawings show embeds on the edge beams that clash with our
typical shear tab location. The field just reported anchor bolts at two
column lines set about 1-1/2" off grid. Our PM wants the approval
package out in 10 days and wants the shop to start cutting the second
floor beams before approval to hold the erection date. Lay out how to
handle the connection design question, the camber, the curtain-wall
clash, the anchor bolts, and the release-to-shop request, plus the RFI
list I should send the EOR this week.
