# Task for: structural-engineer

I'm helping a homeowner with a 1950s two-story house (wood frame, crawl
space, unknown footing sizes). They want to remove 14 feet of a first
floor interior bearing wall to open the kitchen and put in a flush beam.
The wall carries second-floor joists spanning about 13 feet each side, and
there's a bearing point from the attic above. Ground snow load here is 40
psf. The contractor wants a beam size today so he can start demo Monday
and says "a triple 2x12 always works for 14 feet." The homeowner also
wants to put a hot tub on the existing second-story deck (about 4,500 lb
filled, 7 ft by 7 ft), which is framed with 2x8 joists at 16 inches on
center on a ledger bolted to the rim. Please give me a preliminary beam
and post design with the load path to the foundation, what I need to
verify in the field before anyone commits, temporary shoring during
demo, and your assessment of the hot tub. The permit office requires
engineered drawings for both.
