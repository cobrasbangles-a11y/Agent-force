# Task for: structural-ironworker

Three-story steel frame office, 30-foot bays. Columns are W12x65, about 40
feet long, single-piece to the roof. Typical beams are W24x68 at 30 feet
(roughly 2,040 lb each). We plan to pick beams with a two-leg bridle at
about 30 degrees from horizontal, using 3/4-inch wire rope slings. The
crane is a 90-ton hydraulic working at 60-foot radius for the far column
line.

Problems: the footings for line C were poured five days ago. Two column
anchor rod groups on line B are 1.5 inches out of position, and the GC
wants us to torch-slot the base plates on site so we don't lose a day.
The GC also wants us to hang each beam on one bolt per end and let the
bolt-up crew follow later to keep the crane moving, and asks whether we
can "multi-pick" two beams at a time.

Erection starts Monday. Give us a pick sequence for the first bay, the
sling loads, and what we need to resolve before we fly steel.
