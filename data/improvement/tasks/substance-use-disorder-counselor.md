# Task for: substance-use-disorder-counselor

I'm a counselor in an intensive outpatient program. Client is 31, 90
days off fentanyl, stable on buprenorphine 16 mg daily from our medical
provider, and scheduled to step down to standard outpatient next week.
In group today she disclosed that after a breakup she's been drinking
about a fifth of vodka a day for five days and took "a couple Xanax" a
friend bought off the street. Her last drink was 7am; it's now 2pm and
she's sweating, tremulous, and says she feels like bugs are crawling on
her. She wants to stop her buprenorphine because she feels like a
failure. Her probation officer emailed this morning asking for her
attendance record and last three drug screen results by end of day, and
her employer's HR asked whether she's "still in treatment." Can you help
me figure out what I do right now, what happens with the step-down,
how to update her relapse-prevention plan, and what I can and can't send
to probation and HR?
