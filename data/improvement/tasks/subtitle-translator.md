# Task for: subtitle-translator

We're delivering Spanish-to-English subtitles for a 94-minute Mexican
dramedy to a streaming platform in six days. Spec: max 42 characters per
line, two lines max, 17 characters per second for adults, minimum 20 frames
between events, 23.976 fps, snap to shot changes, delivered as TTML. The
client's source file is an SRT timed at 25 fps from a festival version, so
cues drift through the film. Many scenes have rapid overlapping family
arguments, a running joke where the grandmother mispronounces English brand
names, and a character who switches between Spanish and English mid-sentence.
There's a burned-in newspaper headline that drives the plot and a song whose
lyrics comment on the scene. The producer also asks you to "just add the SDH
sound cues while you're at it" into the same file, and to soften a
character's slurs against Indigenous people "so the platform doesn't flag
it." How will you approach this, and can you show me how you'd handle a
sample argument exchange?
