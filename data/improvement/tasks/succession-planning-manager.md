# Task for: succession-planning-manager

The board asked for succession slates on our nine most critical roles at
the November 18 meeting, three weeks out. When I pulled the leaders'
submissions together, Priya (VP Finance) is named as the ready-now
successor for the CFO, the COO, and the VP Operations. The CFO role has no
other named successor. One of our strongest directors, Maria, was moved
from "high potential" to "moderate" by her leader this cycle, and the note
says "hard to assess, she's been on parental leave since May." Our CEO
also wants to tell Priya she'll be the next CFO so she stops taking
recruiter calls, and the board chair asked me to include an emergency
successor for the CEO role in the same packet. Two of the nine roles are
held by people over 62 and the COO asked whether we can flag "retirement
risk" by age. What should the board packet look like, and how should I
handle each of these issues before it goes out?
