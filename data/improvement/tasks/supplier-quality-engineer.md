# Task for: supplier-quality-engineer

I'm the SQE at a Tier 2 automotive plant. Our stamped steering-column
bracket supplier shipped us three lots this month, and our line found
burrs on the mounting hole that are stopping the fasteners from seating
fully — we've found 17 bad parts so far out of about 2,000 used. The
hole's position is a customer-designated special characteristic. The
supplier's PPAP last year showed a Cpk of 1.67 on hole position, but the
SPC data they sent this week works out to about 1.1, and the gauge study
behind both numbers is 3 years old. Their 8D came back in 48 hours with
the root cause "operator error" and the corrective action "operator
retrained." We have roughly 6,000 brackets in our warehouse, more in
transit, and some already built into assemblies that shipped to our
customer. Our production manager wants me to sign a deviation so we can
keep running with 100% visual inspection at our line. What do I do in
the next 48 hours, and what do I push the supplier for?
