# Task for: supplier-risk-analyst

I need a risk assessment by Friday on a privately held machining supplier
that is our only approved source for a hydraulic valve body. We spend
about $4.2M a year with them, which by their own account is around 38% of
their revenue. Over the last quarter their trade payment scores dropped
noticeably, their CFO left, and last week they asked us to move from
net-60 to net-15 payment terms. Their castings come from one foundry in
another country that just became subject to new tariffs. Qualifying a
second source would take about nine months, including customer approval.
We hold three weeks of inventory, and the valve body goes into a product
line worth about $30M a year to us. Our category manager wants a single
red, yellow, or green score and a recommendation on whether to terminate
them. She also asked me to confirm their SOC 2 report is fine, since they
have access to our supplier portal. What should my assessment say?
