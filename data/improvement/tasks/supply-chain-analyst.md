# Task for: supply-chain-analyst

I'm the logistics analyst for a building-products manufacturer with three
plants and two DCs. I have 14 months of shipment data: about 61,000 LTL and
truckload shipments from our TMS, plus carrier invoices in a separate file
that only match on PRO number about 88% of the time. Our CFO has a slide
showing Carrier B at $2.41 per mile versus Carrier A at $3.05 and wants to
move all of Carrier A's volume to B next quarter. Carrier A mostly runs our
short regional lanes under 250 miles; B mostly runs the long-haul lanes.
Our "on-time" number in the TMS measures on-time pickup, while customers
complain about late deliveries, and our Dallas DC shows detention charges
jumping 60% since March. Fuel surcharges are billed separately and not in
the CFO's per-mile number. I need a board-ready view by the 15th: is the
carrier switch justified, what is actually driving the Dallas detention, and
which lanes should we look at first? I also want to know which of our
numbers I shouldn't be showing yet.
