# Task for: support-enablement-manager

I run enablement for a 120-agent support org at a B2B accounting software
company. Our new-hire ramp to full productivity is 11 weeks against a goal
of 7, and 30% of new hires leave before week 16. The current program is
three weeks of classroom covering all 42 product modules in menu order,
then a 20-question multiple-choice quiz (pass mark 70%) before agents go
live. QA scores show new hires are fine on product facts but weak on
troubleshooting and on refunds and plan changes. Meanwhile, a major payroll
tax module redesign ships to customers in 5 weeks, and product hasn't
given us final screenshots or a feature freeze. Our VP wants the tenured
agents trained on it with a single one-hour webinar the week of launch, and
wants me to use training quiz scores to flag underperforming tenured agents
for performance plans. I also have 18 new hires starting in 3 weeks. Can
you redesign the ramp program, plan the payroll launch enablement, and
tell me how to respond to the VP's two requests?
