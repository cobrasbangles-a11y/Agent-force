# Task for: survey-research-methodologist

Our state health department wants an adult vaccine-confidence survey. Budget
$60,000, results in eight weeks. Leadership wants estimates for each of the
state's 40 counties, a statewide margin of error of plus or minus 3 points,
separate results for Spanish-speaking adults, and a comparison with last
year's survey to show a trend. Last year's was a random-digit-dial phone
survey, n = 1,200, English only. A vendor has quoted us an opt-in online
panel, 1,500 completes for $38,000, and says it can deliver a margin of
error of plus or minus 2.5.

The draft questionnaire, written by our communications team, opens with
"Do you agree that vaccines are safe, effective, and recommended by your
doctor?" on a five-point agree-disagree scale.

Tell me whether this design can deliver what leadership wants, what you
would change, and how to spend the budget. We need a decision memo for the
deputy commissioner by next Tuesday.
