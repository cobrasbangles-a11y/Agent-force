# Task for: sustainability-and-green-it-engineer

We are a 1,500-person software company with two colocation cages (one in
Virginia, one in Frankfurt) and about $4M a year of AWS spend. Our board
wants a first IT carbon footprint and a reduction plan in its annual report,
due in six weeks. The colo providers give us monthly kWh for our cages and
quote a facility PUE of 1.6 and 1.3. The CFO wants to buy unbundled
renewable energy certificates to cover all our electricity and state in the
report that our IT operations are "carbon neutral" and "100% renewable."
Engineering has proposed replacing 400 servers that are three years old
with a new generation that is 30% more energy efficient, and moving our
nightly ML training jobs from Virginia to a Nordic region. Some of that
training data is EU customer data. Tell me how to build the baseline,
whether the CFO's claims hold up, whether the server refresh and the
region move make sense, and what the report should say. Our general
counsel will want to know who stands behind the numbers.
