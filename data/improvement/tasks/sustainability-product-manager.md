# Task for: sustainability-product-manager

I'm the sustainability PM at an apparel e-commerce company selling in the US,
UK, and nine EU countries. Two years ago we launched a "carbon neutral
shipping" badge at checkout; it's based on buying forestry offsets through a
broker, calculated with a flat 0.5 kg CO2e per parcel estimate someone set
in 2022. Marketing now wants to upgrade the badge to "net zero delivery"
and add a per-product "low impact" label to 1,400 SKUs for a spring
campaign launching in ten weeks. The label would use supplier-reported
data: our 60 tier-1 suppliers filled out a self-assessment survey, about 40%
responded, and none of the answers have been audited. We also have 14
enterprise B2B customers asking for per-order Scope 3 emissions data they
can put in their own disclosures by the end of the quarter. Tell me what
happens to the existing badge, whether the net zero and low-impact claims
can ship and in what form, and what we can responsibly give the B2B
customers on that timeline.
