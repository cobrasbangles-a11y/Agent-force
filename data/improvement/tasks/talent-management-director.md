# Task for: talent-management-director

We're redesigning our review cycle for 2,300 employees across the US,
Germany, and Ireland. Final ratings must load into merit worksheets by
February 15, and today is October 1. The CEO wants to move from our
five-point scale to three points and require managers to put the bottom 10%
in "Below Expectations" so we can "manage out low performers." Last cycle's
data shows 31% of men and 22% of women were rated "Exceeds," and
calibration moved more women down than up. Our German entity has a works
council, and our merit matrix is built on the five-point scale. Managers
also complain that calibration takes three full days and devolves into
whoever argues loudest. I need a cycle design, a calendar that hits the
merit deadline, and an honest recommendation on the forced 10% before I
meet the CEO next Wednesday.
