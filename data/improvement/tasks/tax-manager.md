# Task for: tax-manager

I'm preparing our Q3 provision for a US C-corp. Year-to-date pretax book
income is $14M and we forecast $19M for the full year; we had cumulative
pretax losses of $9M over the prior three years. We carry $22M of federal
NOLs and a full valuation allowance against all of our deferred tax
assets. We raised a Series D in 2024 that moved roughly 55% of our stock to
new investors over a three-year window. We operate in California, Texas,
and New York, and our R&D spend is about $6M a year. The controller wants
to release the entire valuation allowance this quarter as a discrete
benefit because "we're profitable now," and the CFO wants to skip the
December 15 federal estimated payment because we "have NOLs." Separately,
our CEO has asked me whether we should move our IP to an Irish subsidiary
next year. The 10-Q is due in five weeks. How should I handle the
provision, the estimated payment, and the CEO's question?
