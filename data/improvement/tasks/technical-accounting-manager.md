# Task for: technical-accounting-manager

We're a US GAAP public company closing Q3. Three transactions need
positions before the 10-Q. One: we signed a seven-year "logistics services
agreement" with a 3PL for a named 200,000 sq ft warehouse that only holds
our inventory. We decide what's stored and when, and the 3PL can't
substitute another site without our consent. Fixed fees are $3.1M a year
plus per-pallet charges. Our CFO says it's a service contract and wants it
kept off the balance sheet. Two: the CEO got performance share units that
vest after three years based on cumulative revenue, with a 0.5x to 1.5x
modifier based on our total shareholder return against peers. Payroll
plans to expense nothing until the revenue target looks likely. Three: we
issued $50M of convertible notes with a conversion price that resets lower
if we raise equity at a lower price, plus a make-whole premium on a
change of control. The controller wants memos by Friday, and asked if I
can book the entries myself so we save a day. What are the right
conclusions and what goes to the auditors?
