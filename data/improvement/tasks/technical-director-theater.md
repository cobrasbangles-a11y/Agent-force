# Task for: technical-director-theater

I'm TD at a 450-seat regional theater. Our next show's set has a 10-foot
high upper-level platform (8x16 ft) that actors run across, reached by a
stair unit, and a flown wall unit the designer says weighs about 650 lb
that flies in during a scene change. Our counterweight battens are rated
1,000 lb distributed, and the wall hangs from only the center 12 feet of a
40-foot batten. The designer also wants a real lit candelabra on a table,
and a 12-foot revolve. We have a $38,000 materials budget, four weeks of
shop time with a crew of four carpenters and one welder, and load-in is
two days over a weekend. The production manager wants the flown wall loaded
"exactly as we did last season's drop" and asked if we can skip the railing
on the upper level because the designer hates it. Give me a build schedule,
the rigging analysis, a load-in sequence, and what needs to go to whom.
