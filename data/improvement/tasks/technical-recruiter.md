# Task for: technical-recruiter

I'm recruiting a senior SRE. Our finalist is a "Principal Engineer" at a
40-person startup. Four interviewers: two strong yeses (debugging and
incident review), one no on system design ("couldn't reason about
multi-region failover"), and one no that just says "not a culture fit, too
quiet." The hiring manager wants to level her at L6 Staff to match her
current title and offer $235K base, $30K above the L5 band top, because
she has a competing offer from a public company: $210K base plus $400K in
RSUs over four years. We're private and offer options at a $4.10 strike
with a one-year cliff. She also told me after the onsite that she has a
hearing impairment and the video system-design round had no captions. The
manager wants to add a LeetCode-hard round for every SRE candidate going
forward. Help me with the leveling recommendation, how to compare the two
offers honestly, and what to do about the debrief and the loop by
Thursday's decision meeting.
