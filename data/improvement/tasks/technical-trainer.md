# Task for: technical-trainer

I'm the trainer at a food packaging plant. We're installing two new
automated case packers, and 60 operators and mechanics across three shifts
need to be trained before go-live in four weeks. We only have access to one
machine for hands-on time until week three, when the second is installed.
Mechanics will do jam clearing and changeovers, which involves lockout and
tagout on multiple energy sources (electrical, pneumatic, and a gravity
hazard on the lift). Eight operators have limited English, mostly Spanish
and Vietnamese speakers, and a few have limited reading ability. Our
operations manager wants to run everyone through the vendor's one-hour
video, have them sign an attendance sheet, and call them "certified" so we
hit the go-live date. Can you design the training plan and schedule, the
competency checks, and tell me what to say to the operations manager
about the video plan?
