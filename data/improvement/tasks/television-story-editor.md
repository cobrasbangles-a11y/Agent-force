# Task for: television-story-editor

I'm the story editor on a 10-episode streaming crime drama, first season.
We're breaking episode 7 this week; episodes 1-4 are shot, 5 and 6 are in
prep, and 8-10 are outlined. The showrunner just decided the detective's
partner, Marcus, is the mole, revealed in episode 9. Problems: in episode 3
(already shot) Marcus is shown at a crime scene during the exact hour the
leak happens in episode 9's timeline. The staff writer's draft of episode 6
has Marcus explicitly telling the detective he's never met the crime boss.
The episode 7 draft from our freelance writer runs 64 pages for a 52-minute
slot, and its B-plot, a custody fight for the detective, has no connection to
the case. Production says episode 7's night warehouse shoot and a second
location blow the budget by two days. The executive producer asked me to
"just quietly rewrite the freelancer's script myself over the weekend." Give
me a plan: a beat sheet approach for 7, fixes for the continuity problems,
and how I handle the rewrite request.
