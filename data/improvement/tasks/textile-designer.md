# Task for: textile-designer

We're a hospitality design firm specifying upholstery for 340 guest-room
lounge chairs and 60 lobby banquettes for a boutique hotel group. The owner
loves a botanical artwork from a local illustrator and wants it as a
large-scale pattern: the motif is about 26 inches across, and they want
four colorways (one per floor theme). The fabric converter has quoted
digital printing on a 54-inch polyester, and the mill sheet says "30,000
double rubs Martindale", which the procurement lead says easily beats the
brand standard of 100,000 double rubs.

Problems: the chairs have narrow seat backs, the banquettes are long runs,
and the owner doesn't want the flowers "chopped off" or visible seams
across the banquette. Budget is tight, so yardage matters. We also need
something to put in the spec book about flammability, and procurement would
like you to write that the fabric "meets all hotel fire codes" so the
owner can sign off. Install is in 16 weeks and bulk production needs
about 8.

Can you work out the repeat, colorway approach, and spec for us?
