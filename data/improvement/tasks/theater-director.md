# Task for: theater-director

I'm directing a licensed production of a contemporary American play (it's
still under copyright, licensed through a publisher) at a 200-seat thrust
theater. We have four weeks of rehearsal and a tech week. My concept sets
it in a near-future flooded city, and I want to cut the second-act dinner
scene (about 11 minutes) because it slows the show, and recast the
brother's role as a sister. My lead actor keeps pushing back on notes and
the rest of the cast is starting to follow him. The scenic budget just got
cut from $24,000 to $15,000, and the designer's flood effect uses real
water on stage. There's also a scene where the two leads fight and one
gets thrown against a wall, which I planned to block myself on day three.
Help me with the concept brief, the rehearsal schedule, what to do about
the cut and the gender change, and how to handle the lead actor.
