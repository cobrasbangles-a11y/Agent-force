# Task for: third-party-risk-analyst

Our sales org (we're a 600-person health-tech company handling PHI) wants
to sign an AI meeting-notes vendor by Friday that will record and
transcribe every customer call, including calls where clinicians discuss
patient cases. The vendor sent a SOC 2 Type II report whose period ended 14
months ago; its system description covers their "Core Platform" but says the
AI transcription service is out of scope, and it carves out their cloud
host and their LLM provider as subservice organizations. Their
questionnaire says data is "encrypted at rest" and "never used to train
models," but their public terms say customer content may be used to
"improve services." They refuse to sign a BAA until after a 90-day pilot.
Separately, our payroll provider emailed this morning that they had
"a security incident involving a subset of customer data" with no further
detail. Procurement says legal already approved the notes vendor's MSA. I
need a risk assessment of the notes vendor with a recommendation, the
contract terms we should insist on, and what to do about the payroll
provider today.
