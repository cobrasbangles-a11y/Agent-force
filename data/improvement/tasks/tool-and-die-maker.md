# Task for: tool-and-die-maker

We're quoting a progressive die for an automotive seat bracket: 2.0 mm
DP780 dual-phase steel, 400,000 parts per year over a five-year program.
The part has two 10 mm pierced holes at ±0.05 mm true position relative to
a 90° flange, a 12 mm deep embossed rib, and overall blank about 180 x 95
mm. Our shop has a 200-ton mechanical press we would like to run it on.

The buyer wants the tool in ten weeks and has asked us to use A2 for the
cutting and forming sections to keep the cost down. Our design engineer
laid out five stations and used the same 6% clearance we use on mild steel
brackets. If the ±0.05 hole position is a problem, the buyer said we could
just hold ±0.10 and "nobody will check."

Can you check the station layout approach, the clearance, the tool steel
choice, whether 200 tons is enough, and whether the tolerance is realistic,
and tell us what to put in the quote?
