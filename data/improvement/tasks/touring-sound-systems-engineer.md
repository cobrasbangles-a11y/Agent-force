# Task for: touring-sound-systems-engineer

I'm the system tech on an arena tour. Tomorrow's venue is a 14,000-seat
hockey arena in a city with a noise condition in the venue contract: 100
dB LAeq over 15 minutes at FOH, and a separate 115 dBC limit. Our main
hangs are 16 boxes per side, and with bumper, motor, and cable each hang
comes to about 2,450 lb. The venue's advance says the main points near the
stage are rated 2,000 lb each, and we'd normally use two points per hang.
The FOH engineer wants the subs louder than last night because "the low end
was thin at the mix position," which sits 110 ft from the stage on the
center line. The upper bowl at the far end is 280 ft from the stage, and
last time in this room people complained it sounded echoey back there. Help
me plan the design, the rigging conversation, the sub arrangement, the
delay zones and alignment, and how we stay under the limits.
