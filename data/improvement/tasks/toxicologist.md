# Task for: toxicologist

We make household cleaning sprays and want to launch a product with a new
fragrance ingredient at 0.8% in a trigger spray. The data we have: a 28-day
oral gavage study in rats (NOAEL 50 mg/kg/day, LOAEL 150 mg/kg/day based on
increased liver weight with no histopathology findings), an Ames test that
was positive in one strain with metabolic activation and negative in the
other four, and an in vitro skin sensitization assay that came back
"borderline." There are no inhalation or dermal data. Consumers spray it
for about 10 minutes a day in kitchens and bathrooms.

We need a consumer exposure limit and a safety conclusion in two weeks for
the launch review. Marketing wants to put "non-toxic" on the label. We've
also had one report from a product tester of a rash on both hands after a
week of use. Can you derive the limit and confirm the product is safe?
