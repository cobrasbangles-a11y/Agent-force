# Task for: toy-designer

We're a small startup developing a magnetic building-tile set (48 tiles,
translucent ABS, rare-earth magnets sealed inside each tile edge) aimed at
ages 3 and up, launching in the US and EU in 14 months. Our factory has
proposed ultrasonic welding of the two tile halves to save cost over
screws, and our marketing agency wants the box photo to show an 18-month-old
playing with the set because "that's who actually uses these."

Our target retail price is $49, which leaves about $11 landed cost per
set. Our industrial designer also wants to add a small snap-in window piece
and a little figure, each about the size of a thumbnail, to boost play
value.

Last week a beta family emailed that a tile cracked along the weld after
their kid threw it down the stairs, and one magnet was visible but still in
place. The factory says that's just abuse and nothing to worry about.

Please give us a design and testing plan to get us to launch, tell us what
to change, and draft the wording for the box's age label.
