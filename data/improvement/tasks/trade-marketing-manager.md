# Task for: trade-marketing-manager

We're a mid-size salty snack brand. Our Q3 buy-one-get-one promotion at a
400-store grocery chain shipped 48,000 cases, but scan data shows only
29,000 sold through during the promotion, and our field audit found end-cap
displays in just 55% of the stores we paid for. The retailer has now taken
$86,000 in deductions from our invoices, coded "promo non-compliance" and
"unsaleables." Our sales VP wants to accept them to protect the
relationship. The buyer is also asking for a $120,000 slotting fee for our
new flavor and a 20% temporary price reduction in January, and she wants
us to "make sure" every store sells our core bag at $2.99. Our joint
business planning meeting is in two weeks. Help me figure out how the Q3
promotion actually performed, what to do about the deductions, and how to
go into the meeting.
