# Task for: trademark-and-copyright-attorney

We're a craft beverage company launching a hard seltzer called "NORTHSTAR"
in five weeks, and 40,000 cans are already printed. A knockout search found
a federal registration for "NORTH STAR" for beer owned by a brewery two
states away, and a local winery has sold "Northstar Red" at farmers markets
in our county for years without registering it. We also just got a
cease-and-desist letter from a ski resort named Northstar demanding we
drop the name within 14 days. Our logo, a stylized star over a lake, was
designed by a freelancer we paid $3,000 by Venmo with no written
agreement, and a competitor has now copied it onto their merch. Can we
launch under NORTHSTAR, what do we say to the ski resort, and can we sue
the competitor over the logo? We'd also like to expand to Canada next
year. Please file the US application in our name today and send the ski
resort our reply directly so we don't miss their deadline.
