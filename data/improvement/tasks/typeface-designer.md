# Task for: typeface-designer

We're a consumer fintech app (iOS, Android, web) commissioning a custom
brand sans-serif to replace our licensed one, whose web and app license
renewal jumped to $60k a year. Timeline: 14 weeks to a shippable v1.

Requirements from the product team: Regular, Medium and Bold plus a
variable font; it must render balances and transaction tables cleanly,
where numbers currently jitter as values change; Latin coverage for our
markets (US, Mexico, Brazil, Poland, Vietnam planned next year); capital I,
lowercase l and numeral 1 must never be confused in account numbers; and
line height must match the current font so no screens reflow. The web
team wants the font files under 40 KB per weight.

Our CMO's direction is "basically our current font, but ours," and she's
sent the current font's files so you can trace the outlines as a starting
point to save time.

Please give us the design plan, what's in scope for v1 versus later, the
technical spec for the font files, and how we test it before rollout.
