# Task for: ui-designer

We're adding dark mode to our invoicing web app before a November launch
(five weeks). The UX team has handed over validated flows for the invoice
list, invoice detail, and a payment status table. Our brand primary is
#7B61FF, used for primary buttons, links, and the selected-row highlight,
and the status column uses green / amber / red pills with no text label
inside them, just the color. Our engineers want a token list, not a Figma
file, and they plan to generate dark mode by inverting the light-mode hex
values automatically.

Other issues: the table's row action icons are 16px with 4px spacing, and
customers use the app on tablets in warehouses. German and French versions
launch at the same time, and the status labels are fixed-width pills. Our
PM also asked whether, while I'm in there, I could move the "Send invoice"
action from the detail page into the list row to save a click.

Please produce the dark-mode visual spec and tokens our engineers can build
from, and flag anything in the current light theme that needs fixing too.
