# Task for: urban-planner

I'm a senior planner for a city of 60,000. I have an application to rezone
6.8 acres from R-1 single-family to MU-2 mixed use, proposing 240
apartments and 20,000 sq ft of ground-floor retail. The future land use
map shows "Neighborhood Commercial" on the 300 ft of arterial frontage and
"Low-Density Residential" on the rear four acres. The sewer lift station
serving the area is at about 85% of design capacity, and the arterial is
a state route. The applicant has offered a development agreement limiting
height to four stories and paying for a traffic signal. A council member
emailed me yesterday asking staff to "get this to a yes" and forwarded
neighbors' complaints to the applicant. Our state also passed a housing
law last session that may limit how we can deny multifamily near
commercial corridors. The planning commission hearing is November 12 and
the staff report is due in three weeks. Help me structure the analysis,
the recommendation options, conditions, and how to handle the council
member's email.
