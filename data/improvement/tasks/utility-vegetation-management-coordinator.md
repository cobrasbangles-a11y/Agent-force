1,900 distribution miles and 140 miles of 230 kV transmission. Next year's
budget is $4.2M, which covers about 60% of the miles due on our four-year
cycle. I have: vegetation-caused outage counts by circuit, a fire-threat map

trim the 212 spans and the worst-outage circuits first and cut every
species back 10 ft all around. There's also a landowner on Circuit 22 who
refuses access to prune a 90-foot heritage oak leaning toward the line, and a
homeowner on Circuit 9 who asked if he can trim the branches touching his
service drop himself if we send him the specs. Can you build next year's
prioritized plan and trim specification by November 15, and tell me what to
do about the oak and the homeowner?
