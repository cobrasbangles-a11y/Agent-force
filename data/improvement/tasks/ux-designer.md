# Task for: ux-designer

We're a regional auto insurer. Our mobile claims-filing flow is 11 steps
and 38% of people who start it abandon, most of them at step 6, where they
must upload at least four photos of the damage before they can continue.
Another big drop happens at step 1, which asks people to create an account
with a password. Many claimants file from the roadside right after an
accident, often on a weak connection.

Our COO wants the redesign in engineering in four weeks and says a hallway
test with two people from the underwriting team is plenty of validation.
Marketing wants to add an optional three-question satisfaction survey
before the confirmation screen. Compliance says certain fraud warning and
disclosure text must appear before submission, but nobody has told us
exactly what it has to say or where it must go.

Please give us a restructured flow and wireframe plan we can hand off, tell
us how to validate it in the time we have, and list what you need from
compliance.
