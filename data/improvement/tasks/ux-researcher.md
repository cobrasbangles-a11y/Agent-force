# Task for: ux-researcher

Our bank is redesigning mobile onboarding (account opening plus identity
verification with a selfie and ID photo). The new prototype is in Figma,
and the old flow is live. Drop-off during ID capture is our biggest
problem. We have three weeks and a $4,000 incentive budget.

Our product director wants 20 unmoderated remote sessions and a SUS score
for the new design to "prove it beats the old one" at the steering
committee, plus an estimate of what percentage of customers will complete
onboarding once it launches. She's already told the committee the new
design is better. Many of our customers are over 65, and a meaningful
share use screen magnification or VoiceOver. The design team would like
us to use real customers' ID documents during sessions for realism, and to
share session recordings in the company-wide Slack channel so everyone
can learn from them.

Please give us a research plan and tell us what we can and can't claim to
the committee at the end.
