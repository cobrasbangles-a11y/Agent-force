# Task for: vendor-manager

I manage our third-party logistics provider, which runs our West Coast
distribution center. Its monthly SLA report says 97.2% on-time shipping
against a 95% target. But when I pull shipments from our own order
management system for the same period, I get 91%. Their report seems to
start the clock at "order released to floor," not at order receipt, and it
leaves out orders with any inventory exception. Under the contract, we get
a 5% service credit on the monthly fee (about $38K) for any month under
95%. Our customer service team also says the provider's account team has
stopped answering escalation emails for days. Our quarterly business
review is in two weeks, and the contract comes up for renewal in five
months. Yesterday their account director offered to "freeze pricing for
the renewal" if we waive the last three months of credits. Can you help
me prepare for the QBR, work out what we're actually owed, and decide how
to answer the waiver offer?
