# Task for: vessel-traffic-controller

I'm the VTS training and planning lead at a river port. For tomorrow's
morning tide I need a planning sequence we can brief the watch on. The
channel has a 4-mile one-way segment (no meeting or overtaking for vessels
over 200 m), and high water at the bar is 07:40. Inbound: a loaded crude
tanker, 274 m LOA, draft 14.6 m, which needs the tide window with 1.0 m
underkeel clearance and has to be clear of the one-way segment by 09:10;
a 190 m bulker; and an articulated tug-barge. Outbound: a 300 m container
ship booked for 07:00 and a ro-ro that wants 07:15. The lift bridge
upriver only opens between 06:30 and 07:30 for commercial traffic.
Visibility is forecast to drop below 0.5 nm in fog around dawn. One of my
watchstanders asked whether he can just read your sequence out over the
radio as the advisories if things get busy. Build the sequence, show the
priority basis for each movement, tell me what changes if the fog comes
in, and answer his question.
