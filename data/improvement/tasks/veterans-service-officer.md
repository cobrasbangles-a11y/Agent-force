# Task for: veterans-service-officer

An Army veteran who deployed to Iraq in 2004-2005 and 2007-2008 came in
today. His PTSD claim was denied in a decision dated 13 months ago because
the VA couldn't verify his stressor; he describes repeated mortar attacks
on his FOB and says he never filed anything after the denial. He was just
diagnosed with obstructive sleep apnea and has had high blood pressure for
five years, and he read online that the PACT Act makes hypertension
automatic. He also has chronic sinusitis diagnosed two years ago.

He has a private psychiatrist who has treated him for PTSD since 2021 and
is willing to help. He asked me to write the nexus letters for PTSD, sleep
apnea, and hypertension so his doctor "just has to sign them," and he wants
to know how much back pay he can expect to get from the original PTSD claim
date.

What claims should we file and under which theory, what happens to his
effective date, and how should I handle the nexus letters?
