# Task for: visual-designer

We're launching a new product line in three weeks and need a campaign set
from one key visual: a 16:9 keynote deck template (title, section, data,
and quote slides), a landing-page hero, LinkedIn and Instagram ads (feed
and Stories), a 300x250 and 728x90 display banner set, and a trade-show
backdrop printed at 3 m x 2.4 m.

Our brand guide covers a light palette only, but the event stage and
Stories want a dark treatment. The hero photo our CMO picked is a busy
city street at dusk, and she wants our white headline set directly across
the middle of it. A designer on our team found a great display font on a
free-font site and a couple of lifestyle photos on Google Images we'd like
to use in the ads. The ad network caps banner files at 150 KB, and the
printer wants final files next Friday.

Please give us the composition approach and a production spec for every
deliverable, and tell us what needs sign-off from someone else before we
can go ahead.
