# Task for: vp-of-customer-success

I'm VP of Customer Success at a B2B SaaS company, $84M ARR. NRR fell from
112% to 101% over the last four quarters; the CEO wants a plan at the board
meeting in three weeks. What I know: gross logo churn is flat, but the SMB
segment (about 1,900 accounts, 22% of ARR, 6 CSMs) has seen downgrades
triple since we changed pricing in March. Enterprise expansion fell because
two AEs and the CSMs keep both claiming upsells and the handoff has broken
down. Our top 20 accounts are 31% of ARR, and four of them lost their
executive sponsor this year. The CFO proposes cutting 4 CSMs and moving SMB
to a pooled digital model to save $600K; sales wants CSMs to carry
expansion quotas. One CSM director wants to offer the top-20 at-risk accounts
15% renewal discounts now. Put together what I bring to the board.
