# Task for: vp-of-marketing

I'm VP of Marketing at a Series C SaaS company: 45 people, $14M annual
budget, reporting to the CMO. Finance just cut H2 by 15%, which is $2.1M.
The CMO has already announced our October user conference ($1.8M, venue
deposit paid) and a rebrand that's halfway done with an agency. Demand gen
is 22% behind its pipeline target, we have two open reqs, and a $60K/month
content agency retainer has missed deadlines for two quarters. My directors
each want me to cut 15% evenly across every team so it's "fair." One
director wants to let two of his people go this week and asked me to
handle it quietly without looping in HR. The CMO wants my H2 plan in 10
days. How should I build it, what should I cut, and what do I need the CMO
to decide?
