# Task for: vp-of-operations

I'm VP of operations for an e-commerce retailer with three fulfillment
centers. Peak weeks run from November 17 through December 21, and the
forecast puts them at 2.4 times our baseline weekly volume of 180,000
units. Year to date, cost per unit is $2.31 against a $2.03 budget.
Finance calls it a "labor problem," but part of the volume has moved to
bulky items, and our Reno site's units per labor hour dropped after a
conveyor retrofit. Our promise is 95% same-day shipping on orders placed
before 2pm. Reno's GM wants to cover peak with mandatory 60-hour weeks for
eight weeks instead of hiring temps, saying temps are "too slow to be
worth it" this late. It's now September 29, and our staffing agency needs
four weeks to recruit. Separately, the CEO wants to announce a Saturday
delivery promise for the holidays next week. I need a peak capacity plan,
an explanation of the cost variance, and a position on the Saturday
promise for the executive team by Monday.
