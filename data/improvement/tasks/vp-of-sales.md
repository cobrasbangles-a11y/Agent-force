# Task for: vp-of-sales

I'm VP of Sales at a Series C SaaS company. The board-approved FY27 target
is $48M of new ARR, up from $31M this year. We have 62 ramped AEs with a
$700K quota; average attainment was 68%, and 38% of reps hit plan. AE
attrition was 31%, and new AEs take about six months to ramp. Our CFO
proposes raising quota to $900K so we don't have to hire. I also want
enterprise reps to pick up mid-market accounts to fill coverage gaps. Last
month I told the board Q4 would land at $9.5M; with six weeks left the
roll-up now says $7.8M. The next board meeting is in five weeks and I owe
them the FY27 plan. Can you build the capacity model, tell me whether $48M
is credible, and tell me what to do about the Q4 number and the quota
proposal?
