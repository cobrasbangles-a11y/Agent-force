# Task for: warehouse-manager

I run a 400,000 sq ft DC for a home-goods retailer. Next week is our peak:
outbound demand forecast at 58,000 units a day versus our normal 34,000,
with a 17:30 carrier cutoff for parcel and 42 inbound trailers already
sitting in the yard, 11 of them past their free time. I have three shift
supervisors, 160 permanent associates, and the agency can send 70 temps
starting Monday. Last month's cycle counts showed $86,000 of shrink, about
two-thirds of it concentrated in the high-value electronics cage and spread
evenly across its SKUs. The ops VP's suggestions are: stage outbound
pallets in the main cross-aisles and in front of the east-wall exits since
the shipping lanes will be full, put temps who "have driven forklifts
before" on reach trucks Monday, and have the cage team investigate the
shrink themselves during slow hours. I need a labor plan for the week, a
yard and dock plan, and a response on the VP's three suggestions by
Sunday night.
