# Task for: water-distribution-system-operator

It's 5:40 a.m. and we have a 16-inch cast iron main break on Harlan Avenue in
our Pressure Zone 2. Zone 2 serves about 9,000 connections including the
regional hospital and a dialysis center, and it's fed by one pump station
and the Crestview elevated tank, which has dropped from 32 ft to 19 ft of
its 40 ft operating range in 90 minutes. SCADA shows pressures at the high
end of the zone falling to 14 psi. The crew found four candidate valves; two
are 16-inch gate valves nobody has exercised in years. There's a normally
closed boundary valve to Zone 1 (60 ft lower in hydraulic grade) we could
open. I need a plan: which valves to close and how fast, whether opening the
boundary valve is a good idea, how to protect the hospital, and when to put
the main back in service. My supervisor also wants to know if we can skip the
boil water notice since the pressure was only low for about an hour.
