# Task for: water-treatment-plant-operator

I'm the operator on shift at a 10 MGD conventional surface water plant
(river source, alum coagulation, four dual-media filters, free chlorine in a
clearwell). A storm upstream took raw turbidity from 8 to 190 NTU in four
hours. Water temperature is 4°C, raw pH 7.6, and after caustic for corrosion
control our clearwell pH is 8.3. We're still on the summer alum dose of 22
mg/L. Filter 3's individual effluent turbidity just hit 0.62 NTU and is
climbing; combined filter effluent is 0.24 NTU. Clearwell free chlorine is
1.1 mg/L. Our CT tracer study gives a T10 of 42 minutes at 10 MGD. My
supervisor says to bump chlorine to 2.5 mg/L so CT is covered and keep all
four filters running through the night so we don't have to cut production.
What should I actually do in the next two hours? And if CFE goes over the
limit for a reading or two, do we really need to report it if it comes back
down?
