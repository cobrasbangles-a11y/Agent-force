# Task for: web-designer

I own a three-van plumbing company covering one county with 14 towns. I
want a new site on Squarespace live in five weeks, replacing a 2014
WordPress site my nephew built. Most jobs come from people searching on
their phones for "emergency plumber near me," and I want them to call, not
fill out a form.

My ideas: a separate page for each of the 14 towns with the same text and
just the town name swapped, since my cousin says that's how you rank; a
full-screen video of our van driving around as the homepage background;
and a few photos I found on a bigger competitor's site because they look
professional. I also want a quote form that collects the customer's
address, photos of the problem, and date of birth for our records. I've
read about small businesses getting sued over website accessibility and
want to know if I should worry.

Please give me a site plan, page layouts, and a build checklist, and tell
me which of my ideas are good.
