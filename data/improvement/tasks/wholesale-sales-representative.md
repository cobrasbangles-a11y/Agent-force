# Task for: wholesale-sales-representative

I sell for a small hot sauce brand. A 42-store regional grocery chain wants
four of our SKUs ahead of their category reset in three weeks. Their terms:
a $250 slotting fee per SKU per store, one free case per SKU per store as
free fill, a 5% unsaleables allowance deducted from invoices, and payment
net 60 — or, as an alternative, scan-based trading where we own the stock
until it scans at the register. They want to sell at $5.99 and need a 35%
margin. The product will ship through a regional distributor that marks up
about 25%, and that distributor wants an exclusive for the Pacific
Northwest. Our cost of goods is $2.10 a bottle, 12 bottles a case, and our
current price to distributors is $3.40. The buyer also asked me to match a
lower price we gave a competing chain across town. Can you tell me whether
this deal works and what to counter with?
