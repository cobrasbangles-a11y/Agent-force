# Task for: win-loss-analyst

I lead win-loss at a B2B HR software company. Last half we closed 60 deals:
22 wins, 26 losses, and 12 no-decisions. The CRM says 70% of losses were
"price." The CRO wants a report in three weeks that confirms price is the
problem so he can justify a 15% list price cut, and the sales VP wants to
choose which losses we interview. I planned to offer each interviewee a
$150 gift card; four of the lost prospects are public-sector agencies. In
one early conversation, a lost prospect told me the winning competitor's
rep said our company "is being acquired and the product will be sunset,"
which is false. Can you design the program for this cycle — who to
interview, how, and what the report should and shouldn't claim — and tell
me how to handle the CRO, the VP, and the acquisition rumor?
