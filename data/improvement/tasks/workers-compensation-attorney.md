# Task for: workers-compensation-attorney

We run a regional distribution warehouse. A forklift operator reported a
low-back injury at 7:15 on a Monday morning, saying he hurt himself lifting
a pallet jack. His supervisor saw photos of him playing softball that
weekend and thinks it happened there. Two weeks later we terminated him
for attendance points that had been building since before the injury. He
filed a comp claim three weeks ago; his chiropractor has now assigned a
12% whole-person impairment and permanent restrictions of no lifting over
15 pounds, and his lawyer has sent a letter alleging retaliatory discharge.
Our insurer's adjuster wants to know by Friday whether we'll contest
compensability. Separately, the pallet jack's handle reportedly snapped,
and it was serviced by an outside vendor last month. HR wants to deny the
claim outright, hire an investigator to follow him, and have our HR
manager call him to ask him to drop the claim in exchange for his job
back. What should we do, and in what order?
