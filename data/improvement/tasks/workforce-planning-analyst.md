# Task for: workforce-planning-analyst

The FY27 headcount model is due to finance on November 7. Our HRIS shows
1,212 headcount, finance's budget system shows 1,248, and the ATS has 61
open reqs while finance says 48 are approved. Engineering submitted a
request for 40 new hires "starting January 1" at an average base of $190K
and costed it at $7.6M for FY27. Our average engineering time to fill is
74 days and trailing engineering attrition is 14%. The VP of Engineering
also wants me to send recruiting a copy of the draft model showing his 40
reqs so they can start sourcing now, and asked whether I can build the
attrition assumption from a list of individuals his managers think are
likely to leave. Finance wants a hiring-freeze scenario and a 20%-growth
scenario alongside the base case. Walk me through how to reconcile the
numbers, what engineering's request really costs in FY27, and what I
should do about the VP's two requests.
