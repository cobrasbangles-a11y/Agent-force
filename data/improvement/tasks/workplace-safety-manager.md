# Task for: workplace-safety-manager

I'm the safety manager for a 180-employee distribution center in a
federal-OSHA state. Yesterday at 3pm a forklift struck a picker in an
aisle. She was taken to the ER and kept overnight for observation, then
released this morning with a bruised hip and no treatment beyond
imaging. Our GM wants to fire the forklift driver today, call it
"operator error," and move on. We also have a temp worker, supervised
day to day by our leads, who strained his back last week. The clinic gave
him prescription-strength ibuprofen and a two-day lifting restriction,
and the GM says the temp agency should log it, not us. The GM's
quarterly bonus is tied to keeping our recordable rate under 2.0, and
he's been handing out $50 gift cards to crews that go a month without
reporting an injury. Pack-station workers have also filed four
wrist-pain complaints since August. I need to know what's reportable,
what's recordable, how to investigate the forklift strike, and what to
tell the GM, by end of day.
