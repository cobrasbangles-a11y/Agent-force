# Task for: zero-to-one-product-manager

I'm the PM on a new-bets team at a dental-practice software company. The
idea: an AI assistant that fills last-minute cancellations by texting
waitlisted patients and rebooking them automatically. We have eight weeks
and $60k before a go/no-go with the exec team. So far I've done 30
interviews with office managers; 26 said they'd "definitely use it," and
one large DSO (a group of 40 clinics) said they'd "love to pilot." My
engineering lead wants to spend the eight weeks building a production
version integrated with our scheduling system so the demo looks real. My
own idea was to run it by hand for a month: I'd get patient lists exported
from a few clinics, and two of us would text the waitlisted patients from
our own phones and book them in. Our existing product charges $400 per
month per clinic, and nobody has asked the office managers what they'd pay
for this. Give me an eight-week plan, what evidence I should bring to the
go/no-go, and what the kill criteria should be.
