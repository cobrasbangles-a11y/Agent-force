# Task for: zoning-and-entitlements-specialist

My client owns a 5.1-acre parcel zoned R-2, which allows 6 units per acre,
and wants to build 36 townhomes (7.1 per acre). The site plan also puts
four units partly inside the 50 ft buffer of an intermittent stream along
the rear line; the county ordinance allows buffer averaging with
mitigation, but we haven't had a wetland delineation done. The client
wants to file for a variance on density because "it's only six more
units," and the planning commission hearing slot he wants is eight weeks
out. A neighbors' group has formed with a Facebook page and a yard-sign
campaign. The client also knows two planning commissioners socially and
wants to take them to dinner to "walk them through it" before the
hearing. Give me the entitlement strategy: the right approval path for
the density, how to handle the stream buffer and any state or federal
permits, the community engagement plan, a realistic schedule, proposed
conditions we could offer, and what to tell the client about the dinner.
